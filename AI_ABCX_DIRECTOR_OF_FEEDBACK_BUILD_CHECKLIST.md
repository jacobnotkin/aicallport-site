## 1. Director of Feedback Mission

Director of Feedback is the intake-and-signal lane of AI-ABCX.

Its job is to:

- capture recommendations, complaints, clarifications, and operating-friction signals
- keep missed-opportunity signals visible while they can still change business outcomes
- separate actionable feedback from noise
- preserve freshness, ownership, and context around incoming signals
- connect client-facing communication to executive decision-making
- route feedback into analysis, optimization, support, retention, and President visibility
- prevent useful signals from disappearing into disconnected messages, memory, or office chaos
- help the system improve itself based on real operating truth

Director of Feedback should not feel like a generic inbox.

It should feel like the AI-ABCX feedback-control lane that tells the President what the business is learning, where friction is surfacing, and which signals still matter enough to act on now.

---

## 2. Director of Feedback Launch Standard

Director of Feedback is launch-ready only when it can do all of the following:

1. capture feedback from real business touchpoints
2. separate actionable signals from low-value noise
3. keep signal freshness and visibility clear
4. show which signals affect revenue, continuity, or operating friction
5. preserve clear ownership and next-step accountability
6. connect cleanly with Support, Retention, Reviews / Referrals, Analysis, Optimization, and President visibility
7. support customer-facing transparency without losing internal control
8. support A / B / C levels clearly

If one of these is missing, the director is not complete.

---

## 3. Canonical Role Definition

### Public-facing name

`Director of Feedback`

### Internal role meaning

The AI-ABCX director responsible for capturing business-friction signals, client recommendations, complaints, missed-opportunity notes, and operating observations, then keeping those signals visible enough to become usable action.

### Executive owner

`CPIO`

Director of Feedback belongs to the CPIO lane because feedback is the raw operating input that later becomes analysis, optimization, and system improvement.

## 7. A / B / C Definition Draft

### Level A

Level A Director of Feedback is visible feedback intake and basic signal control.

Includes:

- recommendation capture
- complaint capture
- clarification capture
- basic signal queue visibility
- owner-visible routing
- basic missed-opportunity visibility

Best fit:

- smaller service businesses
- founder-led businesses
- companies that first need important feedback to stop disappearing

### Level B

Level B Director of Feedback adds stronger actionability and pattern visibility.

Includes everything in A, plus:

- stronger signal triage
- repeat-theme detection
- stronger owner assignment
- clearer escalation readiness
- better connection to Support, Retention, and Reviews

Best fit:

- growing businesses
- teams with more than one person handling customer communication
- companies where unresolved friction already affects continuity or conversion

### Level C

Level C Director of Feedback adds advanced feedback intelligence and improvement routing.

Includes everything in B, plus:

- stronger missed-opportunity interpretation
- better cause-linking into Analysis
- stronger prioritization of revenue-relevant signals
- more predictive routing into optimization and system-improvement lanes
- deeper executive learning visibility

Best fit:

- companies using multiple AI-ABCX directors together
- businesses serious about system improvement and accountability
- larger or more complex service businesses where feedback has to influence operations quickly

## 10. Required Shared Record

Director of Feedback needs a structured shared record that can include:

- source of feedback
- signal type
- signal freshness
- related customer
- related job / opportunity / workflow
- full message history
- assigned owner
- escalation status
- current business impact
- linked recommendation / complaint / clarification summary
- next action
- final resolution

This record must not live outside the system in disconnected notes or inbox fragments.

## 13. Required Handoffs

Director of Feedback must hand off cleanly to:

- `Support Director`
  - when the signal requires response or issue handling
- `Retention Director`
  - when the signal affects continuity or churn risk
- `Reviews / Referrals Director`
  - when reputation or advocacy follow-up is relevant
- `Director of Analysis`
  - when recurring patterns need root-cause interpretation
- `Director of Optimization`
  - when a pattern already points to an improvement move
- `Director of System Improvement`
  - when repeated issues show structural logic needs revision
- `President`
  - when the signal has executive consequence

Without these handoffs, Feedback becomes a dead-end holding area.

---

## 14. Guided Activation Requirements

The activation flow should eventually help determine:

- whether the business receives high message volume
- whether customers often ask for updates or clarifications
- whether complaints are currently tracked anywhere reliable
- whether recommendations are captured or lost
- whether the owner wants more transparency with customers
- whether multiple people handle communication
- whether the company struggles with follow-through after intake

This matters because Director of Feedback should be recommended more strongly when the company already loses signal continuity or wants transparency as a competitive advantage.

## 15. Pricing Decisions Still Required

Pricing still needs final decisions for:

- whether Director of Feedback is sold only inside broader system combinations or can be positioned more independently
- whether A / B / C pricing follows the same director pattern as other launch lanes
- whether transparency / communication-board features are bundled with Support or priced as part of Feedback
- whether advanced analysis-routing belongs to Feedback C or to Analysis / Optimization combinations

Current practical recommendation:

- treat Director of Feedback as a real launch director
- keep it available at all levels
- usually recommend it as part of Support / Retention / trust-oriented configurations rather than as a lone first module

## 16. Build Sequence

Recommended build order:

1. normalize canonical role naming
2. define shared signal record
3. define signal categories and states
4. connect communication-board intake to feedback records
5. build President and CPIO visibility
6. build owner assignment and escalation logic
7. connect handoffs to Support, Retention, and Analysis lanes
8. define A / B / C behavior
9. test freshness, routing, and visibility

This order keeps the lane grounded in actual signal flow rather than abstract reporting.

## 17. Internal Tests Required

Director of Feedback is not complete until AI-ABCX can internally prove:

1. a recommendation becomes a visible signal record
2. a complaint can be logged, assigned, and escalated
3. a clarification request stays attached to customer and workflow history
4. stale signals become visible as stale
5. repeat themes can be surfaced
6. missed-opportunity signals can be marked as priority
7. support handoff preserves context
8. President visibility remains short and actionable
9. signal noise does not overwhelm useful signals

Testing should cover:

- single-owner business
- small team with multiple responders
- support-heavy service business
- recommendation / complaint / clarification mix
- same-customer repeated signal history
