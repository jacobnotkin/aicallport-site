# CFO Module Specification

## Purpose

The `CFO Module` is the revenue-accountability layer of AI-ABC.

It exists to show the President not only what work happened, but what money actually happened.

This is where the system moves from opportunity visibility into financial clarity.

---

## Mission

Track the movement from estimate and completed work into actual financial outcome.

---

## Primary Responsibilities

- track estimates
- track payments received
- track payments pending
- track revenue at risk
- track revenue lost
- track open financial outcomes
- support reconciliation
- support goal-based financial visibility

---

## Inputs

- completion reports
- estimate amounts
- job outcomes
- payment updates
- partial payment states
- open estimate states
- lost-job states
- approved revenue goals

---

## Outputs

- revenue received visibility
- revenue pending visibility
- unpaid completed-job visibility
- reconciliation queue
- financial goal progress
- financial risk summary
- revenue forecasting inputs

---

## KPIs

- revenue received
- revenue pending
- revenue lost
- open estimates
- unpaid completed jobs
- average estimate size
- average collection lag
- goal progress
- revenue-at-risk total

---

## Reports

### Daily Financial Reconciliation Report

- completed jobs with missing payment outcome
- new payments received
- partial payments
- open estimate movement
- lost-job declarations

### Weekly Revenue Report

- total received
- total pending
- total lost
- top unpaid completed jobs
- goal progress

### Monthly Financial Review

- revenue trend
- payment collection trend
- open-estimate movement
- reconciliation quality
- risk areas

---

## Recommendations

- reconcile missing outcomes on completed jobs
- follow up on large open estimates
- review a growing revenue-at-risk category
- adjust goal expectations based on recent trend
- investigate partial-payment patterns

---

## Dashboard Position

In the President dashboard, the CFO Module should provide the financial truth layer.

It should answer:

- what money came in
- what money is still pending
- what money may be lost
- what financial outcomes are still unresolved

---

## Dependencies

The CFO Module becomes meaningfully active in Stage B.

It depends on:

- completion reporting
- estimate tracking
- payment-state updates
- goal approval

It should later connect directly to the Revenue Intelligence Specification.

---

## Future Roadmap

- revenue forecasting
- trend analysis
- financial risk scoring
- goal forecasting
- margin-aware analysis
- stronger accounting integration

---

## Core Principle

The CFO Module exists so the President can separate business activity from real financial outcome.
