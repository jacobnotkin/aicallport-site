## 1. Service Director Mission

Service Director is the job-execution and completion-continuity lane of AI-ABCX.

Its job is to:

- keep active service work visible from arrival through completion
- protect job execution quality and operating throughput
- preserve field notes, job outcomes, and completion truth
- surface service issues before they become customer or revenue damage
- keep backlog, bottlenecks, and completion pressure visible
- connect field execution to scheduling, dispatch, support, and customer-success truth
- make service performance visible to the President

It should not behave like a vague operations label.

It should behave like the corporate execution lane under President control.

---

## 2. Service Director Launch Standard

Service Director is launch-ready only when it can do all of the following:

1. show active service throughput clearly
2. show completed, delayed, and issue-flagged jobs clearly
3. preserve job execution notes and outcome truth
4. show where service bottlenecks exist
5. connect service issues to customer and follow-up records
6. report service pressure inside the President view
7. support A / B / C levels clearly
8. support credible guided-activation recommendations and connected bundles

If one of those is missing, the director is not complete.

---

## 3. Canonical Role Definition

## Public-facing name

`Service Director`

## Internal role meaning

The AI-ABCX director responsible for job execution flow, completion visibility, field continuity, service issue awareness, and operational throughput after work is assigned.

## Executive owner

`COO`

Service Director belongs to the COO lane because its purpose is to protect the real execution of work once the business has already captured and scheduled demand.

It also acts as a bridge between:

- Scheduling Director
- Dispatch Director
- Support Director
- Retention Director
- Sales Director
- President visibility

## 4. Commercial Promise

Service Director should promise this:

- clearer visibility into what work is actually happening
- fewer jobs falling into silent operational gaps
- stronger completion and backlog control
- faster identification of execution bottlenecks
- better job-outcome memory
- stronger President visibility into service reality
- better continuity between operations and customer experience

Service Director should not promise only “job tracking.”

It should promise:

- execution control
- completion visibility
- issue detection
- throughput discipline
- operational truth

Based on mainstream field-service systems, job-management software, and contractor operations platforms, Service Director must match the market on:

- service job visibility

- issue visibility

Service Director can beat outside tools in these areas:

- it can report directly into President control

- it can preserve execution truth as part of the full corporate structure instead of a disconnected job board

## 7. A / B / C Definition Draft

This checklist assumes these working level definitions.

They can be refined later, but implementation should move around this structure.

### Level A

- visible service throughput
- completed and open jobs are visible
- service issues are visible
- field reporting is usable
- President can see operational health

### Level B

- stronger service coordination
- clearer bottleneck logic
- better backlog and issue control
- stronger handoff to support, retention, and follow-up
- stronger completion discipline

### Level C

- advanced execution orchestration
- predictive service-pressure awareness
- richer issue and exception handling
- deeper cross-lane coordination with scheduling, dispatch, support, and customer-success
- stronger executive visibility into operating throughput

### Level X

- future fully autonomous service governance
- predictive completion management
- self-improving execution logic
- deeper board-level operating accountability

X should remain future-facing, not launch-blocking.

## 8. Required Service Categories

Service Director must support category-aware execution logic.

Required launch categories:

- active jobs
- completed jobs
- delayed jobs
- issue-flagged jobs
- backlog items
- completion-risk items
- field-note-dependent items
- service-follow-through items

Each category should remain distinguishable in the system.

## 9. Required Service States

At minimum, Service Director must support the following states:

- `job_active`
- `job_completed`
- `job_delayed`
- `service_issue_open`
- `backlog_visible`
- `completion_risk`
- `field_notes_pending`
- `follow_through_needed`
- `customer_impact_risk`
- `president_attention_needed`

Optional later-state refinement can be added after launch, but these are the minimum states needed for real service control.

---

## 10. Required Shared Service Record

Service Director must not operate on a disconnected execution layer.

It needs a shared service record that includes:

- customer identity
- job identity
- scheduled window
- assigned team / owner
- service status
- field notes
- completion notes
- issue status
- follow-up status
- revenue / business-impact context
- President-visible summary state

If service data is separate from the main operating truth, the director is not complete.

---

## 11. Required Product Surfaces

Service Director should not launch as hidden logic only.

It needs visible product surfaces.

### Surface 1: Executive Summary

Purpose:

- show current service operating picture
- summarize completion rate, backlog, and service issues
- give the President a clean execution reading

### Surface 2: Priorities

Purpose:

- show which jobs or operating issues need immediate attention
- show which throughput blockers matter most
- show where service continuity is weak

### Surface 3: Risks

Purpose:

- show backlog pressure
- show completion risk
- show customer-impact risk from service issues

### Surface 4: Dependencies

Purpose:

- show where service depends on scheduling, dispatch, materials, support, or follow-up action
- show which outside lane is blocking completion truth

These four surfaces align with the current dashboard pattern and should remain the minimum launch shape.

---

## 12. Required Dashboard Visibility

Service Director must be visible in:

- President Dashboard
- COO lane
- service-level operating records
- issue-aware execution views

At minimum the President should be able to understand:

- how many jobs are active
- how many are completed
- where service issues exist
- whether backlog is manageable
- whether execution quality is stable

---

## 13. Required Director Handoffs

Service Director must hand off cleanly to:

- Scheduling Director
  - schedule truth shapes execution truth
- Dispatch Director
  - assignment movement directly affects service throughput
- Support Director
  - service issues often become support issues
- Retention Director
  - poor execution weakens continuity and repeat-business likelihood
- Reviews / Referrals Director
  - execution quality affects public trust
- Follow-Up Director
  - unresolved service outcomes often require next-step control
- Sales Director
  - job outcomes can affect upsell, callbacks, and close confidence

If these handoffs are vague, the director is not real yet.

---

## 14. Guided Activation Requirements

Guided activation should help determine whether Service Director is needed and at what level.

Minimum qualifying questions:

- How many jobs does your company complete in a normal week or month?
- Do service teams ever struggle with backlog, job visibility, or completion consistency?
- Do managers clearly know what jobs are complete, delayed, or at risk?
- Are field notes and service outcomes always recorded clearly?
- Do service problems sometimes turn into customer complaints or lost repeat business?
- Is your business trying to improve operational throughput or service quality?

Service Director should be recommendable both:

- as part of broader operational control
- and as a core execution lane where active service delivery is central to the business

---

## 15. Pricing Decisions Required

Pricing is not finalized here, but the following decisions must be made before launch:

- whether Service Director is always included when certain service-heavy system combinations are recommended
- whether A / B / C pricing changes mostly reflect:
  - throughput depth
  - issue handling depth
  - reporting quality
  - exception intelligence
- whether service volume, crew size, or user count affects pricing directly
- whether Service Director is bundled more often with Scheduling and Dispatch in larger operating configurations

Until those decisions are made, Service Director is not commercially complete.

## 16. Build Sequence

Recommended build sequence:

1. normalize canonical role definition
2. normalize A / B / C service logic
3. normalize service categories and states
4. define shared service record structure
5. normalize dashboard tabs and execution metrics
6. define director handoff rules
7. connect guided-activation recommendation logic
8. test throughput and issue workflows
9. finalize launch pricing and bundle rules

Do not start with visuals only.

The operating logic has to exist first.

## 17. Internal Test Scenarios

Service Director is not ready until AI-ABCX can pass realistic internal tests.

Minimum test scenarios:

### Test 1: Active job with strong execution visibility

The system must show:

- the job is active
- current execution truth is visible
- owner and status are clear

### Test 2: Delayed completion

The system must show:

- the job is delayed
- backlog or completion pressure rises
- President visibility reflects the risk

### Test 3: Service issue creating customer impact

The system must show:

- the issue is operationally visible
- customer impact is visible
- handoff to Support / Follow-Up can happen cleanly

### Test 4: Field work completed but notes missing

The system must show:

- the job appears operationally close to complete
- but execution truth is incomplete
- the record stays visibly weak until notes are resolved

### Test 5: Healthy completion with clean continuity

The system must show:

- the job completes cleanly
- the outcome can hand off into Customer Success / Retention / Reviews if needed
- the operating record remains strong

If these tests fail, the director is not launch-ready.
