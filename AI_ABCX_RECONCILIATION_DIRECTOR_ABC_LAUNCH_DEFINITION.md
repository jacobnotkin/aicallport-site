# AI-ABCX Reconciliation Director A/B/C Launch Definition

## 1. Core Rule

Reconciliation Director is the CFO-controlled verification-truth lane inside AI-ABCX.

Its job is to:

- verify that recorded money truth matches actual verified business reality
- keep recorded finance activity separate from fully reconciled truth
- show unresolved mismatches clearly
- preserve visibility over pending finance items before month-close
- keep categorization drift visible before it pollutes reporting
- show whether finance truth is safe enough for the President to govern from
- reduce hidden ambiguity between recorded numbers and trusted verified numbers
- support cleaner month-close confidence across the company
- make financial correction work visible instead of buried

This director should not feel like a generic accounting checklist.

It should feel like the part of the system that tells the President whether the company’s visible financial record is actually verified, what is still unresolved, and what still blocks trusted close readiness.

---

## 2. Public Meaning Of Each Level

### Level A

**Visible reconciliation truth**

The business can clearly see what is reconciled, what is still pending, where mismatches exist, and whether month-close truth is safe enough to trust.

### Level B

**Connected reconciliation workflow**

The business does not just see open finance issues. It can manage ownership, mismatch classification, period-close discipline, and stronger verification flow between Revenue Control, Accounting, and reporting.

### Level C

**Advanced reconciliation orchestration**

The system actively helps prioritize verification work, recognize recurring finance-truth drift, surface deeper root causes, and guide executive attention before unresolved reconciliation issues distort decisions.

---

## 3. Level A Definition

Level A means the company has visible reconciliation oversight and basic finance-verification control.

At Level A, Reconciliation Director should:

- show reconciliation status clearly
- show unresolved mismatches clearly
- preserve pending finance item visibility
- preserve month-close checklist visibility
- show basic categorization-integrity visibility
- separate recorded numbers from verified numbers
- show the President where finance truth is still unconfirmed
- keep close-readiness understandable without forcing the owner into accounting interpretation

Level A is the minimum launch standard for this director.

It creates verification visibility first.

Without this visibility, the business can act on partially corrected records, unresolved pending items, and month-close assumptions instead of trusted financial truth.

---

## 4. Level B Definition

Level B means the company has stronger verification workflow control instead of only visible reconciliation status.

At Level B, Reconciliation Director should add:

- stronger mismatch classification
- clearer issue ownership
- better pending-item discipline
- stronger linkage to Revenue Control and Accounting
- stronger period-close review flow
- better visibility into recurring reconciliation drift
- clearer correction accountability before close

Level B should make reconciliation manageable and accountable, not just visible.

---

## 5. Level C Definition

Level C means the company has advanced finance-verification orchestration across mismatches, pending items, close readiness, and executive decision support.

At Level C, Reconciliation Director should add:

- stronger automated mismatch prioritization
- richer root-cause visibility
- deeper recurring-pattern visibility
- stronger cross-lane finance-truth control
- stronger executive guidance before close
- more advanced correction prioritization
- stronger protection against acting on unreconciled financial truth

Level C should feel like a real management layer for verified finance-governance discipline.

---

## 6. Exact Launch Difference Between A, B, And C

### A vs B

Level A shows reconciliation truth.

Level B adds stronger mismatch classification, issue ownership, pending-item discipline, reconciliation linkage, and period-close review flow.

### B vs C

Level B manages reconciliation issues more clearly.

Level C adds deeper prioritization, root-cause visibility, recurring-drift recognition, and stronger executive guidance.

### Plain-English Lock

- A = we can see what financial truth is still unverified
- B = we can manage reconciliation issues and close-readiness more reliably
- C = the system helps manage verification truth strategically before unresolved finance issues distort decisions

---

## 7. Dependency Guidance

Reconciliation Director has meaningful dependencies, but it becomes especially powerful when connected across the whole operating system.

### Can function at Level A with light dependency

Yes, if the business mainly needs:

- reconciliation visibility
- unresolved mismatch visibility
- pending finance item visibility
- month-close awareness
- President-level clarity on what is still unverified

### Becomes much stronger with these connected directors

- **Revenue Control Director**
  Creates the expected-vs-confirmed revenue truth that reconciliation must verify.
- **Accounting Director**
  Supports close discipline, categorization integrity, and correction workflow.
- **Payroll Director**
  Helps preserve cleaner financial truth where labor-related records affect period confidence.
- **Executive Reporting Director**
  Turns verified finance status into President-facing control signal.
- **Strategy Director**
  Uses verified month-close truth for higher-confidence planning and growth decisions.

### Recommended dependency logic

- Level A can be sold with light dependencies
- Level B is much stronger with Revenue Control and Accounting
- Level C should usually be recommended with Revenue Control, Accounting, Executive Reporting, and Strategy connected, with Payroll included where finance complexity is growing

---

## 8. Recommendation Logic Guidance

Recommend Reconciliation Director when the company says things like:

- “We are not always sure the recorded numbers are fully verified.”
- “Month-close confidence comes too late.”
- “Pending finance items stay open too long.”
- “We need the owner to know what is real versus what is still being corrected.”
- “Categorization or correction drift makes reporting harder to trust.”
- “We want stronger confidence before acting on financial reports.”

### Recommend Level A when

- the business needs verification visibility first
- the company is still relatively simple
- the owner mainly wants to know what is reconciled versus still pending

### Recommend Level B when

- mismatches are recurring
- multiple people affect finance truth
- period-close discipline matters
- the company wants stronger ownership and correction flow before ambiguity compounds

### Recommend Level C when

- the business is scaling complexity
- unresolved finance issues can materially distort decisions
- recurring verification drift is becoming expensive
- the owner wants stronger executive guidance instead of after-the-fact correction awareness

---

## 9. Business-Friendly Explanation For Customers

Reconciliation Director helps your company verify whether the financial record is actually correct and trusted.

At Level A, it shows what is reconciled, what is still pending, where mismatches exist, and whether month-close truth is safe enough for the President to trust.

At Level B, it adds stronger control by showing who owns unresolved issues, how mismatches are classified, and whether period-close discipline is becoming weak before the record is considered safe.

At Level C, it becomes a stronger finance-verification orchestration layer that helps the President and CFO see which issues matter most, which patterns are repeating, and what should be corrected first before weak finance truth affects company decisions.

---

## 10. What Must Be Avoided

Do not describe this director as only:

- a bookkeeping clean-up tool
- a passive close checklist
- a generic accounting dashboard
- a duplicate of Revenue Control Director
- a duplicate of Executive Reporting Director

That would undersell the real function.

The correct launch meaning is:

**Reconciliation Director is the finance-verification control lane that separates recorded money from trusted verified truth, keeps unresolved mismatches visible, and protects the President from governing from partially corrected records inside the AI-ABCX operating system.**

Also avoid:

- collapsing Reconciliation into Revenue Control
- collapsing Reconciliation into Accounting
- treating month-close as “safe” while pending items or categorization drift remain unresolved

This director exists because verified truth needs its own governance lane.
