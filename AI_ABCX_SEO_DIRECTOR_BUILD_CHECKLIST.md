# AI-ABCX SEO Director Build Checklist

This document turns `SEO Director` from a partial launch concept into a normalized AI-ABCX director build checklist.

Its purpose is to answer one direct execution question:

`What exactly must be true for SEO Director to become a real launch-ready director inside AI-ABCX?`

This checklist stays aligned with the existing corporate map, dashboard logic, and broader growth-control architecture already present in the project.

---

## 1. SEO Director Mission

`SEO Director` is the organic search visibility and local discovery lane inside AI-ABCX.

Its job is to make search visibility operate like a governed business function instead of a vague marketing hope.

It must help the President understand:

- whether organic demand is growing or stalling
- whether priority pages and briefs are ready
- whether search intent matches the company’s real services
- where execution gaps are blocking growth
- whether website structure is helping or hurting discoverability
- what SEO action should happen next

This director is not a generic SEO rank tracker.

It is the disciplined organic-demand lane inside the same President-led corporate structure.

---

## 2. SEO Director Launch Standard

`SEO Director` is launch-ready only if it already feels like a real operating lane.

It must not feel like:

- a vague “SEO score”
- a loose content checklist
- a disconnected agency report
- a duplicate of Marketing Director

It must feel like:

- structured organic demand visibility
- brief and page-execution control
- intent-fit discipline
- clear dependency visibility with Website and Marketing
- President-level explanation of what is blocked and what should happen next

At launch, the director should already be able to answer:

- is SEO growing?
- what priority pages are active?
- what briefs are missing?
- what pages are blocked?
- is website fit helping or hurting search growth?
- what is the next SEO priority?

---

## 3. Canonical Role Definition

### Public name

`SEO Director`

### Executive lane

`CMO`

### Core role

Own organic search visibility, local discovery readiness, priority-page execution, intent-fit discipline, and organic growth reporting inside AI-ABCX.

### Distinction from related directors

- `Marketing Director` owns broader growth coordination across channels
- `Advertising Director` owns paid demand execution
- `Website Director` owns website structure and website readiness
- `Lead Generation Director` owns incoming demand quality and routing
- `SEO Director` owns the organic discovery lane specifically

---

## 4. Commercial Promise

The commercial promise of `SEO Director` is:

`Your business becomes easier to discover through structured organic growth instead of scattered page publishing.`

The customer should feel AI-ABCX gives them:

- clearer visibility into organic growth
- disciplined execution of pages and briefs
- stronger alignment between what customers search and what the business actually sells
- less wasted content effort
- clearer next steps for sustainable organic growth

---

## 5. What AI-ABCX Must Match

To be credible at launch, `SEO Director` must match core expectations for:

- visibility into priority pages
- visibility into content / brief readiness
- visibility into blocked pages
- basic intent-fit assessment
- local service business relevance
- practical prioritization rather than vague SEO theory

At minimum, it must feel as useful as a modern SEO operations surface for a service business.

---

## 6. Where AI-ABCX Can Beat The Market

AI-ABCX can beat typical SEO tools by treating SEO as part of one shared operating system rather than a separate analytics subscription.

Main AI-ABCX advantages:

- President-level visibility instead of isolated specialist reporting
- direct connection between SEO, Website, Lead Generation, Marketing, Sales, and paid outcomes
- visibility into execution gaps, not just rankings
- dependency discipline around briefs, website fit, and distribution
- organic growth decisions framed as business actions rather than vanity metrics

The winning promise is:

`SEO becomes managed organic demand, not disconnected content activity.`

## 7. A / B / C Definition Draft

### SEO Director A

Foundational organic visibility control.

Must include:

- priority-page visibility
- brief-gap visibility
- blocked-page visibility
- basic intent-fit status
- basic local discovery readiness
- President summary of SEO health

### SEO Director B

Connected organic growth automation.

Must include everything in A, plus:

- page readiness by service line
- stronger website dependency visibility
- stronger lead-intent feedback from organic demand
- stronger publishing-queue control
- organic contribution visibility to lead quality

### SEO Director C

Advanced organic growth management.

Must include everything in B, plus:

- stronger recommendation engine
- stronger priority ranking across pages / briefs / service lines
- growth-risk warnings
- stronger market and service-line comparison
- President-facing next-action recommendations

### SEO Director X

Highest future intelligent orchestration level.

Not required for launch, but architecture should not block it.

## 13. Required Director Handoffs

`SEO Director` must hand off cleanly to and from:

### Marketing Director

Marketing owns broader growth coordination.

SEO must supply organic-demand truth into that lane.

### Website Director

SEO depends heavily on website fit, page structure, and website readiness.

### Lead Generation Director

Organic traffic should connect to visible lead quality, not just page count.

### Advertising Director

SEO and paid demand should be visible as distinct but comparable growth lanes.

### Sales Director

Organic demand should eventually connect to sold outcomes and business quality.

### Follow-Up Director

If organic demand enters but does not progress, follow-up weakness must be visible.

---

## 14. Guided Activation Requirements

The activation flow should ask enough simple questions to qualify SEO correctly.

Questions should cover:

- do they rely on Google / organic discovery now?
- do they have service pages already?
- do they know which services are searched most?
- do they feel their website is easy to find?
- do they create pages or content now?
- do they feel search traffic is weak, unclear, or untracked?
- do they want more local discovery, stronger service visibility, or stronger inbound lead quality?

SEO Director should not be assigned because “SEO sounds good.”

It should be assigned because the business actually needs organic discovery structure.

---

## 15. Pricing Decisions Required

Before launch, these decisions must be finalized:

- can `SEO Director` be sold independently?
- when should it require `Website Director` support?
- when should it be bundled into broader `Marketing Director` logic?
- what separates A vs B vs C commercially?
- does page count, service-line depth, or execution scope affect pricing?

Strong likelihood:

- A should support visibility and discipline
- B should support connected execution and stronger organic-demand logic
- C should support full management guidance and prioritization

## 16. Build Sequence

Recommended build sequence:

1. normalize the role definition
2. confirm relationship to Marketing and Website
3. define SEO categories and states
4. normalize dashboard surfaces already implied in config
5. define A / B / C gating logic
6. define activation questions
7. define organic-demand / lead connection points
8. build or normalize demo/static product surfaces
9. run internal scenarios
10. mark launch readiness

---

## 17. Internal Test Scenarios

The director should be tested against at least these scenarios:

### Scenario 1

Organic lift looks positive, but priority briefs are missing.

Expected result:

SEO should show growth opportunity constrained by execution gaps.

### Scenario 2

Pages are ready, but website fit is weak.

Expected result:

SEO should hand off clearly to Website Director instead of pretending the issue is purely content.

### Scenario 3

Many pages are published, but intent fit is weak.

Expected result:

SEO should surface intent drift and recommend tighter service alignment.

### Scenario 4

Blocked pages are delaying roadmap expansion.

Expected result:

SEO should rank blocked execution above “more content” activity.

### Scenario 5

Organic demand increases, but lead quality stays weak.

Expected result:

SEO should not report success only from traffic or visibility; it should connect to lead quality truth.
