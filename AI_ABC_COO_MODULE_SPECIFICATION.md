# Bring Corporate Structure to Service Businesses

# COO Module Specification

## Purpose

The `COO Module` is the operations management layer of AI-ABC.

It turns opportunities and bookings into visible execution.

It exists to make sure that work:

- gets assigned
- gets confirmed
- gets completed
- gets reported

without relying on informal follow-up or fragmented supervision.

---

## Mission

Ensure that business activity moves from scheduled work into completed work.

---

## Primary Responsibilities

- monitor appointments
- monitor staff responsiveness
- track confirmations
- trigger reminders
- trigger automatic rerouting
- surface operational bottlenecks
- track completion reporting
- monitor capacity and workload pressure
- support execution visibility for the President

---

## Inputs

- appointment creation events
- service assignments
- sales assignments
- staff availability
- confirmation status
- reminder state
- reroute state
- completion reports
- no-show events
- reschedules
- workflow timing

---

## Outputs

- operations summary
- appointment status visibility
- staff responsiveness summary
- completion summary
- capacity alerts
- bottleneck alerts
- reroute events
- execution recommendations

---

## KPIs

- appointment confirmation rate
- average confirmation time
- reminder count
- reroute count
- completion reporting rate
- completion delay rate
- staff responsiveness rate
- active capacity pressure
- operational bottleneck count

---

## Reports

### Daily Operations Report

- bookings created
- bookings confirmed
- bookings rerouted
- overdue confirmations
- completions received
- completions missing

### Weekly Operations Report

- staff responsiveness trends
- recurring bottlenecks
- reroute patterns
- capacity pressure by team or market
- completion discipline

### Exception Report

- unconfirmed appointments
- repeated staff delays
- incomplete reporting
- execution drift requiring escalation

---

## Recommendations

- reroute to another staff member
- increase availability in specific time blocks
- address recurring non-confirmation by a specific role
- review a bottleneck in one market or service line
- adjust scheduling logic to reduce failure points

---

## Dashboard Position

In the President dashboard, the COO Module should appear as the main execution layer.

It should answer:

- what is booked
- what is confirmed
- what is slipping
- what is completed

---

## Dependencies

The COO Module becomes active in Stage B.

It depends on:

- scheduling
- staff dashboards
- confirmations
- completion reporting
- reminders and rerouting logic

---

## Future Roadmap

- capacity forecasting
- route and schedule optimization
- recurring bottleneck detection
- workload balancing
- autonomous execution recommendations
- tighter integration with Stage C enforcement logic

---

## Core Principle

The COO Module makes sure the business does not confuse scheduled activity with completed outcomes.
