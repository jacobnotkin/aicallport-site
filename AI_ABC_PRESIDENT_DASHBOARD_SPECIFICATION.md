# Bring Corporate Structure to Service Businesses

# President Dashboard Specification

## Purpose

The President dashboard is the heart of AI-ABC.

It is the primary owner-facing operating surface where all active executive modules come together into one command center.

This dashboard should not feel like a generic admin panel.

It should feel like the President’s control surface for the company.

---

## What The President Sees First

The first screen should answer:

- what is happening now
- what needs attention first
- what money is unresolved
- which opportunities matter most
- where execution is drifting

## First-view layout

1. Executive Health Summary
2. Top Priority Actions
3. Revenue Status
4. Opportunity Status
5. Execution Status
6. Approvals / Exceptions / Risks

---

## Daily Briefing

The daily briefing should be short, ranked, and decision-ready.

It should include:

- today’s top priorities
- new opportunities
- missed opportunities
- appointments that need attention
- revenue requiring reconciliation
- unresolved approvals or exceptions
- short CEO summary

### Daily briefing outcome

The President should finish the daily briefing knowing:

- what to act on now
- what can wait
- what may become a problem if ignored

---

## Weekly Briefing

The weekly briefing should be more analytical than the daily view.

It should include:

- weekly goal progress
- lead and opportunity summary
- appointment and execution summary
- revenue received / pending / lost
- operational bottlenecks
- staff accountability concerns
- open approvals or escalations
- executive recommendations

### Weekly briefing outcome

The President should understand whether the company is moving in the right direction.

---

## Monthly Review

The monthly review should be the highest-level operating review inside AI-ABC.

It should include:

- revenue performance
- goal performance
- opportunity trends
- conversion trends
- execution reliability
- unresolved risks
- recurring problems
- recommended changes for next month

### Monthly review outcome

The President should be able to:

- approve new goals
- change focus
- adjust priorities
- respond to risk

---

## Goal Approval Process

Goals should not be silently imposed by the system.

The President remains responsible for goal approval.

### Process

1. AI-ABC recommends a goal.
2. The recommendation explains why.
3. The President can:
   - approve
   - modify
   - reject
4. Once approved, executive modules align around the goal.

### Examples

- monthly revenue goal
- conversion improvement goal
- reconciliation completion goal
- response-speed goal

---

## Financial Reconciliation Process

The President dashboard must include a clear financial reconciliation surface.

### Process

1. Completed jobs are checked against estimate records.
2. Missing financial outcomes are flagged.
3. The President or financial delegate updates the result:
   - paid
   - partial
   - open
   - lost
4. The CFO layer updates reporting and goal progress.

### Required President view

- completed jobs missing outcome
- large open estimates
- partial payments
- revenue at risk
- goal impact

---

## Active Executive Views

The President dashboard should include executive views for:

- CEO
- COO
- CRO
- CFO
- CAO
- future CMO
- future HR

These should feel unified, not like disconnected apps.

---

## Key Widgets Or Zones

- executive summary banner
- recommended actions queue
- revenue truth panel
- opportunity flow panel
- execution flow panel
- approval and exception queue
- goals panel
- business health score

---

## Core Principle

The President dashboard should help the owner run the business through AI-ABC instead of merely checking data inside it.
