# CAO Module Specification

## Purpose

The `CAO Module` is the governance and administrative control layer of AI-ABC.

It exists to make sure exceptions, approvals, and policy-sensitive actions do not disappear into informal communication.

---

## Mission

Turn approvals, exceptions, and governance controls into visible and manageable business activity.

---

## Primary Responsibilities

- route approvals
- manage exception handling
- manage escalation paths
- enforce policy-sensitive controls
- surface unresolved administrative risks
- keep governance decisions visible

---

## Inputs

- approval requests
- access-change requests
- exception events
- workflow violations
- unresolved escalations
- policy override requests
- owner decisions

---

## Outputs

- approval queue
- exception queue
- escalation summary
- policy-enforcement alerts
- governance visibility for the President

---

## KPIs

- pending approvals
- approval resolution speed
- open exceptions
- escalation volume
- policy override frequency
- unresolved governance items

---

## Reports

### Approval Report

- requests pending
- requests approved
- requests denied
- delayed decisions

### Exception Report

- operational exceptions
- repeated exception types
- unresolved exception cases

### Governance Report

- policy overrides
- escalation frequency
- unresolved high-risk items

---

## Recommendations

- prioritize delayed approvals
- investigate a repeated exception type
- tighten one policy-sensitive workflow
- escalate a governance item to owner review

---

## Dashboard Position

The CAO Module should appear in Stage C and later executive views as the control layer for decisions that should not remain informal.

---

## Dependencies

The CAO Module depends on:

- approvals routing
- exception handling
- escalation logic
- workflow enforcement

---

## Future Roadmap

- policy-rule engines
- approval delegation logic
- governance scorecards
- exception pattern learning
- tighter Stage C enforcement integration

---

## Core Principle

The CAO Module should make governance visible before administrative disorder turns into operational or revenue damage.
