# AI-ABCX Customer Success Director A/B/C Launch Definition

## 1. Core Rule

Customer Success Director is the post-sale continuity and customer-health lane of AI-ABCX.

Its job is to control:

- customer-health visibility
- retention-risk visibility
- complaint continuity
- review-response discipline
- referral readiness
- support follow-through visibility
- customer-memory continuity across the system

It is not the same as:

- Support Director, which controls active issue response and resolution
- Follow-Up Director, which controls unresolved next-step discipline
- Reviews / Referrals logic by itself, which is only one part of broader continuity
- Service Director, which controls execution truth

Customer Success Director controls the health of the customer relationship after the business already won the work.

---

## 2. Public Meaning Of Each Level

### Level A
Visible customer-health truth.

### Level B
Connected customer-success workflow.

### Level C
Advanced customer-success orchestration.

---

## 3. Level A Definition

Level A Customer Success Director means the business can clearly see:

- which customers are healthy
- which customers show risk
- which complaints are open
- which reviews need response
- which follow-through items remain unresolved
- which customers are referral-ready
- where continuity pressure is building

Level A is a visibility-and-continuity layer first.

At Level A:

- customer trust signals are visible
- retention risk is visible
- unresolved continuity issues do not disappear
- customer history remains tied to the operating record
- the President can see customer-health pressure

Level A is the minimum level required for real customer-success control.

---

## 4. Level B Definition

Level B Customer Success Director means customer continuity becomes more coordinated, not just visible.

At Level B:

- retention logic is stronger
- complaint and review handling are more structured
- continuity ownership is clearer
- customer-success work connects more cleanly to service and follow-up
- unresolved trust issues move through stronger discipline
- customer-health management becomes a connected workflow

Level B turns customer success from visible continuity into an organized post-sale operating system.

This is the level where the business starts feeling that relationships are being managed intentionally, not just observed after problems happen.

---

## 5. Level C Definition

Level C Customer Success Director means customer continuity becomes a high-control relationship lane.

At Level C:

- customer-risk is surfaced earlier
- continuity intervention logic is stronger
- complaint, review, and retention movement are more structured
- customer-friction patterns are more visible
- cross-lane coordination is deeper
- executive visibility into customer-health movement is stronger

Level C is for businesses that want a more advanced customer-success system with stronger continuity control and earlier trust-risk visibility.

This is the most complete version of customer-success orchestration at launch.

---

## 6. Exact Launch Difference Between A, B, And C

### A = visible customer-health truth

The company can clearly see:

- healthy customers
- at-risk customers
- open complaints
- review-response pressure
- unresolved continuity items
- referral-ready customers

### B = connected customer-success workflow

Everything in A, plus:

- stronger retention discipline
- stronger complaint and review handling
- stronger continuity ownership
- stronger follow-through coordination
- stronger customer-health workflow structure

### C = advanced customer-success orchestration

Everything in B, plus:

- stronger risk detection
- stronger intervention logic
- stronger continuity intelligence
- stronger cross-lane relationship coordination
- stronger executive visibility into customer-health movement

---

## 7. Dependency Guidance

Customer Success Director can exist with partial independence, but it becomes strongest when paired with adjacent trust and continuity lanes.

### Can Customer Success Director exist by itself?

Yes, in a limited form.

This makes sense when a company already has:

- a real customer base
- repeat customers
- complaint or review pressure
- retention concerns
- a need for customer-health visibility first

### Strongest pairings

Customer Success Director becomes much stronger with:

- Support Director
- Follow-Up Director
- Service Director
- Call Handling Director
- Marketing Director
- Reviews / Referrals logic

### Practical rule

- Customer Success A can stand alone more credibly
- Customer Success B usually works best with at least one adjacent director
- Customer Success C works best as part of a broader continuity and growth system

---

## 8. Recommendation Logic Guidance

Recommend **Customer Success Director A** when:

- customer issues or complaints are visible but not well organized
- the business does not clearly know which customers are healthy or drifting
- review pressure exists
- leadership needs continuity visibility first

Recommend **Customer Success Director B** when:

- retention logic needs improvement
- complaints and review-response discipline need structure
- service and follow-up outcomes are not connecting cleanly to customer continuity
- ownership of customer-health movement is weak

Recommend **Customer Success Director C** when:

- the company wants a higher-control customer-success lane
- repeat business matters heavily
- trust and retention risks are operationally costly
- referral growth matters
- leadership wants deeper visibility into customer-health movement

---

## 9. Business-Friendly Explanation For Customers

### Customer Success Director A

You can clearly see which customers are healthy, which ones are at risk, which complaints or review issues are still open, and where continuity needs attention.

### Customer Success Director B

Your customer-success lane becomes more organized and coordinated, with stronger control over retention, complaints, reviews, and unresolved customer follow-through.

### Customer Success Director C

Your customer-success lane becomes a higher-control relationship system with stronger risk visibility, better intervention logic, and deeper coordination across the business.

---

## 10. What Must Be Avoided

Do not describe Customer Success Director as:

- just support
- only complaint handling
- only reviews
- only referral tracking
- only retention reminders

That makes it too narrow.

Customer Success Director is the operating truth of post-sale customer continuity.

Also avoid pretending:

- A is heavily predictive
- B is the same as C
- C is only “more reporting”

The level differences must stay operationally meaningful.
