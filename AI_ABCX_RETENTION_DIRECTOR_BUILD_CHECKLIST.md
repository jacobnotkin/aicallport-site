## 1. Retention Director Mission

Retention Director is the continuity and repeat-relationship lane of AI-ABCX.

Its job is to:

- protect ongoing customer relationships after the first sale or service event
- make repeat-business opportunity visible
- surface churn risk before the customer quietly disappears
- preserve next-step clarity after each meaningful interaction
- keep continuity gaps from becoming lost revenue
- connect support, follow-up, service outcomes, and customer-success truth
- make retention pressure visible to the President

It should not behave like a vague “customer loyalty” concept.

It should behave like a corporate continuity lane under President control.

---

## 2. Retention Director Launch Standard

Retention Director is launch-ready only when it can do all of the following:

1. show customer continuity clearly
2. distinguish healthy repeat relationships from watch accounts
3. show where the next step is missing or unclear
4. show where reopen risk or churn risk exists
5. keep retention risk visibly connected to the main customer record
6. report continuity pressure inside the President view
7. support A / B / C levels clearly
8. support credible guided-activation recommendations and connected bundles

If one of those is missing, the director is not complete.

## 3. Canonical Role Definition

## Public-facing name

`Retention Director`

## Internal role meaning

The AI-ABCX director responsible for customer continuity, repeat-business visibility, churn-risk awareness, next-step clarity, and relationship preservation after the initial demand event.

## Executive owner

`CSO`

Retention Director belongs to the customer-success lane because its purpose is to prevent customer relationships from fading due to weak continuity, weak follow-through, or poor visibility.

It also acts as a bridge between:

- Customer Success
- Support
- Follow-Up
- Reviews / Referrals
- Marketing
- President visibility

Retention Director should not promise only “reminders.”

## 7. A / B / C Definition Draft

This checklist assumes these working level definitions.

They can be refined later, but implementation should move around this structure.

### Level A

- basic continuity visibility
- watch accounts are visible
- next-step clarity is visible
- reopen risk is visible
- President can see customer continuity pressure

### Level B

- stronger repeat-relationship structure
- clearer continuity ownership
- stronger coordination with support and follow-up
- more structured churn-risk handling
- stronger repeat-opportunity visibility

### Level C

- advanced continuity orchestration
- stronger predictive churn prevention
- deeper retention intelligence
- stronger coordination with customer-success, support, reviews, and marketing
- richer executive visibility into relationship movement

### Level X

- future fully autonomous customer-retention governance
- predictive lifecycle modeling
- self-improving continuity and repeat-growth logic
- deeper board-level relationship accountability

X should remain future-facing, not launch-blocking.

## 8. Required Retention Categories

Retention Director must support category-aware continuity logic.

Required launch categories:

- healthy repeat customers
- watch accounts
- churn-risk accounts
- reopen-risk accounts
- next-step-missing accounts
- recent-service continuity items
- repeat-opportunity accounts
- continuity-gap items

Each category should remain distinguishable in the system.

## 9. Required Retention States

At minimum, Retention Director must support the following states:

- `relationship_healthy`
- `watch_account`
- `next_step_defined`
- `next_step_missing`
- `reopen_risk`
- `churn_risk`
- `repeat_opportunity`
- `continuity_gap`
- `follow_through_needed`
- `president_attention_needed`

Optional later-state refinement can be added after launch, but these are the minimum states needed for real retention control.

---

## 10. Required Shared Retention Record

Retention Director must not operate on a disconnected reminder layer.

It needs a shared retention record that includes:

- customer identity
- service history
- recent support / complaint context
- latest interaction date
- next-step status
- continuity health
- repeat-opportunity signals
- churn-risk signals
- assigned owner
- recommended next action
- President-visible summary state

If retention data is separate from the main operating truth, the director is not complete.

---

## 11. Required Product Surfaces

Retention Director should not launch as hidden logic only.

It needs visible product surfaces.

### Surface 1: Executive Summary

Purpose:

- show current continuity picture
- summarize healthy accounts, watch accounts, and next-step clarity
- give the President a clean retention reading

### Surface 2: Priorities

Purpose:

- show which accounts need continuity action now
- show where repeat-business opportunity is being lost
- show where reopen or churn pressure is strongest

### Surface 3: Risks

Purpose:

- show continuity breakdown
- show churn pressure
- show weak follow-through risk

### Surface 4: Dependencies

Purpose:

- show where retention depends on support, follow-up, service, or marketing action
- show which outside lane is blocking continuity

These four surfaces match the current dashboard evidence and should remain the minimum launch shape.

Retention Director must be visible in:

- which customers are healthy

## 13. Required Director Handoffs

Retention Director must hand off cleanly to:

- Customer Success Director
  - retention is one of the broadest customer-success outcomes
- Support Director
  - unresolved support weakens continuity
- Follow-Up Director
  - next-step execution is often a follow-up problem
- Reviews / Referrals Director
  - healthy continuity can become advocacy, weak continuity can become public trust risk
- Marketing Director
  - retention may require targeted re-engagement or repeat-nurture logic
- Sales Director
  - repeat opportunity may return to the sales lane
- Scheduling Director
  - missed or unclear appointment truth can damage continuity

If these handoffs are vague, the director is not real yet.

---

## 14. Guided Activation Requirements

Guided activation should help determine whether Retention Director is needed and at what level.

Minimum qualifying questions:

- Do past customers come back consistently, or do many disappear after the first job?
- Do you know which customers are most likely to buy again?
- Do customer relationships ever fade because there is no clear next step?
- Do you struggle to keep track of who needs follow-up after service?
- Do unresolved issues or delays sometimes reduce repeat business?
- Is repeat revenue important to your growth plan?

Retention Director should be recommendable both:

- as part of broader customer-success continuity
- and as a more commercial repeat-business / relationship-protection lane

## 15. Pricing Decisions Required

Pricing is not finalized here, but the following decisions must be made before launch:

- whether Retention Director is sold only inside broader customer-success combinations or can stand commercially on its own
- whether A / B / C pricing changes mostly reflect:
  - continuity depth
  - churn-risk logic
  - repeat-opportunity intelligence
  - level of automation
- whether retention is bundled more often with Follow-Up, Support, or Reviews / Referrals
- whether number of users or customer volume affects pricing directly

Until those decisions are made, Retention Director is not commercially complete.

## 17. Internal Test Scenarios

Retention Director is not ready until AI-ABCX can pass realistic internal tests.

Minimum test scenarios:

### Test 1: Watch account with no clear next step

The system must show:

- the account is not fully healthy
- next-step clarity is weak
- continuity pressure is visible

### Test 2: Repeat opportunity with no follow-through

The system must show:

- repeat-business opportunity exists
- follow-through is missing
- the relationship is at risk of fading

### Test 3: Service problem creating churn pressure

The system must show:

- service or support friction affects continuity
- the customer becomes a watch account or churn-risk account
- the cross-lane dependency is visible

### Test 4: Reopen risk after recent service

The system must show:

- the record looks operationally closed
- but continuity still carries reopen risk
- President visibility reflects the risk

### Test 5: Healthy repeat customer ready for advocacy

The system must show:

- the relationship is healthy
- repeat potential is strong
- the record can hand off to Reviews / Referrals cleanly

If these tests fail, the director is not launch-ready.
