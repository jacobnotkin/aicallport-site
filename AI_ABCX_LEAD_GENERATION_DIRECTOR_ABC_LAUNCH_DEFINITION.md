# AI-ABCX Lead Generation Director A/B/C Launch Definition

## 1. Core Rule

Lead Generation Director is the CRO-controlled demand-entry control lane inside AI-ABCX.

Its job is to:

- make incoming demand visible by source
- protect lead quality over raw lead quantity
- classify inbound opportunities correctly at the point of entry
- keep ownership and routing visible
- prevent weak-fit, duplicate, or aging leads from distorting the system
- preserve a clean handoff into sales and follow-up
- report demand quality pressure directly to the President

This director should not feel like a vague marketing summary.

It should feel like a structured inbound-demand operating lane under President control.

---

## 2. Public Meaning Of Each Level

### Level A

**Visible demand-entry truth**

The business can clearly see where new demand is coming from, whether it looks strong or weak, and whether it has been routed correctly.

### Level B

**Connected qualification and routing workflow**

The business does not just see new leads. It can qualify them more clearly, compare source quality, and keep routing, response, and handoff more controlled.

### Level C

**Advanced demand-quality orchestration**

The system actively prioritizes stronger opportunities, protects the revenue lane from weak or duplicated demand, and gives deeper President and CRO visibility into source quality drift.

---

## 3. Level A Definition

Level A means the company has visible lead intake and basic qualification truth.

At Level A, Lead Generation Director should:

- show incoming demand by source clearly
- preserve source labeling consistently
- show basic fit logic
- show clear ownership and routing states
- distinguish high-fit from low-fit demand
- show duplicate-review pressure
- show unrouted demand visibly
- give the President top-line demand quality visibility

Level A is the minimum launch standard for this director.

It creates truth first.

Without this truth, demand looks larger than it really is and weak-fit inquiries waste time inside the system.

---

## 4. Level B Definition

Level B means the company has stronger qualification and routing workflow instead of just visible intake.

At Level B, Lead Generation Director should add:

- stronger qualification handling
- better source comparison
- better queue and response visibility
- stronger coordination with Sales Director and Follow-Up Director
- better visibility into where leads stall or weaken
- cleaner handoff preservation from intake into the revenue lane

Level B should make inbound demand manageable and accountable, not just visible.

---

## 5. Level C Definition

Level C means the company has advanced multi-source demand-quality orchestration across entry, prioritization, routing, and downstream coordination.

At Level C, Lead Generation Director should add:

- deeper source-level prioritization
- tighter autonomous routing logic
- stronger opportunity ranking
- deeper coordination with Advertising Director and Website Director optimization
- richer President and CRO visibility into demand mix and quality drift
- stronger protection against weak-fit, duplicate, and aging demand contaminating the system

Level C should feel like a real operating layer for demand quality control.

---

## 6. Exact Launch Difference Between A, B, And C

### A vs B

Level A shows demand-entry truth.

Level B adds stronger qualification, source comparison, routing discipline, and handoff structure.

### B vs C

Level B manages intake and routing more clearly.

Level C adds deeper prioritization, stronger autonomous logic, and executive-level visibility into demand quality drift.

### Plain-English Lock

- A = we can see incoming demand clearly
- B = we can qualify and route incoming demand more reliably
- C = the system orchestrates demand quality and prioritization strategically

---

## 7. Dependency Guidance

Lead Generation Director can operate independently once demand sources exist, but it becomes much stronger when tied into the rest of the growth and revenue system.

### Can function at Level A with light dependency

Yes, if the business mainly needs:

- source visibility
- fit visibility
- queue visibility
- routing visibility

### Becomes much stronger with these connected directors

- **Website Director**
  Supplies form and funnel capture infrastructure.
- **Advertising Director**
  Supplies paid demand that must be judged by source quality.
- **Marketing Director**
  Places source performance inside broader growth coordination.
- **Call Handling Director**
  Converts inbound call demand into visible classified opportunity.
- **Sales Director**
  Receives qualified opportunities and preserves revenue-lane continuity.
- **Follow-Up Director**
  Handles aging or not-yet-converted demand.
- **Estimator Director**
  Becomes stronger when qualified demand can move directly into quoting logic.
- **Reactivation Director**
  Helps distinguish truly new demand from returning or legacy demand.

### Recommended dependency logic

- Level A can be sold with light dependencies
- Level B is much stronger with Sales and Follow-Up
- Level C should usually be recommended with multiple connected demand and revenue lanes, especially Website, Advertising, Call Handling, and Sales

---

## 8. Recommendation Logic Guidance

Recommend Lead Generation Director when the company says things like:

- “We get leads from more than one place.”
- “Not all of our leads are good.”
- “We don’t know which source brings the best opportunities.”
- “Some inquiries sit too long before someone acts.”
- “Weak leads waste our time.”
- “We want better demand quality, not just more demand.”

### Recommend Level A when

- the business needs source and fit visibility first
- the owner wants to stop treating all demand as equal
- routing is still fairly simple but visibility is weak

### Recommend Level B when

- multiple lead sources already exist
- qualification quality matters
- the company needs clearer routing and response control
- Sales and Follow-Up continuity are already important

### Recommend Level C when

- the company is actively scaling inbound demand
- multiple channels are producing opportunities simultaneously
- the owner wants deeper prioritization and stronger autonomous routing logic
- demand quality drift can materially hurt revenue

---

## 9. Business-Friendly Explanation For Customers

Lead Generation Director helps your company control the front door of opportunity.

At Level A, it shows where new demand is coming from, which leads look strong or weak, and what still needs routing.

At Level B, it adds stronger qualification and routing workflow so the best opportunities move more reliably into sales and follow-up.

At Level C, it becomes a stronger demand-quality orchestration layer that helps the President and CRO see which sources are drifting, which opportunities matter most, and where action should happen next.

---

## 10. What Must Be Avoided

Do not describe this director as only:

- a list of leads
- a generic CRM inbox
- a marketing report
- a duplicate of Sales Director

That would undersell the real function.

The correct launch meaning is:

**Lead Generation Director is the demand-entry control lane that makes incoming opportunity visible, qualified, routed, and accountable before it enters the revenue system.**

Also avoid:

- collapsing Lead Generation into Marketing
- collapsing Lead Generation into Sales
- treating source visibility as enough without fit and routing truth

This director exists because inbound demand quality needs its own operating control lane.
