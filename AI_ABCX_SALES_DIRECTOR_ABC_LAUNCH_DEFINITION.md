# AI-ABCX Sales Director A/B/C Launch Definition

## 1. Core Rule

Sales Director is the CRO-controlled conversion lane inside AI-ABCX.

Its job is to:

- make assigned sales ownership visible
- preserve consultation and quote movement clearly
- keep estimate and consult outcomes structured
- show which opportunities are high-value, aging, pending, won, or lost
- surface what action should happen next
- connect conversion pressure to follow-up and revenue truth
- report sales pressure directly to the President

This director should not feel like a generic CRM contact list.

It should feel like a structured revenue-conversion operating lane under President control.

---

## 2. Public Meaning Of Each Level

### Level A

**Visible conversion truth**

The business can clearly see who owns each opportunity, which consults are scheduled or pending, and where obvious sales pressure exists.

### Level B

**Connected conversion workflow**

The business does not just see open opportunities. It can judge quote continuity more clearly, see stronger follow-up coordination, and understand where value is leaking across the conversion path.

### Level C

**Advanced conversion orchestration**

The system actively guides which opportunities should move first, which aging items need escalation, and how conversion pressure should be managed across consults, estimates, follow-up, and revenue outcomes.

---

## 3. Level A Definition

Level A means the company has visible conversion oversight and basic sales ownership truth.

At Level A, Sales Director should:

- show assigned sales work clearly
- show personal calendar visibility clearly
- preserve consultation confirmation flow
- identify basic result-entry states
- show basic next-step ownership
- show the President where open conversion pressure exists
- keep opportunity and consult status understandable without a detached CRM layer

Level A is the minimum launch standard for this director.

It creates visibility first.

Without this visibility, the business can let open quotes age, lose consult ownership, or miss obvious next actions without understanding where revenue is stalling.

---

## 4. Level B Definition

Level B means the company has stronger conversion workflow control instead of only visible opportunity activity.

At Level B, Sales Director should add:

- stronger routing and assignment handling
- better quote and consult continuity
- better follow-up coordination
- stronger opportunity prioritization
- stronger visibility into aging and value leakage
- clearer visibility into where open opportunities are slowing or weakening

Level B should make conversion manageable and accountable, not just visible.

---

## 5. Level C Definition

Level C means the company has advanced conversion orchestration across priorities, consult pressure, aging quotes, next-step logic, and downstream revenue outcomes.

At Level C, Sales Director should add:

- deeper opportunity prioritization
- stronger autonomous next-action guidance
- richer close-pressure visibility
- tighter multi-director coordination
- more advanced President and CRO control visibility
- stronger protection against losing high-value opportunities silently

Level C should feel like a real management layer for revenue-conversion discipline.

---

## 6. Exact Launch Difference Between A, B, And C

### A vs B

Level A shows conversion truth.

Level B adds stronger assignment control, quote continuity, follow-up coordination, and aging-value visibility.

### B vs C

Level B manages open opportunities more clearly.

Level C adds deeper prioritization, stronger next-action guidance, close-pressure visibility, and richer coordination with downstream revenue outcomes.

### Plain-English Lock

- A = we can see conversion pressure clearly
- B = we can manage conversion movement and leakage more reliably
- C = the system helps manage conversion strategically and protect revenue opportunity quality

---

## 7. Dependency Guidance

Sales Director can be sold independently, but it becomes much stronger when connected to the rest of the revenue system.

### Can function at Level A with light dependency

Yes, if the business mainly needs:

- opportunity ownership visibility
- consult visibility
- result-entry visibility
- aging-opportunity visibility
- President-level clarity on conversion pressure

### Becomes much stronger with these connected directors

- **Estimator Director**
  Provides quote and estimate continuity into the conversion lane.
- **Follow-Up Director**
  Preserves unresolved consult and quote outcomes after the sales interaction.
- **Lead Generation Director**
  Reveals where the opportunity came from and how source quality affects conversion.
- **Scheduling Director**
  Supports appointment-based consult timing truth.
- **Call Handling Director**
  Supports inbound opportunities that become routed sales cases.
- **Revenue Control Director**
  Shows whether won business turns into visible revenue truth.

### Recommended dependency logic

- Level A can be sold with light dependencies
- Level B is much stronger with Estimator and Follow-Up
- Level C should usually be recommended with multiple connected revenue lanes, especially Estimator, Follow-Up, Lead Generation, Scheduling, and Revenue Control

---

## 8. Recommendation Logic Guidance

Recommend Sales Director when the company says things like:

- “We provide estimates or consultations before the customer buys.”
- “We lose track of open quotes.”
- “We need clearer ownership of each live opportunity.”
- “We want better control after the estimate is given.”
- “High-value opportunities sometimes age or disappear.”
- “We want the owner to see where revenue is stuck.”

### Recommend Level A when

- the business needs conversion visibility first
- one owner or one salesperson handles most opportunities
- the owner mainly wants structure before scaling

### Recommend Level B when

- multiple salespeople exist
- estimate-heavy conversion matters
- follow-up discipline is inconsistent
- the company wants better control before opportunities leak

### Recommend Level C when

- the business is scaling consult and quote volume aggressively
- multiple live opportunities compete for attention at the same time
- weak next-step discipline can materially hurt growth
- the owner wants stronger strategic control over conversion instead of loose sales tracking

---

## 9. Business-Friendly Explanation For Customers

Sales Director helps your company control what happens between opportunity and decision.

At Level A, it shows who owns each live opportunity, which consults are scheduled or pending, and where obvious sales pressure exists.

At Level B, it adds stronger conversion workflow control by showing where quotes are aging, where follow-up is weak, and where value is leaking before the sale is won.

At Level C, it becomes a stronger conversion orchestration layer that helps the President and CRO see what should move first, which opportunities are becoming risky, and where revenue pressure is building before it turns into lost business.

---

## 10. What Must Be Avoided

Do not describe this director as only:

- a CRM
- a contact board
- a generic pipeline
- a duplicate of Estimator Director
- a duplicate of Follow-Up Director

That would undersell the real function.

The correct launch meaning is:

**Sales Director is the conversion-control lane that makes opportunity ownership visible, consult and estimate movement structured, and revenue pressure business-governed inside the AI-ABCX operating system.**

Also avoid:

- collapsing Sales into Lead Generation
- collapsing Sales into Follow-Up
- celebrating open opportunity volume without ownership, next-step discipline, or outcome quality

This director exists because conversion needs its own revenue-discipline lane.

## 11. Pricing Use

This A/B/C definition can be used directly in pricing architecture:

- **A** = conversion-visibility baseline
- **B** = connected conversion workflow
- **C** = advanced conversion orchestration

This keeps the upsell path understandable.

The customer is not paying for a vague sales dashboard.

The customer is paying for a stronger level of opportunity ownership, consult discipline, quote continuity, and conversion management.
