## 1. Core Rule

AI-ABCX no longer uses release stages as the launch structure.

- all launch-scope directors must eventually be available at launch
- A, B, and C are director capability levels
- X is deferred and is not part of current implementation scope

The build priority below does not change launch scope.
It only defines the order in which directors should be brought to high quality, tested, and connected.

---

## 2. Priority Logic

Directors should be built in this order:

1. directors that directly capture demand
2. directors that turn demand into booked work
3. directors that support conversion and follow-through
4. directors that support traffic, growth, and visibility
5. directors that support business control, reporting, and retention

This order is meant to create the fastest real commercial system:

- attract the lead
- capture the lead
- quote the lead
- book the work
- complete the work
- follow up
- report the result

---

## 3. Priority Waves

## Wave 1: Core Conversion Directors

These are the first directors to build to high level because they define the first commercial value of the system.

### 1. Estimator Director

Reason:

- one of the strongest commercial differentiators in the market
- directly tied to conversion speed and revenue
- must compete with advanced estimator products already in market

### 2. Call Handling Director

Reason:

- captures demand at the front door
- must qualify, route, escalate, and protect missed opportunities
- supports both standalone sale and connected system sale

### 3. Website Director

Reason:

- controls infrastructure for marketing, forms, tracking, and module connection
- can be sold as a standalone entry path into AI-ABCX
- supports future system expansion

### 4. Scheduling Director

Reason:

- turns qualified demand into actual appointments
- essential bridge between quote path and operations path
- required for many connected workflows

---

## Wave 2: Sales and Field Conversion Support

These directors should follow immediately after Wave 1 because they reinforce the quote-to-close path.

### 5. Follow-Up Director

Reason:

- prevents quote leakage
- keeps unresolved demand visible
- increases close rate without needing new acquisition

### 6. Sales Director

Reason:

- converts estimate activity into structured sales control
- tracks open opportunities, close pressure, and priorities

### 7. Dispatch Director

Reason:

- required where booked work must move into active field routing
- operationally strong when connected to scheduling and service

### 8. Lead Generation Director

Reason:

- creates source visibility
- supports acquisition intelligence and marketing tracking

---

## Wave 3: Growth Traffic Directors

These directors should be built after the core conversion path is strong.

### 9. Marketing Director

Reason:

- supports ongoing demand development
- needed for lifecycle communication and growth logic

### 10. Advertising Director

Reason:

- drives paid acquisition
- should connect directly to website, lead generation, and follow-up

### 11. SEO Director

Reason:

- supports organic lead flow
- strongest when website and marketing structures are already clear

### 12. Reactivation Director

Reason:

- creates value from dormant leads and past customers
- increases revenue without relying on only new traffic

---

## Wave 4: Delivery and Financial Truth Directors

These directors deepen execution discipline after the front-end growth engine is strong.

### 13. Service Director

Reason:

- tracks active service execution
- supports workflow truth after booking

### 14. Revenue Control Director

Reason:

- records paid truth and business reality
- necessary for President-level visibility

### 15. Reconciliation Director

Reason:

- prevents operational truth and financial truth from splitting
- supports trust in dashboard reporting

---

## Wave 5: Executive Control and Retention Directors

These directors complete the management layer once the revenue path is already strong.

### 16. Strategy Director

### 17. Executive Reporting Director

### 18. Accountability Director

### 19. Support Director

### 20. Customer Success Director

### 21. Retention Director

### 22. Reviews / Referrals Director

Reason:

- these directors matter strongly for long-term system maturity
- they become more valuable after core acquisition, conversion, and delivery logic is already solid

---

## 4. Immediate Build Track

The immediate implementation track is:

1. Estimator Director
2. Call Handling Director
3. Website Director
4. Scheduling Director

This is the smallest build track that creates the strongest commercial launch story:

- customers can be attracted
- customers can be captured
- quotes can be generated
- appointments can be created
- website infrastructure can support the system

---

## 5. Estimator Director First-Pass Goal

The first active build target is Estimator Director.

For this director, the priority is:

1. remove obsolete rollout-stage framing from the current dashboard
2. define the high-level estimator workflow clearly
3. compare against strongest outside estimator products
4. build toward professional quote generation, routing, approval, and outcome control
5. support multiple estimate formats and connected follow-up visibility
