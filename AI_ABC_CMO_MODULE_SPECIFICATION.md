# CMO Module Specification

## Purpose

The `CMO Module` is the growth and marketing intelligence layer of AI-ABC.

It is a future expansion module that should help the President understand where growth is coming from and where future growth should be directed.

---

## Mission

Turn campaigns, reactivation, and growth channels into measurable management activity.

---

## Primary Responsibilities

- monitor campaigns
- monitor customer reactivation programs
- compare channel performance
- identify growth opportunities
- support marketing recommendations
- connect top-of-funnel activity to business outcomes

---

## Inputs

- campaign metadata
- lead source records
- reactivation outreach results
- referral activity
- media partner activity
- conversion performance by source
- market and language performance

---

## Outputs

- campaign summaries
- source-performance summaries
- reactivation opportunity lists
- market growth recommendations
- growth risk warnings

---

## KPIs

- leads by source
- conversions by source
- reactivation response rate
- campaign-driven revenue
- market growth rate
- referral performance
- customer acquisition trend

---

## Reports

### Campaign Performance Report

- active campaigns
- top-performing channels
- underperforming channels
- conversion quality by source

### Reactivation Report

- dormant customers contacted
- responses received
- appointments generated
- revenue created from reactivation

### Growth Opportunity Report

- strong markets
- weak markets
- high-potential service categories
- growth recommendations

---

## Recommendations

- increase investment in a better-performing source
- reactivate a neglected customer segment
- test a stronger campaign in one service line
- reduce effort in low-conversion channels

---

## Dashboard Position

The CMO Module should appear in later-stage executive views once marketing data becomes structured enough to support decisions.

---

## Dependencies

The CMO Module depends on:

- source tracking
- campaign structure
- conversion visibility
- customer history

It is primarily a future-stage module.

---

## Future Roadmap

- automated growth recommendations
- market-by-market prioritization
- multilingual campaign intelligence
- partner media attribution
- stronger integration with publishing and campaign modules

---

## Core Principle

The CMO Module should make growth visible as a managed system, not as scattered marketing activity.
