# AI-ABCX Marketing Module Master Specification

This document defines the full-scope `Marketing Module` for AI-ABCX.

It is written for the possibility that Marketing becomes one of the primary flagship modules of the system from the beginning.

This module should not be treated as:

- a simple ad dashboard
- a vanity reporting screen
- a generic agency tool
- a disconnected growth add-on

It should be treated as:

`a President-level growth control system inside the AI-ABCX corporate management architecture`

---

## 1. Core Position

The AI-ABCX Marketing Module should help a service business understand:

- where demand comes from
- which demand sources are producing qualified opportunities
- which sources produce booked work and paid revenue
- which marketing efforts are wasting money
- which growth actions should happen next

The module must convert marketing into corporate management truth.

---

## 2. Mission

Turn demand generation, reactivation, conversion quality, and growth decisions into measurable executive control.

---

## 3. Main Product Rule

The AI-ABCX Marketing Module must always support the larger product story:

`AI-ABCX implements corporate structure into service businesses.`

That means this module is not a separate marketing app.

It is one part of the same President-led corporate system.

The future President should feel:

- marketing is no longer scattered
- growth is now visible and governable
- marketing spend is connected to business outcomes

---

## 4. What Makes This Module Valuable

The value is not just campaign visibility.

The value is:

- source-to-revenue attribution
- lead quality intelligence
- reactivation intelligence
- growth recommendation logic
- President-level decision support

The module should help the company answer:

`Where is growth coming from, what is weak, what is being wasted, and what should we do next?`

---

## 5. Required Functional Areas

The module should include these core areas.

### 5.1 Demand Source Control

Track and compare demand sources such as:

- Google Ads
- Local Services Ads
- SEO / organic search
- website forms
- inbound campaign calls
- referral sources
- media and partner channels
- social campaigns
- seasonal campaigns
- reactivation campaigns

This is the top-of-funnel truth layer.

### 5.2 Lead Quality Intelligence

Track not only volume, but lead quality:

- qualified leads
- unqualified leads
- booked leads
- sold leads
- repeat-customer leads
- spam or weak-fit leads

This section should reveal which sources bring real business and which create noise.

### 5.3 Campaign Control

Track:

- active campaigns
- campaign objective
- campaign owner
- target market or service line
- budget
- spend pacing
- status
- notes and risk flags

Campaigns should be visible as controlled business assets, not isolated marketing experiments.

### 5.4 Website and Conversion Control

Track conversion performance across:

- landing pages
- form submissions
- call clicks
- booking starts
- appointment conversions
- weak conversion pages
- strongest-performing offers

The website should be treated as a growth asset that belongs in corporate oversight.

### 5.5 Reactivation Engine

This is especially important for field service businesses.

Track:

- dormant customer lists
- unsold estimate follow-up campaigns
- repeat-service reminders
- maintenance reminders
- review/referral request campaigns
- seasonal reactivation sequences

This should connect marketing to historical customer value, not only new lead acquisition.

### 5.6 Revenue Attribution

The module must connect marketing to business truth:

- source -> lead
- lead -> booked appointment
- booked appointment -> sold outcome
- sold outcome -> amount paid

Without this layer, the module is not strong enough.

### 5.7 Growth Recommendation Engine

The module should produce clear executive recommendations such as:

- increase investment in a high-converting source
- cut or pause a weak source
- reactivate dormant demand before buying more traffic
- fix landing-page conversion before increasing spend
- move spend toward higher-quality service categories
- repair low-quality lead channels

This must feel like management guidance, not analyst noise.

---

## 6. Data Inputs

The Marketing Module should accept and unify:

- campaign metadata
- source tags
- landing-page or website conversion events
- call records linked to campaigns
- form leads
- CRM lead records
- booking outcomes
- closeout outcomes
- amount paid truth
- reactivation activity
- referral and partner data

If Call Handling is active, marketing should connect to call outcomes.

If Call Handling is not active, marketing must still connect through CRM, manual record creation, or imported lead records.

---

## 7. Main Outputs

The module should produce:

- source-performance summaries
- lead-quality summaries
- booked-work summaries
- sold-revenue summaries
- campaign risk warnings
- reactivation opportunity lists
- marketing efficiency recommendations
- growth-priority recommendations for the President

---

## 8. President-Level KPIs

The President should be able to see:

- leads by source
- qualified leads by source
- booked appointments by source
- sold jobs by source
- amount paid by source
- cost per qualified lead
- cost per booked lead
- cost per sold job
- reactivation response rate
- repeat-customer conversion
- strongest-performing service categories
- weakest-performing channels

These KPIs must connect growth to business outcome, not just activity.

## 10. Executive Structure

If this becomes a full real module, it should map to a real executive structure.

Suggested structure:

- `CMO`
- Advertising Director
- SEO Director
- Website Director
- Reactivation Director
- Media / Partnerships Director (later if needed)

This keeps the module aligned with the corporate management story.

---

## 11. Relationship To Other Modules

The Marketing Module becomes much stronger when connected.

### Marketing + Call Handling

- campaign calls become measurable business truth
- source quality improves through real call qualification

### Marketing + CRM

- source tracking connects to lead and relationship history
- repeat and referral opportunities become clearer

### Marketing + Dispatcher

- booked demand can be compared against scheduling and fulfillment capacity

### Marketing + Closeout

- booked work is tied to final outcome and amount paid truth

### Marketing + President Follow-Up

- unsold leads and reactivation opportunities stay alive

This module should work alone, but it becomes far more powerful when connected to the rest of AI-ABCX.

---

## 12. Why This Could Be A Flagship Module

This module may become one of the most demanded modules because:

- many companies already have some phone solution
- many more companies feel strong pain around wasted marketing spend
- growth problems are constant and expensive
- owners can quickly understand growth-control value
- marketing can become a major entry passage into the full AI-ABCX system

This module could become:

- a major wedge into the broader market
- a higher-value entry point than call handling for some companies
- one of the strongest arguments for the full ABCX system

---

## 13. State-Of-The-Art Standard

If the module is built, it must aim for a genuinely high standard.

That means:

- strong attribution logic
- strong lead-quality logic
- strong reactivation logic
- strong President recommendations
- clear actionability
- deep connection to revenue truth

It must not be:

- shallow charts
- disconnected analytics
- vanity metrics with no business action
- a prettier version of ordinary ad dashboards

If it is not strong enough to change decisions, it is not strong enough.

---

## 14. Launch Decision Rule

If AI-ABCX includes Marketing early, the launch should no longer be framed only as:

- field-service core operations

It becomes:

- field-service corporate management with growth intelligence

That is a bigger and more valuable launch position.

But it also means:

- more scope
- more product definition work
- more integration work
- a slower but potentially stronger first launch

---

## 15. Launch Recommendation

If this module is included early, it should be treated as:

- a flagship module
- part of the President system
- one of the strongest commercial entry points into AI-ABCX

It should not be treated as a weak optional extra.
