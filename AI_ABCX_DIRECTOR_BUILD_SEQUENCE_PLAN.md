# AI-ABCX Director Build Sequence Plan

This document turns the status matrix into a practical execution order.

Its purpose is not to describe every director in depth.

Its purpose is to answer one direct question:

`What do we build first, what comes next, and what must be true before we move on?`

---

## 1. Sequence Rule

The build order should follow this logic:

1. `Build the strongest entry offers first`
2. `Then finish the operational chain those offers depend on`
3. `Then finish revenue-growth and expansion layers`
4. `Then finish executive, financial, customer-success, and governance depth`

This keeps the system commercially useful early while still moving toward the full launch vision.

---

## 2. Wave Structure

### Wave 1

Revenue-entry directors that can attract and convert customers fastest.

### Wave 2

Core operating directors that make the system actually work in real business flow.

### Wave 3

Growth and expansion directors that increase value, outcomes, and upsell power.

### Wave 4

Executive-control, financial, improvement, and governance directors that complete the full corporate model.

---

## 3. Wave 1: Revenue Entry Layer

### Primary goal

Make AI-ABCX immediately sellable through strong customer-entry functions.

### Directors in Wave 1

1. `Estimator Director`
2. `Call Handling Director`
3. `Website Director`

### Why these come first

- They are easiest to explain in real business terms
- They can generate direct customer demand
- They create strong before/after value quickly
- They anchor many later bundles and upgrades

### Required outputs before Wave 1 is complete

- Estimator Director has a real product surface
- Call Handling Director has a real product surface
- Website Director has a real product surface
- Each has clear `A / B / C` definitions
- Each has launch pricing logic
- Each has at least one valid standalone offer
- Each has dependency rules written and enforced
- Each has at least one working internal test scenario

### Wave 1 milestone

`AI-ABCX can be sold through estimating, calls, and websites even before the full system is complete.`

---

## 4. Wave 2: Operations Continuity Layer

### Primary goal

Make the business flow work end to end after demand is created.

### Directors in Wave 2

1. `Follow-Up Director`
2. `Scheduling Director`
3. `Dispatch Director`
4. `Service Director`
5. `Sales Director`

### Why these come second

- They convert demand into actual controlled work
- They create operational discipline
- They make the President dashboard meaningful
- They support most realistic service-business workflows

### Required outputs before Wave 2 is complete

- Canonical naming normalized across all operational directors
- Follow-Up workflows tested
- Scheduling workflows tested
- Dispatch workflows tested
- Service visibility tested
- Sales workflow tested
- Dependency rules between Estimator, Call Handling, Scheduling, Dispatch, Service, and Sales are validated
- President dashboard reflects real workflow outcomes

### Wave 2 milestone

`AI-ABCX can run the front half and middle of a service business with visible operational control.`

---

## 5. Wave 3: Growth and Expansion Layer

### Primary goal

Increase customer value, business growth potential, and upgrade paths.

### Directors in Wave 3

1. `Lead Generation Director`
2. `Reactivation Director`
3. `Marketing Director`
4. `Advertising Director`
5. `SEO Director`

### Why these come third

- They improve business growth rather than only workflow control
- They support upsell opportunities after the customer understands the core system
- They connect strongly to Website, Estimator, and Call Handling

### Required outputs before Wave 3 is complete

- Each growth director has clear A/B/C logic
- Each growth director has valid standalone and bundle logic
- Website dependencies are finalized
- Source tracking is visible
- Recommendation logic can suggest growth upgrades
- At least one integrated marketing-to-lead-to-sales workflow is testable

### Wave 3 milestone

`AI-ABCX can be positioned not only as control software, but as a growth system.`

---

## 6. Wave 4: Executive / Financial / Governance Layer

### Primary goal

Complete the full corporate operating model and deepen long-term differentiation.

### Directors in Wave 4

#### CEO lane

1. `Strategy Director`
2. `Executive Reporting Director`
3. `Accountability Director`

#### CFO lane

4. `Revenue Control Director`
5. `Reconciliation Director`
6. `Accounting Director`
7. `Payroll Director`

#### CSO lane

8. `Support Director`
9. `Retention Director`
10. `Reviews / Referrals Director`

#### CPIO lane

11. `Director of Feedback`
12. `Director of Analysis`
13. `Director of Optimization`
14. `Director of System Improvement`

#### CAO lane

15. `Compliance Director`
16. `Approval Director`
17. `Risk Director`

### Why these come fourth

- They are strategically powerful but less necessary for first commercial entry
- They depend on real operational data already existing
- They become much stronger once customer-facing revenue and operations are already working

### Required outputs before Wave 4 is complete

- Executive reporting is formalized
- Accountability scoring is visible
- Financial truth workflows are connected
- Support / retention / referrals are operational
- Feedback and optimization loops are real
- Governance, approval, and risk logic are visible and testable

### Wave 4 milestone

`AI-ABCX becomes the full President-led corporate operating system rather than only a high-performance service business platform.`

---

## 7. Cross-Wave Non-Negotiables

These must move alongside every wave:

### 1. Step 1 diagnostic architecture

- Questions must stay human and easy
- Multiple answers must be allowed where needed
- Branching logic must stay clean
- Output must produce a business summary, recommendation, and projection

### 2. Pricing architecture

- Director pricing must remain simple in dollars
- No hidden-cost positioning must stay clear
- Bundle logic must stay understandable
- Full-system option must remain available when justified

### 3. Recommendation engine

- Must recommend:
  - intent-fit configuration
  - improved configuration
  - advanced configuration
- Must explain why the recommendation exists
- Must show pricing and projected outcome differences

### 4. Testing discipline

- No director is considered complete without scenario testing
- No bundle is considered complete without integrated testing
- No upgrade path is considered complete without A -> B -> C testing

---

## 8. Best Immediate Build Order

If we want the cleanest next practical sequence, it should be:

1. `Estimator Director`
2. `Call Handling Director`
3. `Website Director`
4. `Follow-Up Director normalization`
5. `Scheduling Director productization`
6. `Dispatch Director normalization`
7. `Sales Director completion`
8. `Service Director formalization`
9. `Lead Generation / Marketing / Advertising / SEO`
10. `Executive / Financial / Governance depth`

---

## 9. What This Means For Launch

If Waves 1 and 2 are complete, AI-ABCX already becomes commercially serious.

If Wave 3 is complete, AI-ABCX becomes much stronger as a growth engine.

If Wave 4 is complete, AI-ABCX becomes the full corporate operating model the product vision is aiming for.

So the launch path should not be:

`build everything at once`

It should be:

`build the strongest entry offers first, then complete the operating chain, then complete the corporate depth.`
