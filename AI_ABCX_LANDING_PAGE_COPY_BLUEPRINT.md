## 6. A, B, C, X Automation Levels

Section title:

`Automation Levels That Match The Business`

Intro copy:

`AI-ABCX is not one fixed system. It uses automation levels to match the real needs of each business. Levels A, B, and C are already functional. X represents the projected future state of full corporate automation and accountability.`

- `A | Foundational Automation`
  Creates structure, captures business information, supports core workflows, and gives the President visibility and control.
- `B | Coordinated Automation`
  Adds stronger workflow coordination, connected operating lanes, and more active support across the business.
- `C | Advanced Automation`
  Adds deeper execution, stronger cross-company coordination, and more autonomous operating power.
- `X | Projected Full Corporate Automation`
  Represents the future state where the system reaches its highest level of automated structure, coordination, and accountability, including at the President level.

Closing line:

`Most companies do not need to start at the highest level. They can begin with the level that fits their current business and expand as they grow.`

## 9. President Control, Accountability, And Performance

Section title:

## 10. Guided Activation And Custom Configuration

Section title:

`Guided Activation And Custom Configuration`

Copy:

`AI-ABCX guides the client through a structured activation process designed to prepare the right company setup without confusion or guesswork. During activation, the system diagnoses the client’s current business structure, workflow, tools, and operating needs, then automatically prepares a recommended company configuration.`

`The recommended setup is not shown as a technical list alone. It is presented with a clear description that explains why the configuration was chosen, which functions are included, how they work together, and what level of automation they represent.`

`The client can then review that recommendation and adjust the configuration within the combinations the system allows. Functions can be included or excluded, and each time the setup changes, AI-ABCX updates the chosen configuration description so the client can clearly understand what changed and how the new structure affects the business.`

`Each time a configuration is presented, the client also sees itemized pricing and the total cost of that setup. This makes it easier to compare options, understand the value of the configuration, and move toward a final structure with confidence.`

`The configuration can be reviewed and adjusted more than once until the client reaches a final setup that feels comfortable and right for the business. At any point during activation, the client can ask questions and the AI assistant will help guide the process.`

`At the end of each activation step, the system explains what happens next, so the client always understands the next part of the journey.`

## 11. Three-Step Activation

Section title:

`Three-Step Activation`

Intro copy:

`AI-ABCX uses a guided three-step process to help each client move from diagnosis to activation with clarity.`

- `Step 1 | Diagnose Your Business`
  The system evaluates your current setup, workflow, tools, and operating needs.
- `Step 2 | Review Your Configured System And Pricing`
  You see the recommended structure, understand why it was chosen, review itemized pricing, and adjust the setup if needed.
- `Step 3 | Test The Live Experience Before Activation`
  You review the dashboard, experience the live operating flow, and confirm the system before moving into activation.

Closing line:

`Each step prepares the next one, so the process stays structured, understandable, and transparent from beginning to end.`

## 14. Partner Access

Section title:

`Partner Access`

Intro copy:

`Partner Access is designed for clients who want special launch benefits while also helping AI-ABCX improve through ongoing real customer feedback. This gives us the ability to monitor performance in real business environments, understand the customer perspective, and continue adjusting the system with live operational insight.`

Benefits:
- `15% off` the total price of the chosen configuration
- the same pricing opportunity for eligible added functions and upgrades during the protected period
- pricing protection locked for `one year`
- after the first year, Partner Access pricing increases by only `10%` on a yearly basis under the policy
- a personal referral link
- a recurring referral benefit while the partner remains active
- monthly referral reports showing which companies activated through the partner referral and how much savings each active referral creates
- eligibility for future AI-ABCX promotions
- all terms governed by the `Partner Access Policy`

Referral copy:

`For every company that activates AI-ABCX through a partner’s personal referral link, the partner receives a monthly benefit equal to 10% of their own monthly payment while the partner remains active.`

`Each month, the partner also receives a referral report showing which companies came through their referral and how much they are saving from each active referral.`

Closing line:

`Partner Access is a limited-time relationship with defined benefits, defined expectations, and ongoing value for both the partner and the continued improvement of AI-ABCX.`

- keep the landing page simple and reassuring, not overly technical
- do not present the system as only phone answering or only one fixed package
- preserve the President-led corporate structure framing throughout
- keep Regular Access and Partner Access as commercial paths, not product definitions
- keep A, B, and C as current functional levels, and X as projected future full automation
- preserve space later for a separate long-form article such as `What Is AI-ABCX?` or `How AI-ABCX Works`
