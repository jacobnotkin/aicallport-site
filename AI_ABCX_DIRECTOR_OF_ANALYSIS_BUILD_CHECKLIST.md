## 1. Director of Analysis Mission

Director of Analysis is the cause-and-pattern interpretation lane of AI-ABCX.

Its job is to:

- translate raw feedback into cause-and-effect understanding
- identify which patterns are real and which are misleading
- distinguish symptoms from root causes
- connect operating signals to revenue, service outcomes, and workflow behavior
- surface the strongest explanations for performance movement
- preserve trustworthy inputs before optimization decisions are made
- reduce false conclusions and overreactions
- help the President understand what is actually changing inside the business

Director of Analysis should not feel like a generic reporting layer.

It should feel like the AI-ABCX interpretation lane that tells the President why a change is happening, what is actually causing it, and which explanation is strong enough to act on.

---

## 2. Director of Analysis Launch Standard

Director of Analysis is launch-ready only when it can do all of the following:

1. translate feedback and operating signals into clear patterns
2. distinguish symptoms from deeper causes
3. show pattern confidence and cause quality clearly
4. connect analysis to revenue, service, workflow, and customer outcomes
5. preserve current study inputs and data truth
6. connect cleanly with Feedback, Optimization, System Improvement, and President visibility
7. support action without pretending certainty where certainty does not exist
8. support A / B / C levels clearly

If one of these is missing, the director is not complete.

---

## 3. Canonical Role Definition

### Public-facing name

`Director of Analysis`

### Internal role meaning

The AI-ABCX director responsible for interpreting business signals, building cause maps, validating patterns, and converting raw signal flow into usable management understanding.

### Executive owner

`CPIO`

Director of Analysis belongs to the CPIO lane because analysis is the bridge between captured signals and system-improvement action.

## 4. Commercial Promise

Director of Analysis should promise:

- clearer understanding of what is actually causing performance changes
- less guessing about why leads, jobs, revenue, or continuity are shifting
- fewer bad decisions made from visible symptoms alone
- stronger ability to prioritize the right fixes
- more confidence before changing spend, staffing, marketing, or workflow logic
- better executive visibility into why the system recommends specific next actions

The commercial promise is not merely:

- "You can see reports."

It is:

- "You understand the cause behind the signal before you change the business around it."

## 7. A / B / C Definition Draft

### Level A

Level A Director of Analysis is visible cause-and-pattern interpretation with basic confidence control.

Includes:

- cause maps
- basic pattern visibility
- root-issue review
- revenue linkage visibility
- current study-input visibility

Best fit:

- founder-led businesses
- smaller businesses that still need help understanding why signals are moving
- early-stage AI-ABCX configurations where the system needs to explain itself clearly

### Level B

Level B Director of Analysis adds stronger cross-lane reasoning and decision support.

Includes everything in A, plus:

- stronger cross-lane checks
- better study updates
- deeper pattern prioritization
- stronger decision-support routing
- better linkage into Feedback, Support, Sales, and service outcomes

Best fit:

- growing teams
- businesses using several directors together
- companies that need stronger explanation before adjusting allocation, marketing, staffing, or workflow

### Level C

Level C Director of Analysis adds advanced interpretation depth and more predictive executive reasoning.

Includes everything in B, plus:

- stronger false-signal protection
- deeper root-cause confidence logic
- stronger predictive interpretation of pattern drift
- more advanced cross-system cause mapping
- deeper support for Optimization and System Improvement

Best fit:

- larger or more complex service businesses
- companies using broad multi-director configurations
- businesses wanting executive-grade AI explanation before major system changes

## 13. Required Handoffs

Director of Analysis must hand off cleanly to:

- `Director of Feedback`
  - when more raw signal intake is needed
- `Director of Optimization`
  - when a cause pattern is strong enough to recommend a change
- `Director of System Improvement`
  - when repeated patterns show structural logic should be revised
- `Strategy Director`
  - when the pattern affects larger timing, allocation, or growth decisions
- `Executive Reporting Director`
  - when the board narrative should reflect the analysis result
- `President`
  - when explanation quality is good enough to support a meaningful decision

Without these handoffs, Analysis becomes a dead-end explanation engine instead of a management-control lane.

---

## 14. Guided Activation Requirements

The activation flow should eventually help determine:

- whether the owner already struggles to understand why results shift
- whether the business has multiple lead sources or service lanes
- whether they want recommendations backed by visible explanation
- whether problems repeat without clear causes
- whether they need stronger internal reporting before changing the business

This matters because Director of Analysis should be recommended more strongly where the owner needs not just visibility, but interpretation.

## 15. Pricing Decisions Still Required

Pricing still needs final decisions for:

- whether A / B / C pricing should track feedback / optimization pricing closely

## 16. Build Sequence

Recommended build order:

1. normalize canonical role naming
2. define analysis record structure
3. define categories and states
4. connect Director of Feedback signals into analysis inputs
5. build President and CPIO visibility
6. define confidence, assumptions, and data-gap logic
7. connect handoffs into Optimization and System Improvement
8. define A / B / C behavior
9. test cause interpretation and decision-support clarity

This order keeps analysis grounded in real signals and real decisions.
