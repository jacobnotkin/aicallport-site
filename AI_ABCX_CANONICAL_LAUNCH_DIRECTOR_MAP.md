# AI-ABCX Canonical Launch Director Map

This document is the working source of truth for the `AI-ABCX` launch structure.

Its purpose is to unify:

- product definition
- executive structure
- director naming
- launch scope
- dependency logic
- future benchmark research
- future pricing architecture

This document exists because the current repository contains:

- older narrow-launch documents
- richer dashboard and preview structures
- partial implementation registries

From this point forward, planning should align to this canonical map unless it is deliberately revised.

---

## 1. Core Product Direction

`AI-ABCX` is not a bundle of disconnected software tools.

It is:

- an autonomous AI management system for service businesses
- organized as `AI Executives` and `AI Managing Directors`
- controlled by the business owner as `President`

This is the customer-facing logic:

`Transform your service business with an autonomous AI management system.`

`AI Executives and AI Managing Directors work for you 24/7, while you lead as President.`

The system should be built for:

- one-person companies
- small service teams
- growing multi-person operators
- larger service companies that want stronger automation and control

---

## 2. Canonical Launch Rule

The launch direction is no longer:

- limited to a small subset of modules
- mostly A-level only
- structurally narrow

The canonical launch direction is now:

- all major directors should be available at launch
- every director must support `A / B / C` logic
- every configuration must remain part of one connected corporate system
- `President Core` and shared system truth remain the permanent anchors

This does **not** mean every client gets every director.

It means:

- the full catalog exists at launch
- the diagnostic qualifies what the client actually needs
- the system recommends valid combinations
- the customer can approve a custom configuration

---

## 3. Permanent Anchor Rule

Every valid AI-ABCX configuration must include:

- `President Core`
- `CRM / Shared Operating Record`

These are not optional because they provide:

- unified company truth
- dashboard continuity
- workflow ownership
- executive reporting
- upgrade compatibility

Without these anchors, directors become isolated tools instead of part of one AI company structure.

---

## 4. Naming Rule

The product should use **business-facing names** externally and may keep more technical/internal names behind the scenes if needed.

Examples:

- `Communications Director` may be presented externally as `Call Handling Director`
- `Revenue Control Director` may stay internal if a clearer business-facing name is needed later
- `Reviews / Referrals Director` may become part of a more commercial retention/trust vocabulary

The external naming system should optimize for:

- owner understanding
- sales clarity
- module comparison
- pricing clarity

The internal naming system should optimize for:

- implementation consistency
- dashboard consistency
- data structure consistency

## 7. Directors That Must Be Explicitly Added To The Launch Model

The repository already strongly supports the corporate architecture, but the following directors need explicit normalization because they matter commercially and have already become part of the product direction:

### Estimator Director

Why it must be canonical:

- strongest benchmark gap identified so far
- major sales driver in service businesses
- directly tied to speed-to-quote
- strong standalone commercial entry path
- powerful upsell path into call handling, website, marketing, scheduling, and CRM

### Call Handling Director

Why it must be canonical:

- user has defined it as an advanced universal AI call layer
- multilingual
- qualification-aware
- urgency-aware
- appointment-capable
- one of the strongest business-facing entry modules

### Website Director

Why it must be canonical:

- now part of the core sales strategy
- can be sold as the first entry into the system
- can be operated in two modes:
  - `AI-ABCX hosted / AI-ABCX owned website path`
  - `client-owned website connector path`
- required for many higher-level marketing and automation configurations

### Follow-Up Director

Why it must be canonical:

- critical for real conversion improvement
- key bridge between sales, service, retention, and President visibility
- should be treated as more than a vague “recommended actions” lane

### Dispatch Director

Why it must be canonical:

- needed where field assignment and route movement matter
- should not be hidden inside a generic scheduling story
- especially important for service teams with multiple technicians or crews

## 8. Launch Availability Principle

All directors should be available at launch in `A / B / C` form.

But availability does not mean every client gets every director.

The launch logic should be:

- every director exists
- every director has a level path
- diagnostics determine the right configuration
- dependency rules protect invalid combinations

---

## 9. A / B / C Meaning

The system should use one consistent cross-product rule:

### Level A

- foundational
- simpler automation
- lighter operating depth
- narrower workflow control
- suitable for smaller or less automation-ready businesses

### Level B

- stronger automation
- more structured workflow management
- broader integration and operational visibility
- suitable for companies ready for more coordinated control

### Level C

- advanced automation
- deeper coordination across directors
- stronger operational intelligence
- broader management depth
- suitable for companies that want scale, discipline, and stronger system-wide control

This level logic must be customized per director, not described only in abstract terms.

---

## 10. Standalone vs Dependent Logic

Not all directors are commercially equal.

We should separate them into two categories:

### Commercially Independent Directors

These can be sold as the main reason a company enters AI-ABCX:

- Estimator Director
- Call Handling Director
- Website Director
- Marketing / Advertising entry paths
- Scheduling Director in some business types
- Dispatch Director in some business types

### Dependency-Sensitive Directors

These are stronger when sold as part of a connected operating package:

- Follow-Up Director
- Revenue Control Director
- Reconciliation Director
- Reviews / Referrals Director
- System Improvement / Optimization lanes

Important:

Commercial independence does **not** mean technically free-standing.

It means:

- the customer can enter through that director
- but the system still includes President Core and CRM
