# AI-ABCX Launch Price List Draft

This document is the first concrete draft of the `AI-ABCX` launch price list.

It follows:

- the normalized director model
- the director dependency rules
- the launch pricing architecture draft

This is still a planning document.

It is not yet the final public pricing sheet.

---

## 1. Core Rule

Customers pay for:

- activated directors
- the level of each director (`A / B / C`)
- usage where real variable cost exists
- website infrastructure path where applicable
- extra user seats where applicable

Customers do not pay separately for:

- executive titles
- President Core as a standalone fee

The first activated paid director starts the system.

---

## 2. Customer-Facing Pricing Promise

Public pricing should be explained like this:

- clear monthly pricing
- no hidden costs
- no AI credit confusion
- no forced payment for functions they do not use
- ability to start with one director and expand later

Recommended public language:

`You only pay for the directors your business actually activates.`

`If you expand your system, your price expands with it.`

## 7. Director Combination Pricing Rule

The system should allow one director at a time, but it should reward broader activation.

Suggested discount rule:

- `1–2 directors`: no bundle discount
- `3–4 directors`: `5%` off total monthly director price
- `5–7 directors`: `10%` off total monthly director price
- `8+ directors`: `15%` off total monthly director price

This discount should apply only to:

- monthly director subscription charges

It should not automatically apply to:

- per-minute call usage
- custom ad spend
- third-party pass-through costs

---

## 8. Full-System Recommendation Rule

The modular model remains primary.

But the system should still be able to recommend:

1. `Intent Fit Configuration`
2. `Improved Configuration`
3. `Advanced Configuration`

That means some companies may receive a recommendation that includes many directors at once, including a near-full `C-level` system.

This is valid when:

- their size supports it
- their goals support it
- their intent supports it
- their operations justify it

Full-system pricing is therefore:

- not a separate universal package
- but a calculated multi-director recommendation

---

## 9. User Seat Pricing

User quantity should affect pricing, but lightly.

Suggested launch rule:

- first small operating allowance included
- extra seats added only when meaningful

Working draft:

- `1 President / owner`: included
- `up to 2 additional users`: included in most mid-level configurations
- `each additional user after included allowance`: `$10 / month`

Future refinement can separate:

- office staff seats
- manager seats
- field seats
- read-only seats

But launch pricing should stay simple.

---

## 10. Website Director Special Conditions

Website Director pricing should follow these rules:

### If customer keeps existing website

- they still activate `Website Director`
- they pay connector pricing
- higher levels depend on infrastructure readiness

### If customer needs AI-ABCX website

- they activate hosted Website Director pricing
- AI-ABCX controls hosting and structure
- cancellation disconnects the system-owned website path

### Upgrade rule

- customer can move from `A` to `B` or `C` later
- if current website does not support the higher level, AI-ABCX website path becomes required

---

## 11. Call Handling Director Special Conditions

Call Handling pricing should be explained clearly:

- monthly fee = director capability
- minute fee = actual live usage

Recommended launch message:

`You pay a fixed monthly amount for the AI Call Handling Director, plus only the minutes your business actually uses.`

This is commercially strong because:

- easy to understand
- honest
- predictable
- easier than credit-based systems
