# AI-ABCX 3-Step Corporate Structure Implementation Specification

This document defines how the `3-step activation` experience should work for AI-ABCX.

It should not be treated as ordinary signup, onboarding, or a demo funnel.

It should be treated as:

`corporate structure implementation for the future President's company`

---

## 1. Core Framing

The future customer should feel that AI-ABCX is not simply being purchased.

They should feel that their company is being:

- understood
- structured
- organized
- prepared for President-level control
- brought into a real operating model

The activation experience should feel like implementation of structure, not just access to software.

---

## 2. Master Experience Rule

Every step must feel like:

- executive implementation
- operating structure installation
- company-specific preparation
- increasing operational reality

It must not feel like:

- generic SaaS onboarding
- form submission
- abstract product walkthrough
- disconnected demo sequence

---

## 3. Emotional Arc

The 3-step experience should create this progression:

### Step 1

`They understand my business.`

### Step 2

`They prepared a real operating structure for my company.`

### Step 3

`I just saw that structure work in real life.`

---

## 4. Experience Model

The steps should be renamed in meaning as follows:

| Traditional framing | AI-ABCX framing |
|---|---|
| Intake | Structure Discovery |
| Review | Structure Alignment |
| Demo | Structure In Action |

This is the correct product meaning.

---

## 5. Step 1: Structure Discovery

### Purpose

Step 1 discovers how the business operates and what corporate structure needs to be implemented.

### What the future President should feel

- the system is intelligently learning their company
- the questions are relevant to how the business actually works
- the company is being prepared for a structured operating model

### What Step 1 must do

- capture company identity
- capture business type
- capture field service context
- capture call-handling needs
- capture after-hours needs
- capture urgency patterns
- capture lead qualification needs
- capture scheduling conditions
- capture staffing and workflow conditions

### What Step 1 is really discovering

- how work enters the business
- where the business loses control
- where scheduling breaks
- where follow-up breaks
- where accountability is weak
- what President-level structure is needed

### UX rule

Step 1 must feel like structured executive discovery, not like a generic form.

---

## 6. Step 2: Structure Alignment

### Purpose

Step 2 presents the prepared operating structure and aligns the future President with how AI-ABCX will govern the company.

### What the future President should feel

- this system was prepared for my company
- the structure matches how my business should operate
- I understand what becomes active now and what expands later
- I am stepping into the President role

### What Step 2 must do

- present the prepared company profile
- present the field-service operating fit
- explain the active Stage A structure
- explain how Stage B and Stage C expand the structure
- explain the core systems clearly
- confirm pricing and path
- complete policy acceptance without breaking trust

### What Step 2 should reinforce

- `You lead as President.`
- `AI-ABCX brings corporate structure to service businesses.`
- the company is not getting a tool only
- the company is getting a structured operating model

### UX rule

Step 2 must feel like company structure presentation and alignment, not like administrative paperwork.

---

## 7. Step 3: Structure In Action

### Purpose

Step 3 proves that the prepared company structure works through a real live operating event.

### What the future President should feel

- this is my company
- this is my AI agent
- this is my dashboard
- this is real operating structure in action

### What Step 3 must do

- run a real live proof call
- use the company's own prepared AI agent
- write the call into the company's own prepared dashboard
- show the resulting record, classification, and next-step truth
- create a clear activation moment after proof

### What Step 3 proves

- the system can answer real calls
- the system can classify and qualify correctly
- the system can create business truth
- the President can immediately see the operational result

### UX rule

Step 3 must feel like live operating proof, not a presentation layer.

---

## 8. Corporate Structure Message Across All Steps

Every step should reinforce that AI-ABCX implements structure across:

- call handling
- customer truth
- scheduling and routing
- job or opportunity truth
- closeout and follow-up
- President oversight

The user should understand that the company is moving from informal operation into corporate discipline.

---

## 9. Language Rule

Preferred language:

- implementation
- operating structure
- President system
- prepared company system
- structure alignment
- live operating proof
- company-specific setup
- executive control

Language to reduce or avoid:

- signup
- onboarding
- demo
- preview
- simple setup
- generic app language

The activation experience should sound like executive implementation, not consumer software purchase.

---

## 10. Design Rule

The 3-step flow should feel:

- serious
- premium
- company-specific
- structured
- increasingly real from step to step

Each step should raise confidence, clarity, and perceived value.

The user should feel they are moving deeper into their company’s future operating structure.

---

## 11. Launch Scope Rule

For launch, this experience should be built specifically for:

- small and medium field service businesses

Do not dilute the experience by trying to make the first version generic for all SMBs.

The implementation should be sharp for the initial field-service market.

---

## 12. Functional Mapping

### Step 1 output

- company structure discovery profile
- prepared configuration inputs
- company-specific implementation path

### Step 2 output

- structure-aligned prepared company system
- approved path and pricing state
- ready-for-live-proof state

### Step 3 output

- live proof call result
- operating record created
- prepared President dashboard opened on the proof record
- activation-ready decision state

---

## 13. Main Product Rule

The 3-step AI-ABCX activation flow should be understood as:

`a guided corporate structure implementation experience for the future President's company`

This is the governing definition for redesigning the activation experience.

---

## 14. Immediate Build Consequences

This means:

1. Step 1 should be redesigned around structure discovery, not generic intake.
2. Step 2 should be redesigned around structure alignment, not policy review alone.
3. Step 3 should be redesigned around live proof, not dashboard preview alone.
4. All UI copy should support President-level structure language.
5. The transition between steps should feel like one implementation journey.
