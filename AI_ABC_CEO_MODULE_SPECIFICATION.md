# Bring Corporate Structure to Service Businesses

# CEO Module Specification

## Purpose

The `CEO Module` is the executive coordination and company-health layer of AI-ABC.

Its job is not to do operational work directly.

Its job is to reduce the full business into:

- priorities
- risks
- opportunities
- goal alignment
- owner-facing recommendations

The CEO Module is the clearest expression of the idea that AI-ABC is a company operating system and not only a collection of tools.

---

## Mission

Turn business activity into executive clarity for the President.

---

## Primary Responsibilities

- create daily executive briefings
- create weekly executive summaries
- monitor company health across active modules
- identify top priorities
- surface cross-functional problems
- align the business around approved goals
- reduce noise into a short owner action list

---

## Inputs

The CEO Module consumes signals from all active modules and stages.

### Stage A inputs

- call volume
- missed-call recovery results
- lead counts
- opportunity counts
- urgency events
- follow-up visibility

### Stage B inputs

- appointment completion rates
- staff responsiveness
- scheduling bottlenecks
- open estimates
- revenue pending
- revenue lost
- daily reconciliation state

### Stage C inputs

- approvals pending
- escalations
- exception queues
- workflow drift
- scorecards
- governance events

### Cross-stage inputs

- approved goals
- current goal progress
- customer risk signals
- owner decisions
- unresolved high-priority actions

---

## Outputs

- daily executive briefing
- weekly executive briefing
- monthly company-health summary
- priority queue for the President
- goal-alignment summary
- risk summary
- opportunity summary
- recommended next actions

---

## KPIs

- business health score
- opportunity response speed
- follow-up coverage
- appointment completion rate
- unresolved critical actions
- goal progress percentage
- revenue trend vs approved goal
- operational drift count
- escalation volume

---

## Reports

### Daily Briefing

Should show:

- what changed yesterday
- what needs attention today
- what is urgent now
- what may be lost if ignored

### Weekly Briefing

Should show:

- goals
- operational performance
- opportunity performance
- revenue movement
- main bottlenecks
- recommended executive actions

### Monthly Review

Should show:

- company health trend
- revenue trend
- goal achievement
- major risks
- major opportunities
- recommended structural adjustments

---

## Recommendations

The CEO Module should produce recommendations like:

- increase follow-up on specific opportunity categories
- review staff capacity in one market
- approve or modify next-month goals
- prioritize one revenue bottleneck
- investigate recurring missed opportunity patterns
- escalate unresolved workflow drift to management review

Recommendations should be short, ranked, and tied to business effect.

---

## Dashboard Position

In the President dashboard, the CEO Module should appear first.

It is the top-level executive entry point because it summarizes:

- company health
- cross-module priorities
- executive recommendations

---

## Dependencies

The CEO Module depends on:

- CRO inputs in Stage A
- COO, CRO, and CFO inputs in Stage B
- CAO and governance inputs in Stage C

It becomes stronger as more modules are active.

---

## Future Roadmap

- multi-period goal forecasting
- business health scoring engine
- strategic recommendation ranking
- automated cross-module anomaly detection
- management simulations based on likely outcomes
- Stage X executive autonomy support

---

## Core Principle

The CEO Module exists to help the President understand the company quickly and act on the most important issues first.
