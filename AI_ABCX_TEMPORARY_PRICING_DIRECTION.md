## 1. Temporary Pricing Buckets

These are working internal buckets only.

### `Included Core`

The function is so central to system control or system visibility that it may be included with the operating shell rather than sold as a major standalone module.

### `Low`

Good candidate for a lower-friction add-on.

Typical use:

- simple entry director
- connector-style function
- trust or retention helper

### `Medium`

A meaningful commercial module that creates direct business value and should likely carry a clear monthly price.

### `Premium`

A high-value director with major revenue, automation, or operational leverage.

These are likely to become some of the strongest-priced launch modules.

### `Usage-Based`

A director where monthly access alone is not the whole pricing story.

Typical use:

- voice / minute-driven systems
- transaction-heavy systems
- potentially ad-spend-connected systems later

Most likely structure:

- base director fee
- plus usage

## 3. Strongest Pricing Candidates Right Now

If we only look at temporary pricing direction, the strongest likely top-value directors are:

- Estimator Director
- Call Handling Director
- Marketing Director
- Advertising Director
- Website Director at hosted higher levels

These are the most likely to become the strongest early revenue drivers once they are fully built and tested.

---

## 4. Likely Core-Included Candidates

These are the strongest candidates to be included in the core President-led system rather than sold as visibly separate expensive modules:

- Executive Reporting Director
- System Improvement Director
- Strategy Director at basic level
- Accountability Director at basic level
- Revenue Control Director at basic level

This is useful because it helps the system feel like one real corporate structure instead of a pile of disconnected fees.

## 5. Directors Most Likely to Need Split Pricing

These are the strongest candidates for more than one pricing path:

### Call Handling Director

Likely structure:

- base director fee
- plus per-minute or per-usage pricing

### Website Director

Likely structure:

- lower connector pricing for existing website path
- higher hosted pricing for AI-ABCX-created website path

### Marketing Director

Potential later split:

- base director fee
- higher level for heavier automation and orchestration

### Advertising Director

Potential later split:

- director fee
- ad-spend handled outside fee

## 7. Practical Rule for Now

- do not publish exact prices
- do not lock final bundle discounts
- do not finalize partner discount math

- keep these bucket labels internally
- use them to shape implementation and sales thinking

## 8. Most Important Conclusion

The right way to think about pricing **right now** is:

`we are not pricing finished products yet, we are classifying likely commercial weight.`

That is much healthier than pretending the final prices are already known.

The next strongest move after this document is:

- assign each launch director a temporary commercial priority
- then start building from the strongest premium and usage-based candidates first
