# Revenue Intelligence Specification

## Purpose

Revenue Intelligence is the financial truth layer of AI-ABC.

It should become the long-term foundation of the `CFO Module`.

Its purpose is to track how revenue moves from:

- opportunity
- estimate
- completed work
- actual financial outcome

without losing accountability along the way.

---

## Core Financial Categories

### Estimates

Estimates represent proposed revenue.

They should track:

- estimate amount
- issue or service type
- responsible staff member
- date created
- current state
- client link

### Actual Payments

Actual payments represent confirmed money received.

They should track:

- amount paid
- date paid
- payment type
- partial or full
- related estimate or job

### Revenue Received

Revenue received is confirmed collected money.

This should be the clearest financial truth number in the system.

### Revenue Pending

Revenue pending includes:

- open estimates
- partial payments still outstanding
- completed work awaiting confirmed payment outcome

### Revenue At Risk

Revenue at risk includes:

- aging estimates
- overdue payment outcomes
- high-value open opportunities with weak follow-up
- jobs completed but not financially reconciled

### Revenue Lost

Revenue lost includes:

- closed-lost opportunities
- estimates that failed to convert
- jobs not won
- payments unlikely to be recovered

---

## Goal Calculations

Revenue Intelligence should support goal calculations for:

- monthly revenue goal
- collected revenue vs goal
- pending revenue vs goal
- weighted potential vs goal
- lost revenue vs goal

Goals are recommended by the system but approved by the President.

---

## Forecasting

Forecasting should begin simply and grow over time.

### Early forecasting inputs

- open estimates
- average close rate
- average time-to-close
- pending revenue
- recurring payment patterns

### Later forecasting inputs

- service category trends
- source-quality trends
- staff performance trends
- seasonal patterns
- market differences

---

## Required Data Objects

- estimate
- payment record
- job completion record
- reconciliation record
- revenue goal
- forecast record

---

## Core Reports

### Daily Reconciliation Report

- completed jobs missing payment outcome
- new payments received
- partial payments
- revenue at risk added today

### Weekly Revenue Report

- revenue received
- revenue pending
- revenue at risk
- revenue lost
- largest open estimates
- goal progress

### Monthly Revenue Review

- trend line
- source of growth
- source of loss
- collection discipline
- forecast vs actual

---

## Recommendations

- reconcile completed jobs with missing outcome
- follow up on high-value open estimates
- review partial-payment patterns
- increase focus on high-conversion categories
- adjust goal projections if trend is weakening

---

## Connection To CFO Module

The CFO Module should be the executive surface for this specification.

Revenue Intelligence is the deeper financial model underneath that surface.

---

## Core Principle

Revenue Intelligence exists so AI-ABC can tell the President not only what work happened, but what revenue actually happened.
