# Bring Corporate Structure to Service Businesses

# CRO Module Specification

## Purpose

The `CRO Module` is the revenue-opportunity layer of AI-ABC.

It tracks how demand enters the business, how it is qualified, how it moves, and where revenue opportunity may be created or lost.

---

## Mission

Protect and grow revenue opportunities from first contact to next action.

---

## Primary Responsibilities

- track leads
- track opportunities
- monitor qualification
- monitor follow-up state
- monitor estimate status
- monitor conversion progress
- identify lost opportunities
- identify revenue-risk patterns

---

## Inputs

- inbound calls
- outbound calls
- SMS campaigns
- customer database records
- AI intent classifications
- urgency flags
- qualification signals
- follow-up status
- estimate status
- appointment outcomes
- customer-response history

---

## Outputs

- lead pipeline visibility
- opportunity pipeline visibility
- follow-up queue visibility
- lost-opportunity reporting
- conversion reporting
- revenue-opportunity recommendations

---

## KPIs

- total leads
- qualified leads
- response speed
- follow-up completion rate
- estimate-to-close rate
- lost opportunity count
- opportunity aging
- conversion rate
- revenue opportunity volume

---

## Reports

### Daily Revenue Opportunity Report

- new leads
- qualified opportunities
- delayed follow-ups
- estimate requests
- opportunities at risk

### Weekly Pipeline Report

- conversion flow
- lead source performance
- opportunity backlog
- lost opportunity reasons
- follow-up discipline

### Lost Opportunity Report

- missed calls not recovered
- delayed follow-up
- estimates not advanced
- no-response patterns

---

## Recommendations

- prioritize follow-up on specific high-value opportunities
- improve missed-call recovery in one category
- contact dormant opportunity records from imported CRM data
- review estimate aging before revenue is lost
- shift outreach toward better-performing lead sources

---

## Dashboard Position

In the President dashboard, the CRO Module should sit near the top because revenue opportunity is one of the first questions a President asks.

It should answer:

- what entered the business
- what is qualified
- what is moving
- what may be lost

---

## Dependencies

The CRO Module is active from Stage A and expands in Stage B and Stage C.

It depends on:

- AI call handling
- CRM record creation
- history tracking
- follow-up visibility
- estimate tracking
- execution outcomes

---

## Future Roadmap

- opportunity scoring
- source-quality scoring
- conversion forecasting
- revenue-opportunity prioritization
- lifecycle segmentation
- autonomous reactivation recommendations

---

## Core Principle

The CRO Module makes sure opportunities do not disappear between first contact and real revenue movement.
