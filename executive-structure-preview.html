Highlighted Lane

COO → Scheduling Director → Service Director

This lane shows how one Executive Officer can activate related Managing Directors when the business needs more execution control, timing visibility, and service coordination.

COO governs operational truth and service movement.
Scheduling Director controls timing, appointment flow, and route logic.
Service Director oversees field execution and completion quality.
Growth Logic

Not every company starts with every Director active. AI-ABCX can begin with the Executive Officers and only activate the Directors the company actually needs, then add more structure as complexity grows.
