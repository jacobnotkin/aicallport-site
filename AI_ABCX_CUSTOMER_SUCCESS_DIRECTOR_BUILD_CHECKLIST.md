## 1. Customer Success Director Mission

Customer Success Director is the post-sale continuity and customer-health lane of AI-ABCX.

Its job is to:

- keep customer continuity visible after the sale or appointment is already won
- protect retention, trust, review health, and complaint resolution
- surface early risk before customer dissatisfaction becomes churn
- make customer follow-through visible to the President
- coordinate support, retention, complaints, reviews, and referral readiness
- preserve customer memory across the entire operating system
- turn customer experience into operational truth instead of vague sentiment

It should not behave like a passive support inbox.

It should behave like a corporate customer-success lane under President control.

---

## 2. Customer Success Director Launch Standard

Customer Success Director is launch-ready only when it can do all of the following:

1. show customer-health status clearly
2. distinguish retention, review, complaint, support, and referral signals
3. keep open customer issues visibly connected to the main operating record
4. show what follow-through is still unresolved and who owns it
5. surface customer-risk and continuity-risk early
6. report customer-success pressure inside the President view
7. support A / B / C levels clearly
8. support credible guided-activation recommendations and connected bundles

If one of those is missing, the director is not complete.

## 7. A / B / C Definition Draft

This checklist assumes these working level definitions.

They can be refined later, but implementation should move around this structure.

### Level A

- basic customer-success visibility
- open complaints, reviews, and support issues are visible
- customer continuity signals are present
- President can see customer-risk pressure
- retention and trust are visible even with narrower depth

### Level B

- stronger retention logic
- more structured complaint and review handling
- clearer ownership and continuity rules
- better coordination with follow-up and service outcomes
- more organized customer-health tracking

### Level C

- fully structured post-sale orchestration
- stronger automated continuity logic
- richer risk prediction and exception handling
- deeper coordination with service, follow-up, marketing, and referral growth
- advanced executive visibility into customer-health movement

### Level X

- future fully autonomous customer-success governance
- predictive customer-risk modeling
- self-improving continuity rules
- deeper board-level trust and accountability logic

X should remain future-facing, not launch-blocking.

## 10. Required Shared Customer Record

Customer Success Director must not operate on a disconnected ticket layer.

It needs a shared customer record that includes:

- customer identity
- service history
- current open support items
- complaint status
- review status
- retention signals
- follow-up continuity
- referral / advocacy status
- next-step ownership
- President-visible summary state

If customer-success data is separate from the main operating truth, the director is not complete.

- unify continuity, service, support, and trust signals

## 13. Required Director Handoffs

Customer Success Director must coordinate with:

### Follow-Up Director

Because unresolved customer promises and continuity pressure overlap directly.

### Service / COO-side lanes

Because service quality and follow-through drive customer-health reality.

### Call Handling Director

Because customer frustration, support questions, and continuity signals often begin in calls.

### Marketing Director

Because reviews, referrals, and trust reputation affect growth.

### Sales Director

Because expectation-setting and handoff quality affect long-term success.

### Retention and Reviews sub-lanes

Because these are already visible in the dashboard and should remain structurally coherent.

If these handoffs are vague, the director will feel cosmetic instead of operational.

---

## 14. Guided Activation Requirements

Guided activation must be able to recommend Customer Success Director credibly.

Required qualifying questions:

- Do you have repeat customers?
- Do customer complaints sometimes get lost or delayed?
- Do you ask for reviews consistently?
- Do you know which customers are most at risk of not returning?
- Do unresolved service issues affect future sales or referrals?
- Do you rely on referrals and reputation for growth?

Guided activation should explain Customer Success Director as:

- the lane that protects trust after the job is already won
- the control layer for retention, reviews, complaints, and customer continuity

## 15. Pricing Decisions Required

Before launch, AI-ABCX must decide:

## 17. Internal Test Scenarios

Customer Success Director is not launch-ready until it passes realistic internal scenarios.

Required scenario tests:

### Scenario 1: Open complaint with no owner

System must:

- show complaint visibly
- assign or flag missing ownership
- escalate when aging passes threshold

### Scenario 2: Reviews aging without response

System must:

- show review-response pressure
- identify oldest unresolved reviews
- surface tone / escalation risk

### Scenario 3: Service issue creates retention risk

System must:

- connect service outcome to customer-health risk
- surface watch-account status
- preserve next-step visibility

### Scenario 4: Referral-ready customer identified

System must:

- identify satisfied customer state
- flag referral-ready opportunity
- preserve history and context

### Scenario 5: Support item resolved but continuity still unclear

System must:

- distinguish “issue answered” from “relationship stabilized”
- preserve continuity watch until next-step clarity is restored
