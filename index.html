System depth

Built to run call-based operations for small and medium businesses

AI Call Port is strongest when it can turn repeated inbound and outbound business activity into structured workflows, visibility, and follow-through.

Inbound call handling

  • Incoming calls answered automatically
  • Intent-based behavior across service, sales, and support
  • Urgency, after-hours, and operational routing logic

Outbound follow-up

  • Follow up on website, social, and missed-call leads
  • Use text only, call only, or text-then-call workflows
  • Upload existing client or lead databases and sort contacts into campaigns

Dashboard and CRM

  • Call summaries, transcripts, and recordings
  • Built-in client database and CRM behavior
  • Owner dashboard with visibility, alerts, billing, and reporting

Lead-source connections

Connect website forms, social-media leads, missed calls, and uploaded databases so follow-up happens from the same system instead of scattered tools.

Post-activation setup

Staff names, availability, services, internal prices, and assignment rules are added in the dashboard after activation so the early flow stays fast and guided.

Owner view

  • Calls, appointments, confirmations, and urgent alerts in one place
  • Booked value, client activity, reporting, billing, and requests
  • Full operational oversight without relying on scattered tools

Staff and workflow access

  • Assigned users can see their own work and availability only
  • Acceptance, confirmation, and completion tracking stay visible
  • Owners keep system-level control while staff work inside clean limits
