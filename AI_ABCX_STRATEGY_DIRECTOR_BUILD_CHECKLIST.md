## 1. Strategy Director Mission

Strategy Director is the business-path and timing-discipline lane of AI-ABCX.

Its job is to:

- keep the company focused on the right operating path
- frame strategic direction in simple President language
- decide what should stay fixed versus what should expand next
- prevent premature expansion
- connect present operating truth to near-term growth decisions
- keep priorities narrow when the business is not ready for broader complexity
- show strategic risks early
- preserve strategic alignment across departments

Strategy Director should not feel like vague inspiration or generic planning advice.

It should feel like the part of the system that protects the business from chasing too many things too early.

---

## 2. Strategy Director Launch Standard

Strategy Director is launch-ready only when it can do all of the following:

1. define the current strategic focus clearly
2. show what should remain fixed this month
3. identify the next valid expansion gate
4. surface focus drift and premature breadth risk
5. tie strategy to actual signals instead of opinion
6. connect cleanly with Executive Reporting, Accountability, Operations, Revenue, and Finance truth
7. support daily execution without becoming abstract
8. support A / B / C levels clearly

If one of these is missing, the director is not complete.

---

## 3. Canonical Role Definition

### Public-facing name

`Strategy Director`

### Internal role meaning

The AI-ABCX director responsible for framing the company’s operating path, keeping strategic focus narrow when needed, identifying valid next-stage expansion, and making sure timing decisions are based on real company truth.

### Executive owner

`CEO`

Strategy Director belongs to the CEO lane because it governs business direction, growth timing, and cross-department focus at the highest operating level under the President.

## 4. Commercial Promise

Strategy Director should promise:

- clearer business direction
- less distraction
- smarter timing
- fewer premature upgrades
- stronger alignment between goals and operations
- better growth sequencing
- more confidence in what to do next

The commercial promise is not merely:

- "You get strategy notes."

It is:

- "Your business grows in the right order, with the right timing, instead of expanding into chaos."

Strategy Director must match the practical expectations customers already have from strong planning and strategic review systems:

- clear priorities
- visible next gate
- meaningful risk framing
- focus discipline
- usable decision horizon
- strategy tied to operating signals
- strategic clarity without consultant-style vagueness

At minimum, it must not feel weaker than a good executive planning or operational strategy layer.

## 6. Where AI-ABCX Can Beat the Market

Strategy Director can beat typical outside planning systems because:

- it sits inside one President-led AI company structure
- it reads from live operating truth instead of isolated strategy documents
- it can connect strategy directly to Scheduling, Service, Revenue, Finance, Reporting, and Accountability signals
- it can govern when to unlock broader automation instead of assuming more complexity is always better
- it can treat timing as a measurable dependency, not just an executive opinion

This is stronger than generic strategy software because it links direction directly to the same system that runs the company.

## 7. A / B / C Definition Draft

### Level A

Level A Strategy Director is focused direction and gate discipline.

Includes:

- current strategic focus
- visible next expansion gate
- narrow priority set
- basic strategy risk visibility
- simple timing discipline

Best fit:

- smaller service businesses
- owner-led companies
- businesses needing disciplined direction more than complex planning

### Level B

Level B Strategy Director adds stronger prioritization and planning intelligence.

Includes everything in A, plus:

- better expansion timing logic
- stronger priority conflict detection
- stronger department alignment framing
- clearer 30-day decision horizon
- stronger dependency mapping

Best fit:

- growing businesses
- companies activating more directors
- owners trying to scale without losing focus

### Level C

Level C Strategy Director adds advanced growth-orchestration logic.

Includes everything in B, plus:

- richer strategic scenario framing
- stronger cross-lane synthesis
- better staged expansion management
- more autonomous recommendation logic
- stronger use of operating truth to sequence broader system activation

Best fit:

- larger or scaling service businesses
- companies with multiple simultaneous growth pressures
- businesses seeking broader automation without strategic drift

## 8. Required Strategy Categories

Strategy Director should support at least these launch categories:

- primary focus
- next expansion gate
- strategic priorities
- focus drift
- strategy risk
- dependency review
- decision horizon

These categories should remain distinct so the President can immediately understand whether the issue is timing, breadth, readiness, or cross-lane dependency.

- `focus_locked`
- `expansion_gate_pending`
- `board_aligned`
- `focus_drift_detected`
- `priority_conflict_visible`
- `signal_gap_visible`
- `timing_risk_active`
- `dependency_ready`
- `strategic_refresh_needed`
- `president_attention_needed`

These states should make strategy operational rather than theoretical.

## 10. Required Shared Record

Strategy Director must operate from the same company truth used by the rest of AI-ABCX.

That record should preserve:

- current strategic focus
- next expansion gate
- active priorities
- decision horizon
- focus drift flag
- timing risk level
- dependency readiness
- supporting operating signals
- board alignment state
- President-facing note

Strategy Director should never create a detached planning truth layer.

---

## 11. Required Product Surfaces

Strategy Director should launch with visible surfaces, not just hidden planning logic.

Required surfaces:

- Executive Summary
- Priorities
- Risks
- Dependencies
- focus summary
- expansion gate summary
- decision horizon summary

These surfaces already have strong evidence in the President config and should be normalized into the canonical director product.

## 12. Required Dashboard Visibility

Strategy Director must be visible in the right places:

- President Dashboard
- CEO lane view
- Strategy Director surface
- board-facing summary logic
- growth / expansion recommendation logic
- monthly planning rhythm if present

The President should be able to see:

- what must remain fixed
- what should not be expanded yet
- what can unlock next
- what risks come from broadening too early

---

## 13. Required Handoffs

Strategy Director does not work alone.

It must hand off cleanly to and from:

- Executive Reporting Director
- Accountability Director
- Scheduling Director
- Service Director
- Revenue Control Director
- Reconciliation Director
- Advertising Director
- Marketing Director
- Sales Director

Examples:

- Executive Reporting gives Strategy the clean executive signal picture
- Strategy gives Executive Reporting the direction narrative
- Accountability tells Strategy whether commitments are strong enough to support expansion
- Operations lanes tell Strategy whether execution depth is stable enough for growth
- Revenue and Finance truth tell Strategy whether the business can support the next move
- Marketing and Sales tell Strategy whether demand-side expansion is justified

---

## 14. Guided Activation Requirements

Guided activation must determine:

- whether the owner wants stability or growth
- whether the business is trying to expand right now
- whether current operations are stable enough for broader automation
- whether the company wants partial system support or a stronger full-system path
- whether the owner values discipline over speed or speed over caution

The system should use this to recommend:

- whether Strategy Director stays at A
- whether B is justified for a scaling business
- whether C is appropriate for a larger or more aggressive growth posture

---

## 15. Pricing Decisions Still Required

The following pricing questions are still unresolved:

- whether Strategy Director is always included as a core CEO lane or priced separately
- if priced separately, whether it is treated as a premium advisory layer
- whether B and C strategy upgrades depend on broader multi-director activation
- whether stronger strategy logic should be packaged more directly into improved / advanced system recommendations

Until that is decided, build should proceed independently of pricing finalization.

---

## 16. Build Sequence

Recommended build order:

1. normalize Strategy Director role definition
2. normalize its A / B / C scope
3. define strategy categories and states
4. define shared-record fields for focus, timing, and gate logic
5. normalize Strategy Director dashboard surfaces
6. connect it to Executive Reporting
7. connect it to Accountability and Finance truth
8. connect it to Operations, Revenue, and growth-side lanes
9. define expansion-gate behavior
10. test timing, drift, and dependency logic

This order matters because strategy without real operating truth quickly becomes empty executive language.

---

Strategy Director should not be considered launch-ready until these tests pass:

1. current strategic focus remains visible
2. next expansion gate is shown clearly
3. focus drift appears correctly

10. strategy recommendations do not unlock broader complexity without real readiness
