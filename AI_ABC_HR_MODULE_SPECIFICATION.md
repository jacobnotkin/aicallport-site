# HR Module Specification

## Purpose

The `HR Module` is the staff accountability and workforce performance layer of AI-ABC.

It is not intended as generic HR software.

Its role is to help the President understand whether the business is being carried by a reliable workforce or being weakened by recurring accountability failures.

---

## Mission

Turn staff responsiveness and follow-through into visible management information.

---

## Primary Responsibilities

- monitor response discipline
- monitor confirmation discipline
- monitor completion discipline
- identify recurring accountability gaps
- surface training needs
- support workforce recommendations

---

## Inputs

- staff confirmations
- missed confirmations
- reminder counts
- reroute history
- completion reporting behavior
- repeated delays
- role-based workload patterns
- owner interventions tied to staff performance

---

## Outputs

- accountability summaries
- staff performance visibility
- training recommendations
- workforce-risk alerts
- role-level performance comparisons

---

## KPIs

- confirmation responsiveness
- completion reporting rate
- repeated reroute involvement
- missed confirmation rate
- owner intervention frequency by staff role
- workload consistency
- training-need frequency

---

## Reports

### Accountability Report

- repeated missed confirmations
- repeated late completions
- repeated missing completion reports
- repeated owner intervention events

### Workforce Performance Report

- high-reliability staff
- unstable response patterns
- role-level friction points
- recurring training needs

### Training Recommendation Report

- common error types
- recurring workflow misunderstandings
- staff groups that need additional support

---

## Recommendations

- address repeated confirmation failures
- provide role-specific training
- review workload balance
- reduce reroute dependence on one team member
- correct repeated process mistakes

---

## Dashboard Position

The HR Module should appear in later-stage management views when enough staff behavior data exists to make workforce recommendations meaningful.

---

## Dependencies

The HR Module depends on:

- Stage B staff dashboards
- appointment confirmations
- reminder logic
- rerouting logic
- completion reporting

---

## Future Roadmap

- workforce reliability scoring
- training-path recommendations
- staffing pressure forecasts
- accountability trend analysis
- hiring signal support

---

## Core Principle

The HR Module should help the President move from intuition about staff performance to actual operational visibility.
