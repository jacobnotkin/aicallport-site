# Bring Corporate Structure to Service Businesses

# Company Memory Specification

## Purpose

Company Memory is one of the most valuable long-term assets inside AI-ABC.

It is the structured memory layer that allows the system to remember what happened, what worked, what failed, and what keeps repeating.

Over time this should become a major competitive advantage.

---

## Mission

Turn company history into usable operating intelligence.

---

## What Company Memory Remembers

### Customers

The system should remember:

- identity
- service history
- communication history
- urgency patterns
- buying behavior
- follow-up outcomes
- unresolved issues

### Staff

The system should remember:

- role
- response behavior
- confirmation patterns
- completion discipline
- recurring strengths
- recurring failure points

### Jobs

The system should remember:

- issue type
- timing
- assigned personnel
- estimate
- completion outcome
- financial outcome
- follow-up result

### Outcomes

The system should remember:

- what succeeded
- what failed
- what stalled
- what required intervention
- what converted
- what was lost

### Patterns

The system should remember:

- recurring bottlenecks
- recurring urgency categories
- recurring conversion patterns
- recurring staff delays
- recurring payment issues

### Recurring Problems

Examples:

- missed confirmations
- incomplete reports
- repeated follow-up gaps
- repeated unpaid completed jobs
- repeated customer issue types

### Successful Actions

The system should remember:

- what follow-up timing converts better
- what service categories perform best
- what staff routing works best
- what management actions resolve recurring problems faster

- identify which customer types create the best revenue outcomes
