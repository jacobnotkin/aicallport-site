# AI-ABCX Retention Director A/B/C Launch Definition

## 1. Core Rule

Retention Director is the continuity and repeat-relationship lane of AI-ABCX.

Its job is to control:

- repeat-relationship visibility
- churn-risk visibility
- next-step clarity
- continuity-gap visibility
- reopen-risk visibility
- repeat-opportunity visibility
- relationship preservation after the initial work is done

It is not the same as:

- Customer Success Director, which controls the broader customer-health lane
- Support Director, which controls active issue response
- Follow-Up Director, which controls unresolved next-step discipline
- Marketing Director, which drives outbound demand and nurture

Retention Director controls whether an existing customer relationship stays alive, strengthens, or quietly drifts away.

---

## 2. Public Meaning Of Each Level

### Level A
Visible continuity truth.

### Level B
Connected retention workflow.

### Level C
Advanced retention orchestration.

---

## 3. Level A Definition

Level A Retention Director means the business can clearly see:

- which customer relationships are healthy
- which customers are watch accounts
- where the next step is missing
- where reopen risk exists
- where churn pressure is building
- where repeat-business opportunity is visible

Level A is a visibility-and-continuity layer first.

At Level A:

- continuity pressure is visible
- silent drift becomes visible
- repeat-business opportunity is visible
- next-step clarity is visible
- the President can see relationship pressure

Level A is the minimum level required for real retention control.

---

## 4. Level B Definition

Level B Retention Director means continuity becomes more coordinated, not just visible.

At Level B:

- repeat-relationship structure is stronger
- continuity ownership is clearer
- next-step discipline is stronger
- churn-risk handling is more structured
- retention logic connects more clearly to adjacent lanes
- repeat-opportunity management becomes more organized

Level B turns retention from visible continuity into a connected relationship-protection workflow.

This is the level where the business starts feeling that repeat business is being actively protected, not left to chance.

---

## 5. Level C Definition

Level C Retention Director means continuity becomes a high-control relationship-preservation lane.

At Level C:

- churn-risk is surfaced earlier
- continuity intervention logic is stronger
- repeat-opportunity intelligence is richer
- relationship-friction patterns are more visible
- cross-lane coordination is deeper
- executive visibility into relationship movement is stronger

Level C is for businesses that want a more advanced retention-control system with stronger continuity discipline and earlier drift detection.

This is the most complete version of retention orchestration at launch.

---

### A = visible continuity truth

### C = advanced retention orchestration

Everything in B, plus:

- stronger risk detection

- stronger executive visibility into relationship movement

---

## 7. Dependency Guidance

Retention Director can exist with partial independence, but it becomes strongest when paired with adjacent continuity and trust lanes.

### Can Retention Director exist by itself?

Yes, in a limited form.

This makes sense when a company already has:

- an existing customer base
- repeat customers or repeat opportunities
- continuity loss after completed work
- a need to protect existing customer value first

### Strongest pairings

Retention Director becomes much stronger with:

- Customer Success Director
- Follow-Up Director
- Support Director
- Marketing Director
- Reviews / Referrals logic
- Service Director

### Practical rule

- Retention A can stand alone more credibly
- Retention B usually works best with at least one adjacent director
- Retention C works best as part of a broader continuity and growth system

---

## 8. Recommendation Logic Guidance

Recommend **Retention Director A** when:

- customers disappear after the first job
- the business cannot clearly see who is drifting away
- repeat-business opportunity is not visible enough
- leadership needs continuity visibility first

Recommend **Retention Director B** when:

- continuity ownership is weak
- repeat-business follow-through is inconsistent
- churn-risk needs stronger structure
- support and follow-up outcomes are not connecting cleanly to retention

Recommend **Retention Director C** when:

- the company wants a higher-control retention lane
- repeat revenue matters heavily
- customer drift is operationally costly
- continuity logic needs to coordinate across multiple lanes
- leadership wants deeper visibility into relationship movement

## 10. What Must Be Avoided

Do not describe Retention Director as:

- just reminders
- only reactivation
- only repeat-marketing
- only churn alerts

That makes it too small.

Retention Director is the operating truth of relationship continuity after the first win.

Also avoid pretending:

- A is highly predictive
- B is the same as C
- C is only “more reports”

The level differences must stay operationally meaningful.
