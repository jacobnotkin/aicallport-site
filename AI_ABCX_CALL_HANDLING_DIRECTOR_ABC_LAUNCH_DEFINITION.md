# AI-ABCX Call Handling Director A B C Launch Definition

This document locks the launch meaning of `Call Handling Director` across:

- implementation scope
- pricing logic
- recommendation logic
- activation explanation
- upgrade logic
- customer-facing language

It should be treated as the single reference for what `A`, `B`, and `C` mean at launch.

---

## 1. Core Rule

`Call Handling Director` is not just a generic answering bot.

It is the structured AI communication lane that receives inbound phone demand, understands why the caller is calling, preserves call truth, and routes the conversation into the correct business path under President visibility.

The launch-level meaning must stay simple:

- `A` = visible call continuity
- `B` = connected call workflow
- `C` = advanced call-conversion orchestration

---

## 2. Public Meaning Of Each Level

### Level A

`Call Handling Director A` means the business has AI call answering, intake capture, caller qualification basics, and visible unresolved call pressure.

### Level B

`Call Handling Director B` means the business has stronger call intelligence, stronger after-hours continuity, and connected routing into estimates, scheduling, and follow-up paths.

### Level C

`Call Handling Director C` means the business has the strongest call-handling logic, richer intent capture, deeper exception handling, and the most advanced call-to-workflow orchestration across the company.

---

## 3. Level A Definition

### Positioning

Foundational AI receptionist and continuity layer.

### What it means operationally

The system answers calls, captures who called and why, preserves the interaction, recognizes basic urgency, and creates a visible record that the President and operations lane can trust.

### Level A must include

- inbound call answering
- business recognition and greeting logic
- caller identity capture where available
- reason-for-call capture
- new customer vs existing customer distinction
- basic urgency detection
- basic qualification
- transcript or structured call summary preservation
- call record creation
- callback-needed visibility
- unresolved-call visibility
- after-hours continuity in basic form
- spam / invalid call classification in basic form

### Level A call outcomes must support at minimum

- `answered`
- `information_resolved`
- `qualified_lead`
- `estimate_path_created`
- `booking_intake_created`
- `callback_needed`
- `after_hours_continuity`
- `spam_or_invalid`
- `unresolved`

### Level A is best for

- owner-led businesses
- businesses missing calls
- companies needing continuity first
- simpler inbound call patterns
- companies that want visible communication control before deeper automation

### Level A commercial meaning

The business is buying dependable AI receptionist discipline and visible phone truth, not deep orchestration yet.

---

## 4. Level B Definition

### Positioning

Connected professional communication workflow.

### What it means operationally

The system does not just answer and log calls. It qualifies better, branches better, handles after-hours more intelligently, and routes callers into the correct next business lane with stronger workflow discipline.

### Level B includes everything in A, plus

- stronger behavior branching
- deeper lead and caller qualification
- stronger service-type detection
- stronger estimate-intent and booking-intent detection
- stronger after-hours logic
- stronger reschedule and cancellation handling
- stronger multilingual workflow control where configured
- stronger workflow write-back into the operating record
- stronger routing into:
  - `Estimator Director`
  - `Scheduling Director`
  - `Follow-Up Director`
  - `Sales Director`
  - `Support Director`
- clearer next-step assignment
- stronger callback discipline

### Level B is best for

- growing service teams
- companies with mixed inbound call types
- businesses where phone calls create estimates, appointments, and ongoing customer obligations
- businesses that need stronger connected workflow rather than simple answering only

### Level B commercial meaning

The business is buying connected communication management, not just continuity.

---

## 5. Level C Definition

### Positioning

Advanced multi-behavior call-conversion orchestration.

### What it means operationally

The call lane becomes one of the strongest conversion and routing systems in the company. The AI handles broader intent patterns, preserves more business truth, manages more exceptions, and coordinates more deeply with other directors.

### Level C includes everything in B, plus

- richer intent capture
- stronger service-specific decision trees
- deeper exception handling
- stronger uncertain-intent handling
- stronger complaint / sensitive-case routing
- broader workflow orchestration
- stronger escalation intelligence
- stronger executive visibility into communication pressure
- stronger President-facing communication truth
- stronger recommendation support for what should happen next
- stronger orchestration with:
  - `Estimator Director`
  - `Scheduling Director`
  - `Follow-Up Director`
  - `Sales Director`
  - `Support Director`
  - `Website Director`

### Level C is best for

- higher-volume inbound call environments
- multilingual or multi-scenario businesses
- estimate-driven and appointment-driven businesses with heavy phone dependence
- companies using phone as a primary conversion threshold
- companies needing the strongest connected workflow behavior at launch

### Level C commercial meaning

The business is buying the most advanced AI call-management layer AI-ABCX offers at launch.

---

## 6. Exact Launch Difference Between A B and C

The cleanest way to explain the difference is this:

- `A` answers, captures, preserves, and makes pressure visible
- `B` adds stronger call logic and connected routing
- `C` adds the deepest call intelligence and strongest orchestration

Another way to say it:

- `A` = “we stop losing the phone”
- `B` = “we route phone demand correctly into the business”
- `C` = “the phone becomes a high-quality conversion and workflow-control lane”

---

## 7. Dependency Guidance

### Can A sell independently

Yes.

`Call Handling Director A` is a valid standalone entry product for businesses that mainly need:

- AI receptionist continuity
- missed-call protection
- visible intake
- basic qualification

### When B is strongly recommended

`B` should be recommended more often when the business needs:

- estimate-aware call routing
- booking-aware call routing
- mixed new-customer and existing-customer phone traffic
- stronger after-hours behavior
- clearer next-step movement

### When C is strongly recommended

`C` should be recommended more often when:

- the phone is a major revenue threshold
- call complexity is high
- there are multiple outcomes from one call lane
- stronger exception handling is required
- communication has to coordinate closely with other directors

---

## 8. Recommendation Logic Guidance

Recommend `A` when:

- the owner mainly wants calls answered
- missed calls are the main pain
- the company needs continuity first
- workflow complexity is still low

Recommend `B` when:

- calls regularly create estimates, appointments, and follow-up obligations
- after-hours and call-type branching matter
- the business wants stronger workflow connection

Recommend `C` when:

- call handling is one of the company’s biggest conversion thresholds
- phone demand is complex or high volume
- the business wants strong automation depth and stronger orchestration across the system

---

## 9. Business-Friendly Explanation For Customers

Use language like this:

### Level A

Your AI call handling answers the phone, captures what the caller needs, and keeps missed opportunities visible.

### Level B

Your AI call handling not only answers calls, but routes them more intelligently into estimates, scheduling, and follow-up.

### Level C

Your AI call handling becomes an advanced communication control layer that handles more complex caller intent and coordinates more deeply with the rest of your system.

---

## 10. What Must Be Avoided

Do not describe the levels in vague ways like:

- “A is basic”
- “B is better”
- “C is premium”

That is too abstract.

The levels must always be described in terms of:

- what kind of call behavior exists
- what kind of routing exists
- how much business truth is preserved
- how deeply the call lane connects into the system

---

## 11. Pricing Use

This definition supports the pricing logic already discussed:

- monthly director fee
- visible per-minute usage fee
- no confusing AI-credit public pricing language

Pricing differences between `A`, `B`, and `C` should reflect:

- communication workflow depth
- routing depth
- orchestration depth
- exception-handling depth

Not just “more minutes.”

Minutes are variable operating usage.
The level is the communication-management intelligence layer.
