# AI-ABCX SEO Director Product Spec

This document defines the implementation-grade product specification for `SEO Director`.

It takes the benchmark research, launch checklist, pricing logic, and wave planning and turns them into one practical build target.

SEO Director is one of the core Wave 3 launch directors because it controls organic visibility, local discovery readiness, and priority-page execution inside the AI-ABCX growth system.

---

## 1. Product Identity

### Public name

`SEO Director`

### Executive owner

`CMO`

### Mission

SEO Director makes organic search visibility operate like a governed business function instead of a vague marketing hope. It controls priority pages, brief readiness, local discovery readiness, and website search fit while keeping the President informed about what is growing, what is blocked, and what should happen next.

### Core promise

SEO Director should not feel like a generic rank tracker, a weak scorecard, or a disconnected agency report.

It should promise:

- clearer visibility into whether organic demand is growing or stalling
- stronger execution discipline around pages and briefs
- better alignment between search intent and real services
- less wasted content effort
- better website-readiness visibility for search growth
- stronger President visibility into what is blocking organic growth

---

## 2. Strategic Role Inside AI-ABCX

SEO Director is the organic discovery and search-visibility lane of the system.

It manages the operating span between:

`service intent -> page priority -> brief readiness -> website fit -> publish readiness -> organic demand contribution -> next SEO action`

It is strategically important because:

- many service businesses publish pages without real structure or prioritization
- local discovery often depends on basic execution discipline, not only technical SEO depth
- businesses frequently do not know whether their website is helping or hurting discoverability
- search growth problems are often caused by missing pages, weak briefs, or intent mismatch
- the President should understand whether organic discovery is becoming stronger or staying blocked

SEO Director can be:

- a standalone organic-growth director
- part of a growth bundle
- part of a full-system recommendation

- `HighLevel`

### What they do well

- page and keyword visibility
- local discovery awareness
- page-priority planning
- content and brief visibility
- execution tracking
- site health and publish readiness awareness

### What AI-ABCX must match

- visibility into priority pages
- visibility into brief readiness
- visibility into blocked pages
- basic intent-fit awareness
- local service business relevance
- practical prioritization instead of vague SEO theory

### Where AI-ABCX should beat them

- SEO lives inside one President-led corporate operating system

---

## 4. Ideal Customer Fit

SEO Director is a strong fit for:

- businesses that depend on local search visibility
- companies with websites but weak organic discoverability
- businesses publishing pages without consistent structure
- companies wanting sustainable growth beyond paid ads
- teams unsure which service pages or location pages should exist first

It is especially strong for:

- local service businesses
- multi-service companies
- website-led intake businesses
- businesses expanding by service line or geography
- companies that want a long-term demand layer in addition to paid growth

## 5. Required User Outcomes

SEO Director must let a business owner say:

- “I can see whether organic growth is improving or stalling.”
- “I know which pages matter most right now.”
- “I know which briefs are missing.”
- “I can see what is blocked before search growth suffers more.”
- “My website’s search readiness is visible.”
- “SEO feels like a business system, not a vague promise.”
- “The President Dashboard tells me the next organic priority.”

## 6. Functional Scope

SEO Director must cover eight major functions:

1. priority-page visibility
2. brief and content-readiness control
3. page-blockage visibility
4. intent-fit discipline
5. website search-fit dependency awareness
6. local discovery readiness visibility
7. organic-demand contribution awareness
8. President-visible SEO priorities and risks

## 7. SEO Categories

SEO Director must support category-aware organic-growth logic.

Launch-required categories:

- priority pages
- service pages
- local intent pages
- brief queue
- blocked pages
- publishing-ready pages
- intent-fit evaluation

- distribution / support sync

## 11. Business Logic

SEO Director must follow these operating rules:

1. a service line should not be treated as search-ready without page and brief visibility
2. missing briefs must create visible execution pressure
3. blocked pages must stay visible until cleared
4. intent-fit mismatch must be surfaced as a business-growth issue
5. website structure problems must be visible when they affect search readiness
6. organic growth should be discussed in terms of discoverability, readiness, and opportunity, not just abstract rankings

SEO Director should behave like an organic-demand operating lane, not a theory-heavy SEO report.

---

## 12. Dependencies

SEO Director is commercially strong alone, but operationally it becomes much stronger when paired correctly.

### Strong pairings

- `Website Director`
- `Marketing Director`
- `Lead Generation Director`
- `Advertising Director`
- `Sales Director`

### Why those dependencies matter

- Website Director controls whether page structure, layout, and technical readiness support discoverability
- Marketing Director coordinates message, offer, and broader growth alignment
- Lead Generation Director helps reveal whether organic traffic is producing fit demand
- Advertising Director helps compare paid and organic growth pressure
- Sales Director helps validate whether organic demand is turning into real business outcomes

### Standalone viability

SEO Director can be sold independently to businesses that want better organic discovery and local visibility.

However, for stronger AI-ABCX performance it should usually be recommended with:

- Website Director
- Marketing Director

## 13. A / B / C Definition

### Level A

Level A SEO Director is foundational organic visibility control.

Includes:

- priority-page visibility
- brief-gap visibility
- blocked-page visibility
- basic intent-fit status
- basic local discovery readiness
- President summary of SEO health

Best fit:

- companies starting structured SEO
- businesses with weak or inconsistent page discipline
- owner-led service businesses wanting clarity before scale

### Level B

Level B SEO Director adds connected organic-growth automation.

Includes everything in A, plus:

- page readiness by service line
- stronger website dependency visibility
- stronger lead-intent feedback from organic demand
- stronger publishing-queue control
- organic contribution visibility to lead quality

Best fit:

- businesses with multi-service websites
- businesses using SEO as a meaningful growth lane
- companies coordinating website and marketing together

### Level C

Level C SEO Director adds advanced organic-growth management.

Includes everything in B, plus:

- stronger recommendation logic
- stronger prioritization across pages, briefs, and service lines
- growth-risk warnings
- stronger market and service-line comparison
- President-facing next-action recommendations

Best fit:

- scaling businesses
- multi-location or multi-service companies
- businesses treating SEO as a strategic growth asset

### Level X

Highest future intelligent orchestration level.

Not required for launch, but the architecture should not block it.

## 14. Pricing Direction

SEO Director pricing should reflect long-term growth value, not just a content checklist.

It helps the business become easier to discover, keeps execution disciplined, and improves the value of the website and growth system over time.

Directional positioning:

- A should feel accessible for businesses beginning structured SEO
- B should feel like the serious operating level for connected organic growth
- C should feel like advanced organic-demand management for scaling companies

SEO Director should price above weak SEO scoring tools and below the real cost of fragmented website, content, and SEO operations replacements.

---

## 15. Diagnostic Recommendation Logic

SEO Director should be recommended when any of the following are true:

- the business wants more organic leads
- the company depends on local discovery
- the business has a website but weak search visibility
- the owner wants long-term growth beyond paid ads
- service pages are weak, inconsistent, or missing
- the company plans to expand by geography or service line

SEO Director should become a strong recommendation when:

- Website Director is already recommended
- Marketing Director is already recommended
- the business wants sustainable growth with lower dependence on paid demand

---

## 16. Recommended Diagnostic Questions

Step 1 or later qualification should help determine whether SEO Director is recommended and at what level.

Recommended questions:

- Do you want more customers to find you through Google or organic search?
- Do you know which service pages bring in the best organic leads today?
- Do you currently invest in SEO or publish service pages regularly?
- Is your website organized clearly by services and locations?
- Do you want long-term growth beyond advertising?
- Are some services or areas more important for growth than others?
- Do you feel your website is easy to discover locally?
- Do you want AI-ABCX to guide what pages and search priorities should come next?

These questions should help classify:

- whether SEO Director is needed
- whether Website Director should be paired
- whether Marketing Director should be paired
- whether A, B, or C is the right level

- President Dashboard SEO summary

## 18. Launch Test Scenarios

SEO Director is not launch-ready until it passes practical scenario testing.

Required launch scenarios:

1. a service line has a priority page active and is visible as healthy
2. a key service line is missing a brief and shows visible execution pressure
3. a page is blocked by Website Director dependency and is surfaced clearly
4. intent fit is strong for one service line and drifting for another
5. local page readiness is visible for a business targeting multiple service areas
6. President Dashboard shows whether the next SEO action is page creation, brief completion, or website correction
7. SEO health changes are explained in business terms, not vague SEO terms

---

## 19. Launch Exit Condition

SEO Director can be considered launch-complete when:

- priority pages are visible clearly
- brief gaps are visible clearly
- blocked pages remain visible until cleared
- website dependencies are surfaced meaningfully
- intent-fit logic exists at a usable launch level
- A / B / C behavior is defined clearly
- President-facing next actions are visible
- the director feels like a real organic-demand lane, not a weak SEO score

---

SEO Director is a `Wave 3` launch director.

It should be built after:

- `Website Director`
- `Marketing Director`
- `Advertising Director`

It should be tightly coordinated with:

- `Lead Generation Director`
- `Sales Director`

Its value becomes materially stronger when Website and Marketing are already defined cleanly.

SEO Director is the AI-ABCX organic discovery and search-visibility lane.

It gives service businesses structured visibility into what pages matter, what briefs are missing, what website issues are blocking discoverability, and what should happen next to improve long-term organic demand.

It is not just SEO reporting.

It is managed organic growth under President control.
