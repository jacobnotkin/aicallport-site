AI Call Handling

AI Call Handling

The system answers inbound calls automatically, understands the caller’s intent, and converts the conversation into something operationally useful for the business.

Intent Detection

Understands whether the caller is a new lead, quote request, booking request, support question, or urgent service need.

Call Records

Creates summaries, transcripts, and recordings so the owner can review exactly what happened after the call ends.

Lead and quote captureThe AI gathers the customer details and operational context the business needs to follow up properly.
Urgent routing logicCritical calls can be identified early and pushed toward urgent workflows instead of ordinary handling.

Scheduling and Reservations

When scheduling is enabled, the same system can support service appointments, sales bookings, and reservation-style workflows with reminders and confirmation tracking.

Booking Workflows

Supports service appointments, sales appointments, and reservation-type flows depending on the business model.

Scheduling Add-On

Scheduling is added as a clear monthly upgrade, not hidden inside unclear package language.

Reminders and confirmationsClients can receive reminders and confirm from email or text messages before the appointment or reservation.
Availability supportReservation or booking availability can be supported, but inventory tracking is not part of the standard system.

Client Notifications

The system keeps clients informed by email and SMS, then brings those responses back into the dashboard so the owner and staff are not guessing.

Email and SMS

Clients receive appointment notices, reminders, and confirmation requests through familiar communication channels.

Confirmation Tracking

The dashboard updates when clients confirm, decline, or fail to respond so follow-up becomes operationally clear.

Less manual chasingThe business does not have to manually confirm every booking when automated reminders and confirmations are already in place.
Visible delivery issuesEmail or SMS failures are surfaced so the owner can fix contact records instead of silently losing communication.

Dashboard and Built-In CRM

AI Call Port includes its own dashboard and built-in CRM so businesses can operate from one place without needing external software from day one.

Operational Dashboard

Owners can see calls, appointments, confirmations, urgent alerts, booked value, billing, and business activity in one system.

Built-In CRM

Client records, notes, communication history, and call outcomes live inside the platform automatically.

Searchable client recordsEvery call, booking, and follow-up action can connect back to the same client profile.
Optional external CRM syncIf the business already uses a supported CRM, that becomes an add-on rather than a requirement.

Staff and Provider Workflows

The system supports assigned staff or providers, personal availability, acceptance tracking, and completion tracking without forcing all setup into the pre-activation funnel.

Assigned Work

Appointments and operational tasks can be routed to specific team members instead of staying only at the owner level.

Availability Management

Each staff user can manage their own availability after activation inside the dashboard.

Acceptance and completionStaff can confirm receipt of assigned work and later confirm that it was completed.
Post-activation setupNames, availability, services, and internal pricing are added after activation, not forced into the early signup flow.

Alerts and Sound Notifications

The dashboard includes notification logic so urgent events, confirmations, cancellations, delivery failures, and unfinished work stay visible.

Urgent Visibility

Important events can trigger stronger alerts for the owner or assigned staff instead of blending into routine updates.

Sound Priority

High-priority events can use sound alerts so the dashboard behaves like a real operational tool, not just a passive report screen.

Owner and staff notificationsEach side of the business can be alerted to what needs their attention right now.
Delivery and response trackingThe system can show whether client reminders were delivered, confirmed, ignored, or failed.

Reports and Business Visibility

AI Call Port is not only a voice system. It also gives the owner reporting on how calls become bookings, confirmations, completed work, and booked value.

Operational Metrics

Track call volume, booking conversion, confirmation rate, completion rate, and urgent call activity.

Commercial Visibility

Owners can also track booked value, completed value, and where operational leakage is happening.

Performance reviewStaff follow-through and business response patterns become visible instead of hidden across phones, texts, and memory.
Clear owner pulseThe dashboard homepage and reports page are designed to show what matters now and how the system is performing over time.

Activation and Post-Activation Setup

The flow is designed to make the customer confident before payment, then let the business finish deeper setup inside the dashboard after activation.

Before Activation

The business completes intake, reviews the configured system and pricing, verifies contact details, signs documents, and runs a 3-minute live test.

After Activation

The business adds staff names, availability, services, internal prices, and other deeper workflow settings in the dashboard.
