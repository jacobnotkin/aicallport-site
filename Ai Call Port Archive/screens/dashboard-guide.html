Dashboard Guide

How the active dashboard supports daily business operations

After activation, AI Call Port becomes the business’s operating dashboard. This guide explains the main areas customers use after launch and what the dashboard helps manage day to day.

1. Introduction

The dashboard is used to manage calls, client records, appointments, staff, availability, follow-up activity, reporting, alerts, and requests from one place.

2. Dashboard Overview

The owner dashboard gives visibility into the most important parts of the business.

  • Recent calls, appointments, confirmations, and alerts
  • Client activity, operational requests, and reporting

3. Calls and CRM

The system stores and organizes call activity automatically.

  • Call summaries, transcripts, recordings, and follow-up actions
  • Client records and communication history in the built-in CRM

4. Appointments and Scheduling

If scheduling is enabled, the dashboard supports service and sales appointment workflows.

  • Booking, reminders, confirmations, and completion tracking
  • Visibility into booked activity and follow-through

5. Staff and Availability

The dashboard supports staff records, assignments, personal calendars, availability windows, blocked time, and booked appointments.

Each assigned user sees only their own calendar and assignments in their personal dashboard view.

6. Voice and Package Settings

After activation, the customer can manage voice style and package settings from the dashboard.

  • Voice style can be changed inside the dashboard
  • Package changes between Standard and Luxury take effect on the next billing date

7. Reports, Alerts, and Requests

The dashboard provides notifications, urgent alerts, reports, operational requests, and business activity tracking so the owner can monitor performance and respond quickly.

8. Ongoing Use

After activation, AI Call Port becomes the working system for daily call handling, appointments, client follow-up, staff coordination, and operational visibility.

AI Call Port is not a fixed system. It is designed to continue evolving through updates, refinements, and new versions, so activated customers benefit from ongoing improvements and expanding functionality over time.
