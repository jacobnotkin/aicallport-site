Activation Guide

How activation works from first setup to live system

AI Call Port is designed to let a business understand the system before activation. The process is guided, visible, and structured so customers can review the setup, test the experience, and activate only when ready.

1. Introduction

The activation flow has 3 steps: business intake, system review and verification, and live testing with dashboard preview before final activation.

2. Step 1

Step 1 collects the information needed to prepare the system correctly for the business.

  • Business details, contact details, country, and industry
  • Scheduling needs, staff size, locations, and follow-up needs
  • Preferred package and preferred voice style

3. Step 2

Step 2 shows the configured system and explains how it will work for the business.

  • Configured modules and business benefits are explained clearly
  • Package options and voice samples can be reviewed again
  • Business email and phone are verified before live testing

4. Step 3

Step 3 is where the business experiences the system before activation.

  • Live test of the system
  • Dashboard preview
  • Final package and voice confirmation before activation

5. Payment

After the system is reviewed and tested, the customer sees the final price and can activate.

  • Both packages include the same full AI Call Port system
  • Standard and Luxury change the voice tier, included voice minutes, and overage rate
  • Upgrades are added only if the business needs them

6. Product: User Active Dashboard

After activation, the customer gets access to the active dashboard.

  • Calls, CRM, appointments, reporting, alerts, and requests
  • Staff setup, availability, and workflow management
  • Voice style changes inside the dashboard and package changes on the next billing date

AI Call Port is not a fixed system. It is designed to continue evolving through updates, refinements, and new versions, so activated customers benefit from ongoing improvements and expanding functionality over time.
