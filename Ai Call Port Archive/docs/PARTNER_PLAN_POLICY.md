# AI Call Port Partner Plan Policy

This document defines the partner plan for the public launch of AI Call Port Stage A: the AI Call Agent.

It is meant to be a real operating policy, not a loose idea.

## Purpose

AI Call Port is launched publicly through Stage A: the AI Call Agent.

The regular plan is for standard customers who want to use the product without deeper participation.

The partner plan exists for companies that are willing to work more closely with AI Call Port by giving structured, consistent feedback in exchange for additional benefits.

This means the partner plan is not just a discount.
It is a different relationship with defined benefits and defined obligations.

The partner plan also supports the broader company goal of building not only customers, but loyal participants who help the system grow and gain value as it improves.

## Core Principle

The partner plan is designed to help AI Call Port improve faster without limiting the launch to a small closed group.

This allows:

- broad public launch of Stage A
- stronger learning from selected partner companies
- faster improvement of scripts, routing logic, fallback behavior, and business-specific handling
- stronger proof collection through real feedback, reviews, and case-study participation
- a stronger community layer around the company, not just a transaction layer

## Who The Partner Plan Is For

The partner plan is for companies that:

- are a strong fit for the AI Call Agent
- will use the system consistently enough to generate real operating feedback
- are willing to communicate clearly and regularly
- are open to participating in reviews, proof collection, and structured improvement cycles
- understand that they are receiving extra benefits in exchange for active participation

The partner plan is not for companies that only want the lower price without the added involvement.

## Stage Coverage

The partner plan applies to Stage A at launch.

Stage A means:

- AI Call Agent
- advanced call handling
- conversation-category logic
- lead capture
- qualification
- routing / escalation
- after-hours logic
- emergency logic
- business-specific handling rules

At launch, the partner plan does not automatically include:

- sales scheduling
- service scheduling
- future modules not yet part of the active customer setup

## Pricing Structure

Working pricing structure:

- Regular Stage A plan: `$499/month`
- Partner Stage A plan: `$349/month`

This pricing difference exists because the partner company is giving AI Call Port more than ordinary usage.

They are contributing:

- structured operational feedback
- review participation
- proof participation
- tolerance for faster iteration and product refinement

## Partner Benefits

Partner companies receive:

- reduced monthly pricing
- priority attention during onboarding and setup
- tighter founder/operator review loop
- earlier visibility into improvements that affect their use case
- priority consideration for future module access when relevant
- stronger collaboration around script quality, call handling, and business-fit logic
- a personal referral link tied to their partner account
- a `$15` monthly credit for each referred active customer
- dashboard visibility into referral activity and earned monthly credits

These benefits should be treated as real operational advantages, not just marketing language.

## Referral Credit Rules

Each partner company should receive a personal referral link.

For every referred customer that becomes active, the partner should receive a `$15` credit against its own monthly payment.

The referral-credit structure should follow these rules:

- credits are earned only for active referred customers
- credits reduce the monthly partner invoice
- referral activity and credit totals should be visible in the partner dashboard
- the dashboard should make it clear which referrals are pending, active, or no longer credit-eligible

This benefit is part of the partner plan and should be treated as a defined company policy, not an informal reward.

## Partner Obligations

Partner companies are expected to:

- provide consistent feedback on real call performance
- respond to follow-up questions when issues or patterns appear
- participate in periodic review or check-in conversations
- report obvious failures, awkward call moments, and business-specific edge cases
- help confirm what is working well, not only what is failing
- participate in review, testimonial, or case-study requests when the results justify it

The partner plan should not require dishonest praise.

However, it should require honest participation in proof creation when the system is delivering real value.

## Policy Agreement Requirement

Any company entering the partner plan must explicitly agree to the partner plan policy during the activation pipeline.

This agreement should happen before final activation, not after.

That means the partner activation flow should include clear confirmation that the company understands and accepts:

- the partner pricing
- the partner benefits
- the partner feedback obligations
- the review and proof expectations
- the conditions for losing partner status

Partner pricing should not be activated unless this agreement is completed.

## Reviews And Proof Policy

The partner plan includes an expectation of public proof participation.

This can include:

- written reviews
- short testimonials
- approval to use outcome-based quotes
- participation in lightweight case studies
- permission to reference results when appropriate

The goal is not forced positive reviews.
The goal is real public proof tied to real results.

If a partner company is receiving the benefits of the plan and the system is delivering clear value, they are expected to cooperate reasonably in creating review and proof assets.

## Approval Policy

Not every company should automatically qualify for the partner plan.

AI Call Port should approve partner companies based on:

- business fit
- expected call volume and learning value
- willingness to participate actively
- clarity of use case
- likelihood of producing useful feedback and proof

A company can still become a regular customer without being approved for the partner plan.

## Removal From Partner Status

A company can lose partner-plan status if it:

- stops providing reasonable feedback
- ignores review/check-in requests repeatedly
- only uses the lower price without honoring the relationship
- becomes a poor fit for the collaboration model

If that happens, AI Call Port may:

- move the company to the regular plan at the next billing point
- end partner-only benefits
- preserve service under the normal plan if the customer still wants to continue

This should be handled clearly and professionally.

## Internal Operating Rule

The partner plan should be treated as a structured product policy, not as random founder discretion.

That means:

- approval criteria should be clear
- obligations should be explained before acceptance
- benefits should be consistent
- proof requests should be reasonable and tied to real value
- pricing should be justified by participation
- partner-policy agreement should be part of the activation pipeline

## Strategic Meaning

The partner plan allows AI Call Port to:

1. Launch Stage A publicly without relying on a hard company limit.
2. Keep a stronger learning loop with selected businesses.
3. Improve the AI Call Agent faster through real operating feedback.
4. Build stronger reviews, testimonials, and case-study proof.
5. Keep the launch looking like a real company launch, not only a closed beta experiment.
