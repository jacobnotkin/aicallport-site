## 1. Lead Generation Director Mission

Lead Generation Director is the demand-entry control lane of AI-ABCX.

Its job is to:

- make incoming demand visible by source
- protect lead quality over raw lead quantity
- classify demand correctly at the point of entry
- keep ownership and routing visible
- prevent weak-fit, duplicate, or aging leads from distorting the system
- preserve a clean handoff into sales and follow-up
- report demand quality pressure directly to the President

Lead Generation Director should not feel like a vague marketing summary.

It should feel like a structured inbound-demand operating lane under President control.

---

## 2. Lead Generation Director Launch Standard

Lead Generation Director is launch-ready only when it can do all of the following:

1. show incoming demand by source clearly
2. distinguish lead quality from lead volume
3. support source labeling and source taxonomy consistently
4. support qualification and routing states cleanly
5. coordinate directly with Website Director, Advertising Director, Call Handling Director, and Sales Director
6. show where leads are aging, weak-fit, duplicated, or unrouted
7. surface high-fit lead pressure visibly to the President
8. support A / B / C levels clearly

If one of these is missing, the director is not complete.

---

## 3. Canonical Role Definition

### Public-facing name

`Lead Generation Director`

### Internal role meaning

The AI-ABCX director responsible for source-level inbound demand visibility, lead quality control, routing discipline, and clean handoff of qualified demand into the revenue lane.

### Executive owner

`CRO`

Lead Generation Director belongs to the revenue lane because it controls the front door of opportunity quality before the sales process begins.

---

## 4. Commercial Promise

Lead Generation Director should promise:

- clearer visibility into where demand is coming from
- better understanding of which sources are producing high-fit opportunities
- fewer weak or mismatched leads entering the queue
- cleaner ownership and routing before revenue work begins
- faster movement on the best leads
- better President visibility into demand quality

The commercial promise is not merely:

- "You get a lead dashboard."

It is:

- "Your demand becomes visible, structured, and qualified before it wastes time or money."

---

## 5. What AI-ABCX Must Match

Lead Generation Director must match the practical market expectations customers already have from strong lead and demand systems:

- source attribution
- lead capture visibility
- routing ownership
- qualification awareness
- duplicate awareness
- response timing awareness
- source-level performance visibility

At minimum, it must not feel weaker than a modern service-business lead pipeline tool.

---

## 6. Where AI-ABCX Can Beat the Market

Lead Generation Director can beat typical outside demand tools because:

- it lives inside one President-led corporate structure
- it can coordinate directly with Advertising, Website, Call Handling, Sales, Follow-Up, and Reactivation
- it can preserve one opportunity record instead of fragmenting demand across ads tools, web forms, phone logs, and CRM notes
- it can make lead quality visible as an executive operating issue, not just a marketing metric
- it can route demand into the rest of the company model without creating disconnected sub-systems

This is stronger than a typical standalone lead tool because it keeps demand quality connected to the entire operating system.

## 7. A / B / C Definition Draft

### Level A

Level A Lead Generation Director is visible lead intake and qualification basics.

Includes:

- visible lead source labeling
- lead capture visibility
- basic fit logic
- clear ownership and routing states
- high-fit vs low-fit lead awareness
- President visibility into demand quality pressure

Best fit:

- small businesses
- early-stage marketing setups
- owner-led teams trying to stop wasting time on weak demand

### Level B

Level B Lead Generation Director adds stronger routing and quality control.

Includes everything in A, plus:

- stronger qualification handling
- better source comparison
- better queue and response visibility
- stronger coordination with Sales and Follow-Up
- better visibility into where leads stall or weaken

Best fit:

- growing businesses
- teams using multiple lead sources
- companies that want clearer control over conversion quality

### Level C

Level C Lead Generation Director adds advanced multi-source orchestration.

Includes everything in B, plus:

- deeper source-level prioritization
- tighter autonomous routing logic
- stronger opportunity ranking
- deeper coordination with Advertising and Website optimization
- richer President and CRO visibility into demand mix and quality drift

Best fit:

- businesses actively scaling
- businesses buying leads across multiple channels
- companies wanting high-automation demand quality control

## 8. Required Lead Categories

Lead Generation Director should support at least these launch categories:

- ad lead
- website form lead
- inbound call lead
- referral lead
- reactivation lead
- direct outreach lead
- repeat customer lead

These categories should remain distinguishable so the system can apply different routing and follow-up logic.

---

## 9. Required Lead States

At minimum, Lead Generation Director should support:

- `new_lead`
- `awaiting_review`
- `qualified`
- `unqualified`
- `routed`
- `unrouted`
- `duplicate_review`
- `follow_up_needed`
- `in_sales_handoff`
- `lost`
- `president_attention_needed`

These states align with the current dashboard logic and should be normalized rather than replaced with vague labels.

---

## 10. Required Shared Job / Opportunity Record

Lead Generation Director must operate on the same shared record used by the rest of AI-ABCX.

That record should preserve:

- source channel
- source category
- lead type
- fit score
- assigned owner
- routing target
- time received
- time reviewed
- next-step status
- handoff status
- follow-up requirement

Lead Generation Director should never create a detached source-only truth layer.

---

## 11. Required Product Surfaces

Launch surfaces should include:

- Lead Source Summary
- Open Lead Queue
- Lead Quality Summary
- Routing / Ownership View
- Duplicate and weak-fit review surface
- Source performance summary

If a dedicated Lead Generation dashboard does not exist at launch, these surfaces still need to be visible in the President and CRO lanes.

---

## 12. Required Dashboard Visibility

Lead Generation must be visible in the right places:

- President Dashboard
- CRO demand summary
- sales handoff visibility
- follow-up visibility where unrouted or aging leads need action

The President should always be able to see:

- where leads are coming from
- which leads are high-fit
- which leads are weak-fit
- which leads are unrouted
- which sources are drifting

---

## 13. Required Director Handoffs

Lead Generation Director must hand off cleanly with:

### Advertising Director

- receives paid demand
- compares source quality across campaigns
- surfaces weak or strong channel quality

### Website Director

- receives web form demand
- depends on website capture clarity
- requires form and funnel connection visibility

### Call Handling Director

- receives inbound call demand
- depends on call classification quality
- hands off qualified caller intent into the lead queue

### Sales Director

- routes qualified opportunities into conversion control
- preserves ownership at the moment of handoff
- supports revenue-lane continuity

### Follow-Up Director

- preserves contact rhythm for leads not yet converted
- supports aging lead recovery
- keeps next-step ownership visible

### Reactivation Director

- distinguishes new demand from returning and legacy demand
- prevents old records from polluting new lead logic

---

## 14. Guided Activation Requirements

The activation process must determine:

- where the company gets leads now
- whether they trust current lead quality
- whether source attribution exists today
- whether weak-fit leads are wasting time
- whether leads are being routed clearly
- whether response speed is a problem
- whether the company is trying to grow volume, improve quality, or both

Without these answers, Lead Generation Director cannot be configured correctly.

---

## 15. Pricing Decisions Required

Pricing for Lead Generation Director still needs final decisions on:

- whether it can be sold independently
- when it must be bundled with Sales Director
- when it must be bundled with Website Director or Advertising Director
- whether businesses using only inbound phone demand still need it through Call Handling
- how source complexity affects A / B / C classification

Key commercial question:

- Is Lead Generation Director a standalone growth-control director, or is it automatically included whenever the system is qualifying inbound demand from any source?

This must be decided before final pricing architecture is locked.

---

## 16. Build Sequence

Recommended build order:

1. normalize lead categories and states
2. normalize source labels and source taxonomy
3. normalize shared opportunity record fields
4. build President and CRO source / quality surfaces
5. build routing and ownership views
6. connect handoff logic to Sales and Follow-Up
7. define A / B / C rules
8. test source-specific scenarios

Do not start by designing a separate lead dashboard shell without normalizing the underlying lead truth first.

---

## 17. Internal Test Scenarios

At minimum, the team should test:

1. an ad lead enters and is routed correctly
2. a website form lead enters and is labeled correctly
3. an inbound call lead becomes a qualified opportunity
4. a weak-fit lead is visible as weak-fit
5. a duplicate lead is flagged for review
6. an unrouted lead becomes visible to the President
7. a qualified lead hands off cleanly to Sales Director
8. an aging lead hands off cleanly to Follow-Up Director

If these scenarios are not reliable, Lead Generation Director is not launch-ready.
