## 1. Reviews / Referrals Director Mission

Reviews / Referrals Director is the trust-amplification and customer-advocacy lane of AI-ABCX.

Its job is to:

- protect and grow public trust after customer interactions
- make review-response discipline visible
- turn satisfied customers into referral opportunity instead of wasted goodwill
- show where review pressure is aging
- show where customer advocacy is strong or weak
- connect reputation activity to actual business growth
- preserve review and referral truth inside the President-visible operating system

It should not behave like a generic reputation widget.

It should behave like a corporate trust and advocacy lane under President control.

---

## 2. Reviews / Referrals Director Launch Standard

Reviews / Referrals Director is launch-ready only when it can do all of the following:

1. show open reviews and review-response pressure clearly
2. distinguish positive review momentum from unresolved review risk
3. show referral-ready customers and active referral sources visibly
4. connect review and referral activity to customer-success context
5. keep review and referral work connected to the main customer record
6. report trust and advocacy pressure inside the President view
7. support A / B / C levels clearly
8. support credible guided-activation recommendations and connected bundles

If one of those is missing, the director is not complete.

## 7. A / B / C Definition Draft

This checklist assumes these working level definitions.

They can be refined later, but implementation should move around this structure.

### Level A

- basic review visibility
- open review-response pressure is visible
- referral-ready customers are visible
- main referral sources are visible
- President can see trust pressure and advocacy opportunity

### Level B

- stronger review-response workflows
- more structured referral follow-through
- clearer ownership and escalation logic
- stronger referral-source organization
- better coordination with Customer Success and Follow-Up

### Level C

- advanced review and referral orchestration
- stronger automated prompting and follow-through
- richer reputation-risk handling
- stronger advocacy performance visibility
- deeper coordination with Marketing and growth strategy

### Level X

- future fully autonomous trust and advocacy orchestration
- predictive advocacy identification
- self-improving review/referral timing logic
- deeper board-level reputation control

X should remain future-facing, not launch-blocking.

## 8. Required Reviews / Referrals Categories

Reviews / Referrals Director must support category-aware trust and advocacy logic.

Required launch categories:

- open reviews
- positive reviews awaiting acknowledgment
- high-risk / escalated reviews
- referral-ready customers
- active referral sources
- partner referral sources
- stalled referral follow-through
- reputation watch signals

Each category should remain distinguishable in the system.

- `review_resolved`
- `reputation_watch`
- `referral_ready`
- `referral_requested`
- `referral_active`
- `referral_follow_through_needed`
- `advocate_identified`
- `president_attention_needed`

Optional later-state refinement can be added after launch, but these are the minimum states needed for real trust and advocacy control.

## 10. Required Shared Trust Record

Reviews / Referrals Director must not operate on a disconnected review app layer.

It needs a shared record that includes:

- customer identity
- service history
- support / complaint context
- review status
- response ownership
- referral readiness
- referral source history
- advocacy state
- next-step ownership
- President-visible summary state

If review or referral work is disconnected from the main customer truth, the director is not complete.

## 13. Required Director Handoffs

Reviews / Referrals Director must coordinate with:

### Customer Success Director

Because review and referral quality are direct outcomes of customer continuity.

### Follow-Up Director

Because review responses and referral next steps often remain unresolved work.

### Marketing Director

Because referrals and public trust affect growth, campaign quality, and commercial credibility.

### Sales Director

Because expectation-setting and proposal quality affect review outcomes and advocacy.

### Call Handling Director

Because initial frustration or delight often begins with call experience.

### Service / COO-side lanes

Because field execution directly affects trust, complaints, and review tone.

If these handoffs are vague, the director will feel cosmetic instead of operational.

## 14. Guided Activation Requirements

Guided activation must be able to recommend Reviews / Referrals Director credibly.

Required qualifying questions:

- Do reviews matter to how customers choose your company?
- Do you respond to reviews consistently?
- Do happy customers often refer new business?
- Do referrals happen informally instead of through a visible process?
- Do negative or unanswered reviews create growth pressure?
- Do you want stronger customer advocacy and social proof?

Guided activation should explain Reviews / Referrals Director as:

- the lane that protects trust publicly
- the lane that turns satisfied customers into visible growth opportunity

## 15. Pricing Decisions Required

Before launch, AI-ABCX must decide:

1. whether Reviews / Referrals Director is sold standalone or only with Customer Success
2. whether review logic and referral logic remain one director or split later
3. whether it is more naturally bundled with Marketing in certain configurations
4. what A / B / C commercial boundaries really mean
5. whether review requests, response logic, and referral-tracking depth differ by level

These decisions do not block checklist writing, but they do block final commercialization.

## 17. Internal Test Scenarios

Reviews / Referrals Director is not launch-ready until it passes realistic internal scenarios.

Required scenario tests:

### Scenario 1: Old review remains unanswered

System must:

- show review age visibly
- show response ownership
- escalate if aging passes threshold

### Scenario 2: Negative review after service failure

System must:

- connect review risk to actual service context
- preserve customer-history visibility
- show reputation-watch status

### Scenario 3: Happy customer becomes referral-ready

System must:

- identify advocacy potential
- show referral-ready state
- preserve next-step visibility

### Scenario 4: Referral source produces repeat leads

System must:

- track active source
- show follow-through quality
- preserve source visibility in summary surfaces

### Scenario 5: Referral opportunity is missed

System must:

- show stalled follow-through
- keep opportunity visible until resolved or closed
