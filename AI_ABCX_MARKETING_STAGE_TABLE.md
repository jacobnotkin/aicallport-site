# AI-ABCX Marketing Stage Table

This document gives the short working definition of what the `Marketing` module does at each AI-ABCX automation level.

It is meant to be a compact operational reference.

The governing release rule is:

- `Stage A` launch includes `Marketing A`
- `Stage B` release unlocks `Marketing B`
- `Stage C` release unlocks `Marketing C`
- `Marketing X` remains the highest later intelligent level

---

## Marketing A

### Meaning

Foundational growth structure.

### What it should do

- source tracking
- campaign list and campaign ownership
- channel visibility
- referral source tracking
- reactivation visibility
- qualified lead visibility
- President growth summary

### What it should answer

- where are leads coming from?
- which channels are active?
- which growth lanes look promising?

### What it should not try to do yet

- deep attribution to paid revenue
- full budget optimization
- advanced recommendation logic

---

## Marketing B

### Meaning

Connected growth automation.

### What it should do

- everything in Marketing A
- attribution to booked appointments
- attribution to sold work
- amount-paid visibility by source
- stronger lead-quality tracking by source
- stronger campaign comparison
- stronger reactivation performance tracking

### What it should answer

- which sources create real business?
- which sources create booked work?
- which sources create paid revenue?
- which channels are weak and wasting money?

---

## Marketing C

### Meaning

Advanced growth management automation.

### What it should do

- everything in Marketing B
- recommendation engine
- budget direction
- growth warnings
- service-line comparison
- market comparison
- President next-action recommendations

### What it should answer

- what should the President do next?
- where should spend increase or decrease?
- what growth lane deserves priority now?

---

## Marketing X

### Meaning

Full intelligent growth orchestration.

### What it should do

- everything in Marketing C
- strongest cross-module growth intelligence
- deepest attribution layer
- highest reactivation intelligence
- strongest optimization logic
- full President-level growth command value

### What it should answer

- how should growth be orchestrated across the company as one system?
- what actions create the strongest long-term growth value?

---

## Short Ladder Summary

| Level | Meaning | Short Description |
|---|---|---|
| Marketing A | Foundational growth structure | Source visibility and structured growth truth |
| Marketing B | Connected growth automation | Booked/sold attribution and stronger lead-quality truth |
| Marketing C | Advanced growth management | Recommendations, budget direction, and President growth control |
| Marketing X | Full intelligent orchestration | Deepest growth intelligence and optimization across the system |
