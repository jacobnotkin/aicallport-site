## 1. Advertising Director Mission

Advertising Director is the paid-demand control lane of AI-ABCX.

Its job is to:

- make paid acquisition visible by channel
- preserve ROI discipline before spend scales
- show which campaigns are producing fit, not just traffic
- identify weak channels early
- connect spend to demand quality
- protect the company from wasting budget on low-fit acquisition
- report campaign pressure directly to the President

Advertising Director should not feel like a generic ads manager.

It should feel like a structured paid-growth operating lane under President control.

---

## 2. Advertising Director Launch Standard

Advertising Director is launch-ready only when it can do all of the following:

1. show paid channels clearly
2. show spend visibility clearly
3. support source-to-lead attribution
4. distinguish strong ROI from weak ROI
5. coordinate directly with Website Director and Lead Generation Director
6. show where campaign fit and landing-page fit are breaking down
7. surface budget risk visibly to the President
8. support A / B / C levels clearly

If one of these is missing, the director is not complete.

---

## 3. Canonical Role Definition

### Public-facing name

`Advertising Director`

### Internal role meaning

The AI-ABCX director responsible for paid campaign direction, spend visibility, attribution discipline, and the control of campaign quality before budget is expanded.

### Executive owner

`CMO`

Advertising Director belongs to the marketing lane because it controls paid acquisition pressure and campaign investment quality.

---

## 4. Commercial Promise

Advertising Director should promise:

- clearer visibility into where ad money is going
- better awareness of which channels actually produce business-fit demand
- fewer weak campaigns consuming budget silently
- better connection between spend, landing-page fit, and lead quality
- better protection against scaling bad campaigns
- better President visibility into acquisition efficiency

The commercial promise is not merely:

- "You get ad reporting."

It is:

- "Your paid growth becomes visible, disciplined, and tied to real business outcomes."

---

## 5. What AI-ABCX Must Match

Advertising Director must match the practical market expectations customers already have from strong advertising systems:

- channel visibility
- spend tracking
- ROI awareness
- attribution visibility
- weak-channel identification
- landing-page fit awareness
- budget risk awareness

At minimum, it must not feel weaker than a modern paid-growth reporting layer.

---

## 6. Where AI-ABCX Can Beat the Market

Advertising Director can beat typical outside campaign tools because:

- it lives inside one President-led corporate structure
- it connects directly to Website Director, Lead Generation Director, Call Handling Director, and Sales Director
- it can show not just clicks and spend, but actual business-fit demand consequences
- it can keep one shared operating record from ad source to lead to sales outcome
- it can turn campaign quality into an executive operating issue instead of a disconnected marketing report

This is stronger than a standard ads dashboard because it links paid spend to the full business system.

## 7. A / B / C Definition Draft

### Level A

Level A Advertising Director is visible paid-channel oversight.

Includes:

- visible paid channels
- spend visibility
- basic attribution awareness
- weak-channel identification
- President visibility into ad pressure

Best fit:

- small businesses beginning paid acquisition
- businesses running limited campaign volume
- teams trying to avoid obvious wasted spend

### Level B

Level B Advertising Director adds stronger efficiency control.

Includes everything in A, plus:

- stronger channel comparison
- better landing-page fit visibility
- better source-quality visibility
- stronger coordination with Lead Generation Director
- stronger pause / continue decision support

Best fit:

- businesses with multiple paid channels
- businesses actively buying demand
- teams trying to improve efficiency before scaling

### Level C

Level C Advertising Director adds advanced paid-growth orchestration.

Includes everything in B, plus:

- deeper campaign prioritization
- stronger autonomous spend guidance
- richer fit and ROI logic
- tighter connection to website optimization and sales quality
- more advanced President and CMO control visibility

Best fit:

- scaling companies
- multi-channel advertisers
- businesses trying to expand aggressively with control

## 8. Required Advertising Categories

Advertising Director should support at least these launch categories:

- search ads
- social ads
- local service ads
- retargeting campaigns
- call-focused campaigns
- form-focused campaigns
- awareness / visibility campaigns

These categories should remain distinguishable so the system can evaluate fit and ROI properly.

---

## 9. Required Advertising States

At minimum, Advertising Director should support:

- `active_campaign`
- `under_review`
- `weak_roi`
- `high_fit`
- `landing_fit_issue`
- `budget_watch`
- `paused`
- `scaled`
- `president_attention_needed`

These states should stay simple and decision-oriented rather than drift into generic marketing labels.

---

## 10. Required Shared Demand Record

Advertising Director must operate on the same shared demand and opportunity record used by the rest of AI-ABCX.

That record should preserve:

- campaign source
- channel type
- spend origin
- landing target
- attributed lead count
- fit quality
- routed owner
- handoff outcome
- next-step status
- budget review status

Advertising Director should never create a disconnected campaign-only truth layer.

---

## 11. Required Product Surfaces

Launch surfaces should include:

- Paid Channel Summary
- ROI / Weak ROI Summary
- Attribution View
- Landing-Page Fit View
- Campaign Risk Summary
- Budget Pressure Summary

If a dedicated Advertising dashboard does not exist at launch, these surfaces still need to be visible inside the President and CMO lanes.

---

## 12. Required Dashboard Visibility

Advertising must be visible in the right places:

- President Dashboard
- CMO summary
- Lead Generation dependency visibility
- Website dependency visibility

The President should always be able to see:

- which paid channels are active
- which channels are weak
- whether attribution is healthy
- whether landing-page fit is breaking down
- whether spend is being protected or wasted

---

## 13. Required Director Handoffs

Advertising Director must hand off cleanly with:

### Website Director

- depends on landing-page fit
- requires CTA and trust continuity
- needs campaign-to-page alignment

### Lead Generation Director

- hands off paid demand into source-quality review
- depends on visible lead attribution
- depends on fit, routing, and ownership visibility

### Call Handling Director

- supports call-focused paid campaigns
- depends on clean call capture and classification where ads create inbound calls

### Sales Director

- must ultimately connect paid demand to conversion reality
- should never optimize only for traffic if conversion fit is weak

### Marketing Director

- remains inside the same broader growth lane
- should preserve continuity between advertising, message, website, and demand quality

---

## 14. Guided Activation Requirements

The activation process must determine:

- whether the company already runs paid ads
- which channels they currently use
- whether they know which channels work best
- whether they believe current spend is efficient
- whether they want more leads, better leads, or stronger ROI
- whether they already have attribution
- whether landing pages are connected to campaigns correctly

Without these answers, Advertising Director cannot be configured correctly.

---

## 15. Pricing Decisions Required

Pricing for Advertising Director still needs final decisions on:

- whether it can be sold independently
- when it must be bundled with Website Director
- when it must be bundled with Lead Generation Director
- whether call-focused campaigns require Call Handling Director as well
- how channel count and complexity affect A / B / C classification

Key commercial question:

- Is Advertising Director a standalone spend-control director, or is it automatically part of a broader growth system whenever the customer is buying paid demand?

This must be decided before final pricing architecture is locked.

---

## 16. Build Sequence

Recommended build order:

1. normalize paid channel categories
2. normalize ad states and weak-ROI logic
3. normalize attribution fields inside the shared demand record
4. build President and CMO campaign / ROI surfaces
5. connect landing-fit visibility to Website Director
6. connect demand-quality visibility to Lead Generation Director
7. define A / B / C rules
8. test paid-demand scenarios

Do not start by designing a visual ad dashboard shell without first normalizing the campaign truth underneath it.

---

## 17. Internal Test Scenarios

At minimum, the team should test:

1. a search ad campaign produces attributed leads correctly
2. a social campaign is visible as active and measurable
3. a weak-ROI campaign becomes visible for review
4. a landing-page mismatch becomes visible
5. a call-focused campaign routes correctly through Call Handling
6. a paid lead enters Lead Generation with source attribution preserved
7. the President can identify which campaign should be paused first
8. a scaled campaign remains visible as healthy only when fit and ROI justify it

If these scenarios are not reliable, Advertising Director is not launch-ready.
