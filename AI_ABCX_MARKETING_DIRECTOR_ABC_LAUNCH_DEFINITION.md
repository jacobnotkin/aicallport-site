# AI-ABCX Marketing Director A/B/C Launch Definition

## 1. Core Rule

Marketing Director is the CMO-controlled coordinated growth lane inside AI-ABCX.

Its job is to make marketing visible as managed business activity instead of scattered effort.

It must help the President understand:

- where demand is coming from
- which channels create qualified opportunities
- which channels create booked work and paid revenue
- where growth friction exists
- what growth action should happen next

This director is not just paid advertising.

It is the broader coordination layer across:

- Advertising
- Website
- SEO
- social distribution
- referral and reactivation logic
- lead-quality visibility

---

## 2. Public Meaning Of Each Level

### Level A

**Visible coordinated growth truth**

The business can clearly see its channels, campaigns, sources, and growth pressure in one place instead of guessing what is working.

### Level B

**Connected growth outcome workflow**

The business does not just see marketing activity. It can track what channels produce better leads, bookings, sold outcomes, and paid results.

### Level C

**Advanced growth management orchestration**

The system actively guides where growth is weak, where money or effort is being wasted, and what marketing action should happen next.

---

## 3. Level A Definition

Level A means the company has foundational growth structure and President-visible marketing truth.

At Level A, Marketing Director should:

- show active and inactive channels clearly
- show campaign list and campaign ownership
- classify major sources of demand
- show referral-source visibility
- show website and form-source visibility
- show reactivation visibility
- distinguish qualified versus weak-fit lead flow
- show a President-facing growth summary
- make channel and campaign status understandable without needing an agency report

Level A is the minimum launch standard for this director.

It creates visibility first.

Without this visibility, the business cannot govern growth because it does not know what is helping and what is weak.

---

## 4. Level B Definition

Level B means the company has connected growth outcome visibility instead of just campaign awareness.

At Level B, Marketing Director should add:

- source-to-lead attribution
- lead-quality tracking by source
- booked outcomes by source
- sold outcomes by source
- amount-paid visibility by source
- stronger campaign comparison
- stronger reactivation performance tracking
- clearer visibility into where growth effort turns into business results

Level B should make marketing operational and accountable, not just observable.

---

## 5. Level C Definition

Level C means the company has advanced growth management orchestration across channels, sources, offers, and business outcomes.

At Level C, Marketing Director should add:

- recommendation engine
- budget-direction guidance
- growth-risk warnings
- service-line comparison
- market comparison logic
- President-facing next-action recommendations
- stronger coordination across Website, Advertising, SEO, Lead Generation, Sales, and Follow-Up lanes

Level C should feel like a real management layer for business growth, not a bundle of channel reports.

---

## 6. Exact Launch Difference Between A, B, And C

### A vs B

Level A shows coordinated growth truth.

Level B adds outcome visibility tied to better leads, bookings, sold work, and paid revenue.

### B vs C

Level B shows real growth outcomes.

Level C adds deeper recommendation, prioritization, risk visibility, and President-level growth guidance.

### Plain-English Lock

- A = we can see where growth activity is happening
- B = we can see which growth activity produces better outcomes
- C = the system helps manage growth strategically and recommends what should change next

---

## 7. Dependency Guidance

Marketing Director is a coordination lane, so it becomes much stronger when connected to the other growth and outcome directors.

### Can function at Level A with light dependency

Yes, if the business mainly needs:

- channel visibility
- campaign visibility
- source awareness
- growth-priority visibility

### Becomes much stronger with these connected directors

- **Advertising Director**
  Handles paid execution while Marketing evaluates broader effectiveness and fit.
- **Website Director**
  Exposes whether website structure is helping or hurting growth.
- **SEO Director**
  Places organic visibility inside broader growth logic.
- **Lead Generation Director**
  Supplies demand-quality truth after sources start producing leads.
- **Reactivation Director**
  Helps compare return-growth activity against new acquisition effort.
- **Sales Director**
  Connects marketing performance to sold and paid outcomes.
- **Follow-Up Director**
  Helps expose when weak follow-up is reducing otherwise-healthy demand.
- **Call Handling Director**
  Helps when inbound-call quality strongly affects marketing outcomes.

### Recommended dependency logic

- Level A can be sold with light dependencies
- Level B is much stronger with Lead Generation, Website, and Sales visibility
- Level C should usually be recommended with multiple connected growth lanes, especially Website, Lead Generation, Advertising, and Sales

---

## 8. Recommendation Logic Guidance

Recommend Marketing Director when the company says things like:

- “We market in different places but don’t know what is actually working.”
- “We get leads, but quality is inconsistent.”
- “Our marketing feels scattered.”
- “We want more structured growth.”
- “We don’t know which source brings real revenue.”
- “We want to improve demand quality, not just volume.”

### Recommend Level A when

- the business needs basic marketing structure first
- multiple channels already exist but visibility is weak
- the owner wants a President-level growth view before deeper automation

### Recommend Level B when

- the business has meaningful lead flow
- source quality matters
- campaign comparison matters
- the owner wants to connect marketing to bookings, sold work, and amount paid

### Recommend Level C when

- the business is growth-focused
- multiple channels are active
- marketing waste or weak-fit lead flow is a real concern
- the owner wants strong strategic guidance without building a full internal marketing department

---

## 9. Business-Friendly Explanation For Customers

Marketing Director helps your company stop treating marketing like scattered activity and start treating it like a real managed business function.

At Level A, it shows where growth is coming from, which channels are active, and where pressure is building.

At Level B, it connects those channels to real outcomes like better leads, booked work, sold work, and paid results.

At Level C, it becomes a stronger management layer that helps the President understand what is weak, what is being wasted, and what marketing action should happen next.

---

## 10. What Must Be Avoided

Do not describe this director as only:

- a social posting tool
- an ad dashboard
- a generic analytics page
- a duplicate of Advertising Director
- a vanity report

That would undersell the real function.

The correct launch meaning is:

**Marketing Director is the coordinated growth-governance lane that connects channels, campaigns, lead quality, and business outcomes into one President-visible system.**

Also avoid:

- collapsing Marketing into Advertising
- collapsing Marketing into Website
- treating source visibility as enough without outcome visibility

This director exists because growth needs coordination, not just activity.
