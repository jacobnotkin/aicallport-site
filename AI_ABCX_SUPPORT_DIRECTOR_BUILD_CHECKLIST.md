## 1. Support Director Mission

Support Director is the customer-response and issue-resolution lane of AI-ABCX.

Its job is to:

- keep customer issues visible after they enter the system
- protect response timing and follow-through discipline
- make unresolved support threads impossible to lose
- preserve support history and context inside the customer record
- surface escalations before they become retention damage
- connect support issues to service, follow-up, and customer-success truth
- make support pressure visible to the President

It should not behave like a disconnected helpdesk inbox.

It should behave like a corporate support lane under President control.

---

## 2. Support Director Launch Standard

Support Director is launch-ready only when it can do all of the following:

1. show open support items clearly
2. show response timing pressure clearly
3. show who owns each open support thread
4. preserve support context and history across the operating record
5. distinguish ordinary support from escalation risk
6. report support pressure inside the President view
7. support A / B / C levels clearly
8. support credible guided-activation recommendations and connected bundles

If one of those is missing, the director is not complete.

## 3. Canonical Role Definition

## Public-facing name

`Support Director`

The AI-ABCX director responsible for support continuity, issue handling, response timing, escalation control, customer-context preservation, and visible follow-through.

## Executive owner

`CSO`

Support Director belongs to the customer-success lane because its purpose is to protect active customer relationships once a question, issue, or friction point already exists.

It also acts as a bridge between:

- Customer Success

- Follow-Up

- President visibility

## 7. A / B / C Definition Draft

This checklist assumes these working level definitions.

They can be refined later, but implementation should move around this structure.

### Level A

- basic support visibility
- open support items are visible
- response ownership is visible
- delayed replies are visible
- President can see current support pressure

### Level B

- stronger response discipline
- clearer escalation routing
- stronger cross-lane coordination with service and follow-up
- better context and history preservation
- more structured support ownership rules

### Level C

- advanced support orchestration
- predictive escalation prevention
- stronger support intelligence and intervention logic
- deeper coordination with customer-success, reviews, and service outcomes
- richer executive visibility into support-health movement

### Level X

- future fully autonomous support governance
- predictive issue prevention
- self-improving response and escalation logic
- deeper board-level service accountability

X should remain future-facing, not launch-blocking.

## 8. Required Support Categories

Support Director must support category-aware issue handling.

Required launch categories:

- open support items
- waiting-for-response items
- delayed replies
- escalated support items
- context-gap items
- unresolved follow-through items
- history-quality risks
- customer-friction watch items

Each category should remain distinguishable in the system.

## 9. Required Support States

At minimum, Support Director must support the following states:

- `support_open`
- `awaiting_response`
- `awaiting_internal_action`
- `delayed_reply`
- `context_gap`
- `follow_through_needed`
- `escalated`
- `resolved`
- `history_risk`
- `president_attention_needed`

Optional later-state refinement can be added after launch, but these are the minimum states needed for real support control.

---

## 10. Required Shared Support Record

Support Director must not operate on a disconnected inbox layer.

It needs a shared support record that includes:

- customer identity
- communication history
- service history
- current open issue
- response owner
- escalation level
- current blocker
- next step
- support timestamps
- customer-success context
- President-visible summary state

If support data is separate from the main operating truth, the director is not complete.

---

## 11. Required Product Surfaces

Support Director should not launch as hidden logic only.

It needs visible product surfaces.

### Surface 1: Executive Summary

Purpose:

- show current support operating picture
- summarize open items, delayed replies, and escalation pressure
- give the President a clean support reading

### Surface 2: Priorities

Purpose:

- show what support items need immediate response
- show where ownership is weak
- show which issues are aging

### Surface 3: Risks

Purpose:

- show escalation risk
- show churn-pressure risk
- show context and history breakdown risk

### Surface 4: Dependencies

Purpose:

- show where support depends on service, follow-up, scheduling, or customer-success action
- show which outside lane is blocking resolution

These four surfaces already align with the current dashboard evidence and should remain the minimum launch shape.

## 12. Required Dashboard Visibility

Support Director must be visible in:

- President Dashboard
- CSO lane
- customer-level support record
- escalation-aware operating views

At minimum the President should be able to understand:

- how many support items are open
- which ones are delayed
- which ones are escalated
- whether context quality is strong or weak
- whether support pressure is creating customer-risk

---

## 13. Required Director Handoffs

Support Director must hand off cleanly to:

- Customer Success Director
  - support problems often become retention problems
- Reviews / Referrals Director
  - support failures can become review pressure
- Follow-Up Director
  - unresolved support often needs follow-through discipline
- Call Handling Director
  - support issues may begin in calls or require callback handling
- Scheduling Director
  - service-related support often depends on timing and appointment truth
- Dispatch Director
  - field execution failures may create support issues
- Sales Director
  - expectation-setting issues can create support friction

If these handoffs are vague, the director is not real yet.

---

## 14. Guided Activation Requirements

Guided activation should help determine whether Support Director is needed and at what level.

Minimum qualifying questions:

- Do customer questions or problems sometimes get lost?
- Does your team struggle to answer customers quickly?
- Are support requests handled through scattered calls, texts, and email?
- Do customers ever have to repeat the same issue more than once?
- Do unresolved issues sometimes become complaints or bad reviews?
- Do you need visible ownership for customer issues?

Support Director should be recommendable both:

- as part of broader customer-success / trust control
- and as a more directly visible support lane where active response discipline matters

---

## 15. Pricing Decisions Required

Pricing is not finalized here, but the following decisions must be made before launch:

- whether Support Director is sold only inside broader customer-success bundles or can stand commercially on its own
- whether A / B / C pricing changes mostly reflect:
  - response depth
  - escalation logic
  - predictive intelligence
  - number of visible users
- whether support seats / users affect pricing directly
- whether support becomes part of larger trust / continuity combinations for some customer types

Until those decisions are made, Support Director is not commercially complete.

## 17. Internal Test Scenarios

Support Director is not ready until AI-ABCX can pass realistic internal tests.

Minimum test scenarios:

### Test 1: Open issue with no owner

The system must show:

- the issue is open
- ownership is missing
- President visibility reflects the risk

### Test 2: Delayed response

The system must show:

- the reply is overdue
- support pressure rises
- the item becomes a real priority

### Test 3: Escalation after service issue

The system must show:

- the issue escalated out of ordinary support
- service context is visible
- the cross-lane dependency is clear

### Test 4: Missing context / history gap

The system must show:

- the record is incomplete
- history quality is weak
- the issue cannot hide behind a vague conversation trail

### Test 5: Resolved issue with no continuity follow-through

The system must show:

- issue resolution happened
- continuity is still incomplete if needed
- customer-success risk can still remain visible

If these tests fail, the director is not launch-ready.
