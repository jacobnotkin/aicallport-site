# AI-ABCX Marketing Director Build Checklist

This document turns the existing AI-ABCX marketing concept into a launch-ready build checklist.

It is written to normalize `Marketing Director` as a real director inside the AI-ABCX launch structure rather than leaving it as a broad spec-only idea.

---

## 1. Marketing Director Mission

The `Marketing Director` is the coordinated growth-control lane inside AI-ABCX.

Its job is to make marketing visible as managed business activity instead of scattered effort.

It must help the President understand:

- where demand is coming from
- which marketing channels produce qualified opportunities
- which channels produce booked work and paid revenue
- where growth friction exists
- what growth action should happen next

This director is not only paid advertising.

It is the broader coordination layer across:

- Advertising
- Website
- SEO
- social distribution
- referral and reactivation logic
- lead-quality visibility

---

## 2. Marketing Director Launch Standard

At launch, `Marketing Director` is only complete if it already feels like serious business-control logic.

It must not feel like:

- a vanity dashboard
- a generic agency report
- a disconnected analytics panel
- a duplicate of Advertising Director

It must feel like:

- coordinated message and channel oversight
- growth visibility tied to business outcomes
- structured demand management
- President-level marketing governance

Launch standard means the director can already answer:

- what channels are active?
- what channels are weak?
- what demand sources look high quality?
- where is conversion friction happening?
- what should be improved first?

---

## 3. Canonical Role Definition

### Public name

`Marketing Director`

### Executive lane

`CMO`

### Core role

Own coordinated growth logic across channels and connect marketing effort to lead quality, conversion quality, and business outcomes.

### Distinction from related directors

- `Advertising Director` controls paid demand execution and paid-spend discipline
- `SEO Director` controls search visibility and organic performance
- `Website Director` controls website structure, conversion infrastructure, and website readiness
- `Lead Generation Director` controls incoming demand intake quality and routing
- `Marketing Director` sits above these as the broader growth-control and coordination lane

---

## 4. Commercial Promise

The commercial promise of `Marketing Director` is:

`Your marketing stops being scattered and starts operating like a managed business function.`

The customer should feel that AI-ABCX gives them:

- visibility into where growth comes from
- visibility into what is weak
- visibility into what is wasting money
- structured growth coordination across channels
- clear next actions instead of guesswork

---

## 5. What AI-ABCX Must Match

To be launch-ready, `Marketing Director` must match modern expectations for:

- channel visibility
- campaign coordination
- source-to-lead visibility
- lead-quality insight
- reactivation visibility
- website conversion awareness
- President-level summaries

At minimum, it must feel as organized as a serious modern marketing ops tool.

## 6. Where AI-ABCX Can Beat The Market

AI-ABCX can beat typical marketing tools because it is not trying to be a disconnected “marketing app.”

It can be stronger by making marketing part of one corporate operating structure.

Main AI-ABCX advantages:

- President-led visibility instead of department isolation
- shared truth between Marketing, Sales, Follow-Up, Website, Call Handling, and Scheduling
- one system of record instead of stitched dashboards
- business-outcome framing instead of vanity reporting
- recommendation logic tied to actual operations and paid outcomes

The winning promise is:

`Growth is no longer scattered. It becomes governable.`

## 7. A / B / C Definition Draft

### Marketing Director A

Foundational growth structure.

Must include:

- source tracking
- campaign list and campaign ownership
- channel classification
- referral source visibility
- website/form source visibility
- reactivation visibility
- qualified vs unqualified lead visibility
- President growth summary

### Marketing Director B

Connected growth automation.

Must include everything in A, plus:

- source-to-lead attribution
- lead-quality tracking by source
- booked appointments by source
- sold outcomes by source
- amount-paid visibility by source
- stronger campaign comparison
- stronger reactivation performance tracking

### Marketing Director C

Advanced growth management automation.

Must include everything in B, plus:

- recommendation engine
- budget-direction guidance
- growth-risk warnings
- service-line comparison
- market comparison
- President-facing next-action recommendations

### Marketing Director X

Highest future intelligent orchestration level.

Not required for launch, but the architecture must not block it.

## 8. Required Marketing Categories

The director should support these categories as first-class marketing lanes:

- paid campaigns
- organic / SEO demand
- website conversion activity
- social distribution
- referral and partner sources
- seasonal campaigns
- reactivation campaigns
- review / trust-building campaigns
- offer-based growth campaigns

## 9. Required Marketing States

The system must define clear states for marketing activity.

Minimum recommended states:

- `active_channel`
- `inactive_channel`
- `campaign_live`
- `under_review`
- `message_fit_issue`
- `conversion_friction`
- `weak_lead_quality`
- `roi_watch`
- `reactivation_opportunity`
- `growth_priority`
- `paused`
- `president_attention_needed`

These states should appear consistently in reporting and recommendation logic.

## 10. Required Shared Demand / Campaign Record

`Marketing Director` cannot be strong if campaign and demand truth stay fragmented.

The director needs a shared record that can connect:

- source
- campaign
- offer
- landing path
- lead count
- qualified lead count
- booked count
- sold count
- amount paid
- reactivation output
- notes / warnings

This record does not need to be perfect at first launch, but it must exist clearly enough for A/B/C logic to be real.

## 13. Required Director Handoffs

`Marketing Director` must hand off cleanly to and from the following directors:

### Advertising Director

Advertising handles paid execution.

Marketing must evaluate broader effectiveness and fit.

### SEO Director

SEO handles organic visibility.

Marketing must place that visibility inside broader growth logic.

### Website Director

Website controls conversion infrastructure.

Marketing must expose when weak conversion limits growth.

### Lead Generation Director

Lead Generation receives and classifies demand.

Marketing must understand whether demand quality is strong or weak.

### Call Handling Director

Call outcomes must be visible to marketing where inbound call demand matters.

### Sales Director

Marketing must connect not only to leads, but to sold outcomes and paid revenue.

### Follow-Up Director

Marketing recommendations should expose when follow-up weakness is reducing growth results.

---

## 14. Guided Activation Requirements

The guided activation flow should ask enough human-readable questions to qualify Marketing Director correctly.

Questions should cover:

- do they run paid ads now?
- do they depend on referrals?
- do they rely on SEO or website forms?
- do they send reactivation campaigns?
- do they use social media for business growth?
- do they know which sources bring real business?
- do they feel their marketing is scattered or unclear?
- is their main goal more leads, better conversion, reactivation, or better visibility?

Marketing Director should not be assigned only because “marketing exists.”

It should be assigned because growth coordination is actually needed.

---

## 15. Pricing Decisions Required

Before launch, these decisions must be finalized:

- can `Marketing Director` be sold independently?
- when should it be bundled with `Website Director`?
- when should it be bundled with `Advertising Director`?
- when should it be bundled with `Lead Generation Director`?
- what separates A vs B vs C commercially?
- do active channel count, campaign complexity, or attribution depth affect pricing?

Strong likelihood:

- A should be viable for visibility-focused businesses
- B should be the first serious attribution tier
- C should be the first true management-control tier

---

## 16. Build Sequence

Recommended build sequence:

1. Normalize role definition
2. Confirm handoffs against Advertising / Website / Lead Generation / Sales
3. Define required states and categories
4. Define dashboard surface map
5. Define A/B/C gating rules
6. Define activation questions
7. Build static or demo marketing views
8. Build business-truth connection logic
9. Run internal scenarios
10. mark launch readiness

---

## 17. Internal Test Scenarios

The director should be tested against at least these scenarios:

### Scenario 1

Paid ads are active, but lead quality is weak.

Expected result:

Marketing flags weak quality and does not present volume as success.

### Scenario 2

Website traffic is healthy, but form conversion is weak.

Expected result:

Marketing exposes website conversion friction and recommends Website Director attention.

### Scenario 3

Reactivation campaigns outperform new paid demand.

Expected result:

Marketing recommends reactivation priority before more spend.

### Scenario 4

Lead volume is high, but sold revenue is low.

Expected result:

Marketing surfaces downstream weakness instead of celebrating top-of-funnel numbers.

### Scenario 5

One source produces fewer leads but stronger paid outcomes.

Expected result:

Marketing ranks business quality over raw lead count.
